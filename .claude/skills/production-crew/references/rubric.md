# The rubric: six areas, scored 1–5, anchored on our own films

Score 1, 3 or 5 by matching the anchors; 2 and 4 sit between. Always give the evidence (time + what you saw).
When comparing two versions, say which is better per area and why — comparisons are more reliable than scores.

## 1. Script (story, clarity, hook)
- **1** — a line needs decoding ("你发的那张合照，也在别人的凌晨一点"); the title overclaims or misleads
  ("只有我过得不好"); a later scene repeats an earlier point ("后面的内容好无聊").
- **3** — clear and correct, but slow: lines sit after they are read (《换不换》 v1: 1:38, ~22 % waiting), the
  surprise arrives late, the ending restates rather than lands.
- **5** — the paradox is posed with a concrete number in the first ~8 s (《越修越堵》: 65 → 80 by 0:05, "没有事故，
  也没人开错路" removes the easy explanation); every beat adds something new; one clear takeaway; the end-card
  question invites comments.

## 2. Visual design (look, composition, readability)
- **1** — something looks broken or wrong: odd hands (ep6), half-rendered scissors (《越修越堵》 v2), the moon
  blown to a white disc (《红线》 first stills), text over text.
- **3** — clean but generic or flat; low contrast; the key visual competes with labels; the first frame is dark
  or empty (《红线》 v2 0:00).
- **5** — a distinctive, coherent look that serves the idea (《越修越堵》 light trails; the 同心结 in 《红线》);
  the eye goes straight to the one thing that matters; every frame would work as a still.

## 3. Information design (charts, numbers, diagrams)
- **1** — the key number is too small, too brief, or covered; the diagram needs the subtitle to be understood.
- **3** — readable but cluttered; several numbers compete; labels collide with roads/lines.
- **5** — passes the 5-second test: a single frame says what the scene means (《越修越堵》 route panel counting
  65 → 80 live while "人人都这么想" plays).

## 4. Editing and transitions (cuts, rhythm, music)
- **1** — a jump between unrelated pictures (《隔着几个人》 v1 globe → dorm at 1:11), a dip to black as a
  bridge, hits that land off the music's structure.
- **3** — smooth crossfades that are not motivated; rhythm even but undramatic.
- **5** — every transition is motivated by something on screen (the dive into the "你" dot in 《隔着几个人》 v2;
  the scissors snapping in the musical gap and the weight rising on the drop in 《越修越堵》); hits only on
  structural moments.

## 5. Motion and animation
- **1** — the picture barely changes for 10 s (《越修越堵》 spring set-up 0:57–1:07); stiff, linear moves; things
  pop in without easing.
- **3** — constant gentle drift; motion present but not meaningful.
- **5** — purposeful motion every few seconds that carries the story (《越修越堵》 low flight along the highway
  into the reveal; 《换不换》 v3 stamp slam), eased and physical.

## 6. Sound
- **1** — silence at the start, clipping, music fighting the picture.
- **3** — music only, at the right level (all episodes so far).
- **5** — music plus designed sound: whooshes on moves, impacts on hits, ticks on counters, ambience that places
  the scene; nothing distracting.

## Overall
Not an average. Ask: would the owner post this today without a note? 5 = yes, proudly; 3 = with 1–2 notes;
1 = no.
