# Fact check: ep53 v3 「越王勾践剑 / 真的没锈吗？」

Checker: independent re-check, 2026-10-30 (08:12–08:40 UTC). Checked: `lines_v3_draft.json`, `stamps_v3_draft.json`, every tag and label in brief §3, and the picture claims in `storyboard_v3.md` / `script_v3.md`. I did not trust `facts.md`: I re-opened each source listed below. The classical texts were read on two transcriptions: zggdwx.com (simplified) and zh.wikisource.org (traditional). ctext.org returned only its anti-scraping page.

## 1. Verdict

**The subtitles and stamps are correct. Two things must be fixed before the rebuild:** one picture (the envoy scene) and the end-card sources line. Three small changes are recommended. No subtitle row has to change.

### MUST FIX
1. **G2 picture (54.00–58.25): take 勾践 and his wife out of the envoy-before-夫差 vignette.** In 《史记》 the envoy 种 goes **alone**, and 勾践 stays on 会稽山. When 种 comes back with a refusal, 勾践 wants to kill his wife and children and die fighting: 「种还，以报句践。句践欲杀妻子，燔宝器，触战以死。」 No text puts 勾践 or his wife in front of 夫差 during the plea. The wife being in 吴 at all appears only in the Eastern Han 《吴越春秋》, and there it is a later scene (勾践五年 入臣). 《国语》 (越语上, 越语下) never mentions her. **Fix:** show the envoy kneeling alone before 夫差. If 勾践 should be in the frame, make him a small far silhouette on the 会稽 slope (the G1 mountain) in the back, with no wife.
2. **End card: add the 勾践 sources.** The current line names only the sword sources. Proposed line (2 rows, about 22 px):
   > 资料：湖北省博物馆（2025研究成果）· 光明日报（陈振裕 2016；江旭东 2025）· 北京日报（后德俊 2016）
   > 《左传》哀公元年、二十二年 · 《国语》越语上、下 · 《史记》越王句践世家、吴太伯世家

   If row 1 is too long, shorten it to: 「资料：湖北省博物馆 2025 · 光明日报（陈振裕 2016；江旭东 2025）· 北京日报（后德俊 2016）」.

### SHOULD FIX (cheap, and they remove points a commenter could pick on)
3. **G5 picture (66.75–71.00): set the gall on or at his seat; don't hang it.** The stamp quotes 《史记》 「置胆於坐，坐卧即仰胆」, which says *placed* at his seat, and he looks up at it when he sits or lies. The *hanging* gall is 《吴越春秋》 「悬胆於户，出入尝之」, which also says it was at the **door**. A picture that contradicts the stamp's own quote is easy to catch. Fix: the gall sits on a small stand or tray beside his mat, a little above his eye line. Keep "he leans to it and tastes it".
4. **G3 subtitle row 2: soften the certainty.** The original is a guess, not a flat prophecy: 「二十年之外，吴其为沼乎！」 (其…乎 = "probably / I fear"). Replace 「"二十年后，吴国将变成沼泽！"」 with **「"二十年后，吴国怕要变成沼泽！"」** (16 字 with the quote marks, same meaning). Optional: 沼 means ponds or pools (吴's palaces in ruins), so 「沼泽」 is a literal rendering. It is acceptable, and the stamp 「吴其为沼」 gives the original.
5. **G4 picture: 勾践 runs ahead of the horses; he does not hold the bridle.** 《国语·越语上》 says 「其身亲为夫差前马」, and 韦昭注 glosses 前马 as 「前驱在马前也」, a forerunner in front of the horses. Walking with the bridle in hand is close but not the same thing. Fix: 勾践 on foot a pace ahead of the chariot horses, facing the road. The chariot itself is right: 前马 implies horses, and a king rode a chariot, not a horse, in 494 BC.

### OK, with a note (no change needed; keep the wording as it is)
- **「他去吴国服侍夫差三年」 is 《国语》 only, and the stamp already says so (「《国语》：在吴三年」). Keep that stamp.** 《史记·越王句践世家》 tells it differently: **范蠡** goes to 吴 as the hostage, for two years: 「使范蠡与大夫柘稽行成，为质於吴。二岁而吴归蠡。」 《史记》 never has 勾践 serve in 吴. If a commenter cites 《史记》, the answer is 《国语·越语下》 「与范蠡入宦于吴。三年，而吴人遣之」. The next line (「回国后…苦胆」, 《史记》 stamp) joins the two traditions, which is a normal synthesis.
- **「卧薪尝胆」 (H2) is an idiom label.** 《史记》 has only 尝胆, and 卧薪 is not in 《史记》 or 《吴越春秋》 (checked). The film shows only the 《史记》 尝胆. Fine.
- **「夫差自尽」:** the texts disagree on how he died. 《左传》 has 「乃缢」 (hanged himself), 《吴太伯世家》 「遂自刭死」 (cut his throat), 《越王句践世家》 「遂自杀」. 「自尽」 covers all three. The fallen cap plus dropped sword leans toward 《史记》. Acceptable, but **don't show a rope or a blade on a throat**.
- **「吴王答应了。伍子胥退下后说」:** the order matches 《左传》: 吴子将许之 → 伍员谏 → 「弗听，退而告人曰」 → 「三月，越及吴平」. ✓
- **G3 / G7 picture, the palace going under water:** this is a visual metaphor for 「吴其为沼」. Nothing records 吴 being flooded. It reads as metaphor because it sits in the mirror's shadow play; keep it.
- **「保住它的…主要是那座墓」:** 后德俊's view (「主要是保存环境使然，并非是经过特殊工艺处理」), and the stamp attributes it to him. 江旭东 gives the material as reason 1 of 3, and the film covers that with 「先说料好」. Fine.
- **「镀铬？…是热镀锡」:** the 2025 museum release does not mention chromium at all. It says what the pattern is (tin) without refuting chromium in so many words. The line claims no more than that, so it is fine. Keep the Cr tile as a question and never strike it through, as the storyboard already says.
- **Stamp 28 「1965 → 2026 · 据后德俊 · 渐失光泽」:** 后德俊 said it in 2016 (「出土几十年来…已经不如出土时明亮」). The arrow to 2026 is our framing. Fine.
- **「10.8米深」:** measured 「自现存封土堆至椁底板」 (from the surviving mound to the 椁 floor), not from the field surface. The line says 「10.8米深的楚墓」, which is how the source puts it. ✓
- **Paper test:** 湖北日报 2024 says 「十余张白纸」. The film follows the eyewitness 陈振裕 (30余张, 20多张, 1977), and the stamp names him. ✓
- **Width 「约5厘米」 is not on screen.** Sources disagree: museum 5 cm, 新京报 4.6 cm. Don't add it.
