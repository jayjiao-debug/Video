## Verdict: FIX FIRST

The cut is not ready for the owner: the one take is broken twice (a hard cut at 95.53 s and a one-frame brightness pop at 107.97 s), and the story's two key pictures (the paper cut at 59 s and the rust bloom at 75 s) are not readable. Facts on screen are clean: I found nothing outside the ✓ rows, 「镀铬？」 is never struck through, the counts and the 10.8 m tag agree with facts.md.

**The three things that hurt most**
1. **The one-take is not one take.** 95.533→95.567 s is a hard cut (from looking up through the fill to an empty dark museum floor). 107.967 s is a one-frame luminance drop (mean 42 → 29). 80.2–81.95 s is an expanding opaque disc that wipes to the next scene. 56.15–56.48 and 61.24–61.58 are crossfades between two blades that don't match.
2. **The payoff pictures are too small or missing.** The line "30多张纸，一下划破20多张" never shows the blade cutting the paper (the white stack is the brightest, biggest thing and the sword is a thin strip). The "其实，它也锈了" blooms are ~60 px olive dots (≈12 px on a phone). In 11.8–16.3, 83–91 and 95.6–99 the sword is 5–15 % of the frame width or a speck.
3. **The sword is not the same sword in every leg, and the people look cheap.** Lattice style, pitch and colour change between legs (tight tan diamonds 5–8 s; chain-link 47–56 s; big pale diamonds with dark centres 57–75 s; thin fishnet 101–105 s). The excavator "arms" at 41–52 s are two stiff navy sticks with block fists, off-palette.
