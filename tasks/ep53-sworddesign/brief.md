# Task: three accurate design options for the 越王勾践剑 model (for the owner to choose), ep53. Research the real sword, then draw.

You are the art director and prop designer for a Chinese Douyin channel of subtitle-only history shorts built in Remotion as code (SVG; we never show photos in the film: we draw the object from reference). Episode 53 is about 越王勾践剑 and the sword is the hero of every shot. The owner looked the real sword up and says **ours "looks kinda different"**. Before the next rebuild he wants **"a few designs"** of a more accurate sword to choose from.

## 1. Research (the most important part)
- Find several good reference photos of the real sword, from official or authoritative sources: 湖北省博物馆 (hbww.org.cn / its 珍宝档案 pages), 国家文物局, 中国国家博物馆, 光明日报 / 人民日报 / 新华社 picture pages, university or museum catalogue pages. Look for: the whole sword lying flat (front), the guard close-up (both faces: blue glass on one, turquoise on the other), the pommel with its concentric circles, the inscription close-up, and the sheath if shown. Download them to `/tmp/ref/` (curl/wget) and LOOK at them (Read the image files). **Never put any photo inside the repository** (it is public).
- Write down what the real sword looks like, as numbers and plain descriptions, citing which photo each comes from: overall length 55.6 cm (facts B1); blade width; hilt length; the exact OUTLINE of the blade (where it is widest, whether and where it narrows (the 吴越 "束腰" / 前锷收狭), the shape of the point, the 剑脊 ridge and the 从 / 锷 bands); the **diamond pattern** (how many diamonds across the blade, their proportion, line thickness, the colour of the dark lines against the ground, whether the edge bands are plain, how the pattern looks near the guard and near the tip, whether the inscription sits on top of the pattern); the colour of the metal today (gold? brown-gold? greenish patina? where?); the **guard** (its plan shape: a wide V / trapezoid?, its thickness, the inlay layout and the beast-face 兽面 pattern, blue glass on the front, turquoise on the back); the **grip** (round, two raised hoops 箍, traces of silk binding 缠缑); the **pommel** (an outturned disc 剑首, 11 concentric circles, its diameter relative to the grip); the **8 bird-script marks** 「越王鸠浅 / 自作用剑」 (two columns of four, where on the blade, their size and colour); the **black lacquer sheath** (shape, mouth, fittings) if references exist.
- Compare with OUR current spec (`episodes/37-qiqing/remotion/src/qq53/swordspec53s.tsx`, rendered by `sword3d.tsx`, `flat53s.tsx`, `land53.tsx`, `macro53.tsx`) and list every difference you find, ranked by how much it hurts recognisability.

## 2. Three design options, drawn
Build a test composition `SWORDDESIGN` in a NEW file `episodes/37-qiqing/remotion/src/qq53/sworddesign53x.tsx` (plus helper files named `*53x.tsx`; don't edit the existing sword files). One frame per option, plus one comparison frame, each 1920×1080, style B「漆」 (dark warm background, the sword the brightest thing), labelled in a small top band (option name + one line). Each option frame shows: the whole sword lying flat, large (≥ 85 % of the frame width); below it, three close-ups at the same scale for all options: the guard + inscription zone, the pommel face, the grip with its hoops; and the blade pattern at macro scale.
- **Option A, "museum-faithful":** as close to the photos as our flat-vector style allows: true proportions, true pattern scale and line weight, true colours of the metal today.
- **Option B, "clean hero":** the same accurate geometry and details, but the colours and contrast tuned for a phone screen at small size (the pattern still reads when the sword is 30 % of the frame width).
- **Option C, your best judgment:** whatever you think gives the most recognisable 越王勾践剑 for a 20-year-old Chinese viewer who knows it from the textbook photo.
- **Comparison frame:** our CURRENT sword (render it with the existing `Sword3D`/flat renderer at the same view) next to A, B, C, small, with labels.
Render all frames yourself (`ENTRY=qq53/sworddesign53x CID=SWORDDESIGN SFPS=1 BUNDLE_DIR=$(mktemp -d) REMOTION_BROWSER=<path> node scripts/frame_ms.mjs stills /tmp/sd 0 1 2 3`; setup: in `episodes/37-qiqing/remotion`, `npm ci`; Remotion needs Chromium's new headless shell: `find ~/.cache/ms-playwright -type f \( -name 'chrome-headless-shell' -o -name 'headless_shell' \)` → `REMOTION_BROWSER`, or `npx @puppeteer/browsers install chrome-headless-shell@stable` in /tmp). Put each option side by side with the reference photos (in /tmp only) and fix what doesn't match. Keep every option buildable: write each as parameters/variants of ONE shared spec (outline function, lattice constants, guard/grip/pommel/marks drawing) so the chosen one can replace `swordspec53s.tsx` cleanly.
