Honesty notes: the table is a *model*, not a prediction; it will be called 「估算」 on screen. Ma Yuanjiang (#23) and the Guinness case (#3) show that real survival can run longer than the model, which is why the film says so and says 别试.

## Most surprising / story-worthy
1. A 70 kg adult carries ~42 L of water, yet even doing nothing loses ~1–1.5 L a day: skin 0.5 + breath 0.5 + urine ≥0.5 (#6–#9). You leak water by breathing.
2. The "3 days" rule is real but it's the hot-weather fast case, and 72 h is a "生理极限" doctors quote (#1, #2).
3. 汶川 2008: 马元江 buried 179 h, doctors had said 72 h is the usual limit; he told himself “一定要活下去” (#23).
4. The body protects the blood first and takes water from cells and tissue (#12); thirst is an alarm triggered when the blood gets more concentrated (#13).
5. Guinness's longest recorded case is 18 days, but it is a single outlier whose details are disputed (#3, #4).
6. Weight-loss thresholds are small: ~2 % hurts endurance, ~5 % brings headaches, 15–20 % of body water is where death usually occurs (#14–#16, #2).
