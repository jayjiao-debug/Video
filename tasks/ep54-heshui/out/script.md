## 6. Risks / what I wasn't sure of
- **The day-by-day table is a model, not data.** No allowed source gives per-day labels. I took skin 0.5 L + breath 0.5 L + urine ≥0.5 L (facts 7–9), allowed for metabolic water/less urine (fact 10) → 1.0–1.5 L, used 1.25 L/day for a 70 kg person (42 L). Symptom thresholds (2 %, 5 %, 8–10 % of body weight; 15–20 % of body water) come from one 2025 review plus ACSM/Cheuvront; the review quotes older papers (Craig 1966, Wolf 1958) that I did not open. The film says 「估算」 and shows the assumption once.
- **% of weight vs % of body water.** The review gives 5 % and 8–10 % of *body weight* but death at 15–20 % of *body water* (≈9–12 % of weight). Stamps use % of body weight; only the gauge band uses body water. A reviewer should check the stamps vs the band.
- **StatPearls pages could not be opened** (NCBI blocks scripts); I quoted the PubMed/Europe PMC abstracts. StatPearls' own clinical severity bands conflict between pages, so the film avoids "mild/moderate/severe".
- **18 days (Guinness).** Verified for "forgotten, 18 days, longest recorded case without food and water". The wall-condensation detail, the exact discovery date (18 vs 19 April) and the location (Höchst vs Bregenz) vary between secondary sources, so none is on screen. The 1979 newspaper text (Press/Reuters, Arbeiter-Zeitung, Hamburger Abendblatt) could not be opened.
- **汶川.** 马元江 is named because 新华网 named him (2008-06-05, read on 央视网). Only "179 h", "没吃没喝", and his own words are used; no injuries, no body, no face. The same article says 181 h without food or drink from his last meal; the film uses the headline 179 h. The "72 h" is a doctor's statement in that article.
- **Official trapped-survivor advice**: the 应急管理部 page also says urine can be drunk "if necessary" — not used (brief). "少出汗" was not found in any official text, so it is not in the film.
- **Dropped:** "brain shrinks" (2014 MRI found no change after 16 h thirst), "blood thickens" (reworded 体液变浓), any Mihavecz condensation claim, per-day symptom labels from infographics.
- **The model says death by day 6–7, yet 马元江 lived 179 h and Mihavecz 18 days.** The film addresses it (line D7b "估算…都会改写它", O1 "极端个例", W2–W4). Owner may want one more line if it still feels contradictory.
