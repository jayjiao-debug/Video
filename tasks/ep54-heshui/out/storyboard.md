# storyboard.md — 《假如你7天不喝水》

Look: flat-vector lacquer; faceless one-tone silhouettes with a thin warm rim; gold hairlines; **water is the only true cool-white/blue** (gauge, glass, droplets). 时代色 → 日色: day 0–1 fresh cool teal · day 2 dusty olive · day 3 ochre · day 4–5 hot red-ochre/parched red · day 6–7 cold grey-violet. Furniture on every frame: subtitle top-left, two-line stamp top-right, hollow J seal bottom-right, no bottom-left counter. Colour keys follow `palette51.ts`/`master51.tsx` (cream #FFF6E6, gold #E8BC66, cinnabar #D0583C, rim #F0B262).

Sound effects used (24 cues are listed; reused ones count once): the owner asked for about 12–15 distinct SFX — distinct set: water-drop plink, room tone, riser, title thud, breath whoosh, drip, quiz tick, gauge level-slide, heat drone, dry crackle, heartbeat thump, sub drop, door clank, bell, knocks, glass clink/pour (16).

Key frames: `board/B01.html … B17.html`, 1920×1080, same order as below.
