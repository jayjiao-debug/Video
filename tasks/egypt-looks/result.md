I made all 9 style frames (3 looks × 3 frames) and `looks.md`, and committed only HTML and Markdown. I checked every frame at full size and at 25 % in `/tmp/eg`, and fixed the weak spots I found. The branch is not pushed.

The files are `A1`–`A3.html`, `B1`–`B3.html`, `C1`–`C3.html` and `looks.md` in `tasks/egypt-looks/out/`. `looks.md` has the palettes, subtitle designs, animation cost and recommendation for each look. There are no PNG or JPG files in the repo.

I used your exact subtitle lines and stamps. Subtitles are 58–62 px, stamps are 40 px, "230万" is 196 px and the rest of that line is 120 px. The J seal is about 70 px at 65 % opacity, and there is no bottom-left counter. Hieroglyphs are generic decorative shapes only. The left and right glyph columns in A sit inside the 120 px edge margin; they are decoration, not content.

## A 「墓室壁画」 tomb painting
- **Summary:** Plaster wall texture and registers with ground lines. It has a khekher frieze, hieroglyph side columns, and flat ochre/lapis/malachite figures with thin outlines in the Egyptian canon. The subtitle sits in a cream cartouche with a lapis bar.
- **Recommendation:** The most clearly Egyptian look. It is the cheapest and most consistent to animate, so it is good for transitions and "people at work" scenes.
- **Weak:**
  - The keyword is vermilion, not gold, because gold doesn't read on the cream cartouche. Gold appears only on the sun disc and pyramid caps.
  - The haulers' arms read slightly crossed.
  - The sledge block is plain.

## B 「黄金与青金石」 gold and lapis
- **Summary:** A night-dark lapis sky with stars, gold line-art and gold silhouettes with fine inner lines. The subtitle is on a lapis band with a gold, turquoise and carnelian frieze edge and a winged sun disc at each end. Turquoise and carnelian are kept to small inlays so gold stays the hero.
- **Recommendation:** The most luxurious look. I'd use it for treasure and big-number beats rather than a whole film.
- **Weak:**
  - The dark ground makes it the least "full-colour" of the three, against your 「全彩」 wish.
  - The winged-disc wings are small.
  - The block's cross-hatched joint lines look slightly busy.

## C 「沙海电影感」 desert cinema
- **Summary:** A modern flat-vector film look with sunset and dawn skies, hazy layered pyramids, the Nile, palms and a half-built pyramid with a ramp. The subtitle is a papyrus scroll with a hieroglyph frieze on both edges, a lotus at one end and an ankh at the other.
- **Recommendation:** My overall pick for the channel. It is the most cinematic and the most colourful, and it animates best (parallax, sunrise, god-rays). Egypt still shows in the scroll, glyph rows and figure canon.
- **Weak:**
  - The big sky gradients may band in H.264, so add grain.
  - The foreground blocks in C3 are a bit muddy.
  - The figures are shaded flat with no outline, so they are less crisp than A's.

I suggest C for the main scenes, A-style register wipes for the people-at-work scenes and B for the number beat.

I took the sample lines as the style test, so nothing is sourced. The scene, figures and stamps are placeholders.
