# What works for Chinese 18–25 viewers (mostly young men) on Douyin, Bilibili and 视频号 for 泛知识 content, 2023–2026

Scope note: these notes are for a roughly 2-minute, landscape, subtitle-only, music-only, animated knowledge video. Research ran on 2026-10-04. Each finding is marked [OFFICIAL] (platform, regulator or company executive), [MEDIA] (journalist or industry outlet reporting data) or [ANECDOTAL] (operator blogs, MCN tips, self-media). Many Chinese "algorithm" articles are SEO or marketing content with no official basis. They are flagged where that applies. Several key questions had only thin sourcing; see the Gaps sections.

## 1. Platform mechanics and benchmarks (完播率, 5-second retention, 互动率, 复播, landscape vs portrait, Bilibili mid-length norms, 视频号)

### Takeaway
Douyin has not published numeric weights or thresholds for 完播率, 2s/5s retention or 互动率. Every "流量池 / X% completion" figure in circulation is operator folklore. What Douyin has said officially, at its September 2025 creator conference, is that it is boosting "优质内容" and long-watch-time verticals, including 人文社科. On Bilibili the norm is long-form: the average video length across 2025 was about 20 minutes, and 泛知识 was officially about 45% of plays back in 2021. 视频号 distributes through social trust, meaning content spreads via friends' likes, and its audience skews older (26–30).

Play duration of 优质内容 is 17 times that of ordinary content. 优质内容 play duration doubled year on year in 17 verticals, including 游戏, 二次元 and 人文社科. **视频号**
- [MEDIA aggregator, ~2025, low confidence] 视频号 runs on "熟人信任" (social-graph) distribution, unlike Douyin's "广场式" algorithm. Over 40% of its users are aged 26–30, and 19–29 is the fastest-growing age band. The same article claims a "1.2x base coefficient" for 科普/education and "2.3x fan value" for knowledge accounts. These numbers cannot be traced to an official source and should be treated as unverified. — [数英 digitaling](https://www.digitaling.com/articles/1363193.html)

### Inferences
- Douyin publishes no numeric thresholds, so a 2-minute video cannot be tuned to a target 完播率. The defensible goal is to maximise average watch time and avoid early drop-off. That fits Douyin's own official emphasis on play duration (the 220% figure and the "17x" comparison).
- Douyin is officially pushing 人文社科, 科技 and 科普 depth content. A 2-minute animated knowledge piece fits the "中视频 / 优质内容" track better than the 15-second entertainment track.
- On Bilibili, 2 minutes is very short against a roughly 20-minute average. It will read as a "短片" or trailer-style piece, so it must be dense and rewatchable. A 合集 (series) of short pieces may work better there than single uploads.
- On 视频号, shareability to friends and family groups matters more than 弹幕 or meme density. With its 26–30+ audience, it is a weaker fit for an 18–25 male target.

### Gaps
- No official Douyin numbers for completion-rate or 5-second-retention benchmarks for 1–3-minute videos. I found no credible 巨量引擎, 新榜, 飞瓜 or 卡思 benchmark tables in accessible sources. Treat any "30–40% completion is good" claim as folklore.
- No official Douyin data comparing landscape and portrait performance for 泛知识.
- No current (2024–2026) official Bilibili share of plays for 泛知识. The 45% figure dates from 2021.
- No reliable gender split or 18–25 split for knowledge viewers on any of the three platforms.

## 2. Audience tastes of 18–25 men for knowledge content (topics, tone, what feels cringe, 弹幕 and comment bait)

### Takeaway
Credible evidence shows that the knowledge creators who grew fastest on Bilibili mixed rigour with entertainment: 半佛仙人's 表情包 + 脱口秀 style and 毕导's absurd premise + rigorous calculation. The 2026 youth report frames young users as "pleasing oneself" and seeking "instrumental" knowledge, especially AI and 科技. Military content has real legal risk (see section 6). I found no quantified survey of gender- or age-specific topic over-indexing.

### Cited Findings
- [MEDIA, ~2020] 半佛仙人 explained finance by combining "表情包与脱口秀类段子结合热点品牌". He reached 2.499M Bilibili followers with 28 videos in 3 months, every one over 1M views. — [36氪](https://www.36kr.com/p/1725380689921)
- [MEDIA] 毕导 (Tsinghua PhD) pairs absurdist premises (for example, whether to eat potato chips dropped on the floor) with rigorous calculation. He built his audience on a WeChat public account before moving to video. — [人人都是产品经理](https://www.woshipm.com/it/5499297.html)
- [OFFICIAL joint report, 2026-01] Young users seek "instrumental rationality of acquiring solutions" and treat AI content as high practical value. Video volume for DIY and hands-on content "surged several times in 2025". — [Bilibili × CTR via Tiger](https://www-web.itiger.com/news/1133690869)
- [OFFICIAL, 2025] 游戏, 二次元 and 人文社科 are among the 17 Douyin verticals where play duration of quality content doubled. — [新华网](https://www.news.cn/tech/20250924/187cfb6418bd4e8c94111c273cc2bbaf/c.html)
- [OFFICIAL, 2025] 影视飓风 (film tech and experiments) drew the most 弹幕 on Bilibili in 2025, 2.37M. This shows that 弹幕 interaction scales with "experiment / reveal" content. — [界面新闻](https://m.jiemian.com/article/13901729.html)

### Inferences
- The likely topic sweet spot for this demographic is 科技/AI, 军事装备 history (carefully framed), 经济/金融 "how it really works", and 冷知识/反常识 physics. This rests on the creators who succeeded and on the official vertical-growth signals, not on a demographic survey.
- Tone should be "rigorous but self-aware": absurd premise, real maths, dry jokes. For a music-only, subtitle-only video, the "voice" has to live in the subtitle copy itself, with short, punchy, 吐槽-style lines.
- Cringe triggers (说教, 爹味, 营销号腔, AI味) are widely discussed in Chinese internet culture, but I did not find a credible survey quantifying them. Treat this as practitioner knowledge, not evidence.

### Gaps
- No credible survey of over-indexing topics among 18–25 men specifically. 新榜, 卡思 and QuestMobile reports were not reachable.
- No source quantified the effect of 弹幕 or comment bait (留悬念, 站队 questions) on reach.
- No source measured the "AI味" penalty on audience reception.

## 3. Language devices that land (金句, contrast, rhetorical questions, slang, numbers, second person, student-life 共鸣)

### Takeaway
I found no rigorous source on copy devices for knowledge videos. The only data-backed title case found is Bilibili's 2021 "反正没人看" trend, where curiosity-gap titles carrying no information reached 2.59M–9.5M views. The source itself notes that content, not the title, keeps viewers.

### Cited Findings
- [MEDIA, 2021-08-20, 刺猬公社] Low-information, self-deprecating titles drove large views for amateur student creators: "就是因为没人看，我才敢发上来的" reached 9.5M views, and similar titles reached 2.59M and 7M+. The article stresses "能够留住用户的还是视频内容". — [人人都是产品经理 / 刺猬公社](https://www.woshipm.com/it/5082348.html)
- [MEDIA, ~2023] 小Lin说 is known for explaining complex finance through vivid analogies, for example describing a trust as "打仗托妻". She describes her aim as "把复杂的事情解释得简单易明、生动有趣". — [ourchinastory](https://www.ourchinastory.com/cn/amp/10214)

### Inferences
- Analogy-first explanation (the 小Lin说 style) and self-deprecating curiosity titles both have case support. Second-person framing, numbers in titles and student-life references (宿舍, 早八, 考研) are common practitioner advice, but I found no measured evidence for them.
- 网络热词 date quickly. For an evergreen landscape video, keep slang light and reserve it for one or two subtitle punchlines, not titles.

### Gaps
- No A/B or platform data on title devices (numbers, questions, "你") for Chinese knowledge videos.
- No source on how fast 网络热词 lose effect.

## 4. Case studies with numbers (format choices that drove growth)

### Takeaway
The best-documented breakout knowledge creators (罗翔, 半佛仙人, 巫师财经, 小Lin说, 何同学, 影视飓风) mostly grew on Bilibili with 5–15+ minute videos, high research investment, and a distinctive persona or comedic voice. Early growth came from very few uploads (12–28 videos). The large-scale animated cases (回形针, 混知, 柴知道, 漫士沉思录) lacked accessible, credible format data.

### Cited Findings
- [MEDIA, 2020] 罗翔说刑法 gained 3.795M Bilibili followers within 25 days of joining. — [36氪](https://www.36kr.com/p/1725380689921)
- [OFFICIAL 百大 data, ~2026-01] 罗翔说刑法 had 32M+ followers and six consecutive Bilibili 百大 selections. 何同学 had 12M+ followers and returned to 百大 for 2025 after missing 2024; his annual best-work award was for a single-hand keyboard accessibility video. 影视飓风 had 14M+ followers and the most 弹幕 in 2025 (2.37M), with e-commerce attached. 百大 creators averaged nearly 7 years of production experience. — [界面新闻](https://m.jiemian.com/article/13901729.html)
- [MEDIA, ~2020] 巫师财经 reached 2.8M followers in about six months with only 12 videos. He was later hit by plagiarism controversy. — [ifanr](https://www.ifanr.com/1317254); [36氪](https://www.36kr.com/p/1725380689921) (the 36氪 figure is 2.913M followers from 13 videos)
- [MEDIA, ~2020] 半佛仙人 had 2.499M followers from 28 videos, each over 1M views within 3 months, using 表情包 + 段子 style. — [36氪](https://www.36kr.com/p/1725380689921)
- [MEDIA, ~2023] 小Lin说 (Lindsay Zou; PKU finance, Columbia; ex-JP Morgan) makes episodes of about 10+ minutes. Each takes 1–2 months of research and 3–4 hours of filming. Topics include ChatGPT, the SVB collapse and the energy crisis. — [ourchinastory](https://www.ourchinastory.com/cn/amp/10214)
- [MEDIA] 毕导 pairs an absurd premise with rigorous calculation. — [人人都是产品经理](https://www.woshipm.com/it/5499297.html)
- [OFFICIAL, 2025] Institutional 公开课 also scale on Douyin: PKU's 12-course series had 66M+ plays, and its courses overall reached 100M+ viewers. — [光明网](https://m.gmw.cn/toutiao/2025-04/25/content_37990361.htm)

### Inferences
- The common pattern is a clear persona or voice, an analogy-led explanation, a heavy research investment per piece, and a distinctive visual system. A subtitle-only, music-only animated video has no on-camera persona. It therefore needs a recognisable visual signature and a recurring subtitle "voice" or catchphrase to build the identity that these creators get from their faces and voices.
- Most of these creators broke out with 5–15-minute Bilibili videos, not 2-minute ones. A 2-minute format is closer to Douyin norms. On Bilibili it may suit a series or 合集 better.

### Gaps
- I could not verify credible, dated format or metric data for 回形针PaperClip, 星球研究所, 混知/混子曰, 柴知道, 漫士沉思录, 二次元的中科院物理所, 无穷小亮 or 李永乐老师. Searches returned no usable primary sources within the tool budget.
- One source quotes 影视飓风 figures ("20 million fans, 6.18 million total views, 1.8 million yuan") that look garbled or inconsistent. They are excluded. — [ifanr](https://www.ifanr.com/1317254)

## 5. Packaging (title formulas, covers, first frame, hashtags, 合集) and AI-content labelling

### Takeaway
I found no credible quantified evidence on title or cover formulas for knowledge videos. AI labelling, on the other hand, is a firm legal requirement: since 2025-09-01, China's 《人工智能生成合成内容标识办法》 requires explicit and implicit labels. Douyin requires creators to declare "作品含AI生成内容" and auto-labels content it detects as "疑似使用了AI生成技术". Removing or hiding labels is prohibited, and unlabelled AI content risks 限流 or removal.

### Cited Findings
- [OFFICIAL, issued 2025-03-14, effective 2025-09-01, CAC] AI-generated content must carry explicit labels users can perceive (text, sound or graphics) and implicit labels in file metadata. Platforms must detect AI content and control its spread. Maliciously removing, altering, faking or hiding labels is forbidden. — [国家网信办](https://www.cac.gov.cn/2025-03/14/c_1743654685899683.htm); [新华网 2025-03-19](https://www.news.cn/tech/20250319/a572ec4366f149c1b0b04a07ebf4931b/c.html)
- [MEDIA, 2025-09] From 2025-09-01, Douyin requires creators to add the "作品含AI生成内容" declaration and uses metadata detection to add "疑似使用了AI生成技术" labels to undeclared content. Weibo, Xiaohongshu, Bilibili and WeChat likewise require self-declaration plus auto-tagging. Penalties for violations include removal, throttling and bans. Some creators reported being mislabelled ("误伤") or limited after labelling. This is anecdotal, and no platform states that labelled content is down-ranked. — [36氪](https://www.36kr.com/p/3451032302147200)
- [MEDIA, 2021] The Bilibili "反正没人看" curiosity-gap titles are the only title-performance case with view numbers found (see section 3). — [人人都是产品经理](https://www.woshipm.com/it/5082348.html)

### Inferences
- If AI tools generate any visuals, voices or music in the video, the safe course is to declare "作品含AI生成内容" on every platform. Douyin's metadata detection will otherwise flag it, and the penalty for unlabelled content (限流 or removal) is clearer than any penalty for labelled content. Video made by code, such as Remotion animation, is probably not "AI-generated synthetic content" in the legal sense. But any AI-generated images, models or music inside it may be, and AI-generated metadata in assets can trigger auto-labels.
- First frame = cover, big-character covers, and 合集 strategy are standard practitioner advice. Bilibili's long-form norm and the official push for series content are consistent with 合集 use, but I found no measured evidence.

### Gaps
- No official or credible data on title length, cover style (大字 / 人物 / 对比) or hashtag effects for 泛知识 in 2023–2026.
- No platform statement on whether correctly AI-labelled content gets less reach.

## 6. Risks: topics and framing that trigger moderation or 限流 for knowledge accounts

### Takeaway
The clearest documented risk for a young-male knowledge audience is military content. China's Ministry of State Security warned in December 2023 and March 2024 that sharing military equipment, deployments, restricted-zone details or insider knowledge can bring 3–7-year sentences in the most serious cases. Beyond that, I found no credible public documentation of moderation rules for politics, foreign governments, disasters or death imagery in knowledge videos.

### Cited Findings
- [MEDIA reporting OFFICIAL warnings, 2024-03-26] Risky content for 军迷 creators includes photos of advanced weapons and deployments, technical specs, theater-command analysis, military construction, aircraft markings and plane-spotting, and restricted zones. The Ministry of State Security warned in December 2023 of "3-7 years" for the most serious cases and in March 2024 targeted ex-insiders who use classified-adjacent knowledge to gain followers. A January 2024 case involved a blogger showing how to sneak into a restricted military zone in Wuhan. — [RFA 2024-03-26](https://www.rfa.org/english/news/china/natsec-03262024161920.html) (RFA is US-government funded; the underlying MSS warnings are official Chinese statements)
- [MEDIA] The regulator requires platforms to control the spread of unlabelled AI content, and the 36氪 report lists throttling and bans as enforcement tools. — [36氪](https://www.36kr.com/p/3451032302147200); [国家网信办](https://www.cac.gov.cn/2025-03/14/c_1743654685899683.htm)

### Inferences
- For military or history topics, stay with historical, publicly documented and non-PRC-current material, and avoid specs or locations of current PLA equipment.
- For foreign-government or geopolitical topics, neutral, mechanism-based framing (how something works) is likely safer than opinion or 站队 framing. Comment-bait that asks viewers to take sides on politics is likely to backfire. This is an inference; no source documents it.

### Gaps
- No credible public source documenting 限流 triggers for politics, disasters or death imagery in knowledge videos. Moderation rules are not public, and creator accounts of them are anecdotal and were not found in reliable form.
