# 第一案 · 雨夜书房 (Case 1: The Study on a Rainy Night), v2

Vertical 9:16, about 2:36. The viewer is the detective: the camera is your eyes, a lantern lights where you look, suspects answer you in interrogation boxes, and clues fill your notebook. Sherlock only hands over the case and explains at the end.

## Why it's harder now
- **Two layers.** The obvious answer (the nephew, who lies about his arrival time) is wrong.
- **The key clue is never pointed out.** A dark-red hair lies on the saucer in the tea close-up and on the evidence card at the pause, but nobody mentions it until the reveal.
- **The umbrellas aren't labelled wet or dry.** The doctor's is dripping and the nephew's isn't. The viewer has to notice.

## What the viewer is shown
| Clue | Detail |
|---|---|
| Clock knocked to the floor | stopped at 9:45 |
| Tea, half drunk | poison in the cup, none in the pot (so it went in after 9:40); **a dark-red hair on the saucer (not mentioned)** |
| Window | rain started at 9:30 |
| Will, ink still wet | "From tomorrow: if the nephew marries the secretary, he loses his whole inheritance." |
| Butler | brought the tea at 9:40, then stayed in the kitchen; the cook confirms it |
| Secretary | typed upstairs from 8 to 10; the typing never stopped for two hours |
| Nephew | "I walked from the station in the rain, got in at 10:05." He hides his hands; his fingertips are black: "ink from writing letters" |
| Umbrella stand | the doctor's (just arrived) is dripping; **the nephew's is dry (not said)** |

## Pause
"Who poisoned him? Pause and put your reasoning in the comments." Evidence board with 6 cards and a 5-second countdown.

## Solution
1. Most people say the nephew. He did lie: his umbrella is dry, so he arrived before the 9:30 rain.
2. Where was he? His black fingertips are typewriter ribbon, not ink. The second half of the nonstop typing was him.
3. So where was the secretary? Go back to the tea: a dark-red hair on the saucer. The butler's hair is white and the nephew's is blond; only she has dark red.
4. At 9:42 she slipped downstairs, poisoned the cup, and went back up to keep typing, because tomorrow's will would leave her with nothing.
5. **The secretary did it. The nephew was the helper she used.**
