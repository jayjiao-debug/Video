# Brief: 鸡皮疙瘩 (working title) — 为什么音乐让人起鸡皮疙瘩

- **Episode:** ep13 · **Series:** VIBE知识大赏 (Juno)
- **Idea in one sentence:** 起鸡皮疙瘩不是因为音乐"好听"，而是因为你的大脑被吊了胃口，又刚好被满足——张力和释放；把那一刻从歌里删掉，鸡皮疙瘩就没了。
- **Format idea (owner, 2026-10-06):** "explain music theory by doing real-time changes on the bgm." The film's own background track IS the experiment: we change it live, on screen, and the viewer hears the difference (delete the drop, play the drop without the build, remove the silent breath before the drop, delay the drop, muffle then open the filter, …).
- **Music:** the owner's channel track `explainer/assets/music/bgm.mp3` (164.5 s, ~118 BPM, a finished mix: intro build → first drop 16.6 s → loud → break 49.1 s → build 65.2 s → one quiet breath bar ~79.7 s → **hardest drop 81.39 s** → loud → outro). Because it is a mixed track, the changes are edits on the audio itself (cut/repeat/reorder bars, filters, volume, speed/pitch, silence), not re-orchestration.
- **Why a 18–25 viewer cares:** everyone has felt the shiver at a drop/high note; few know why, and the film lets them feel it switch on and off with their own ears. 看完会发给谁？给那个听歌会起鸡皮疙瘩的朋友 / 给做音乐的朋友。
- **Takeaway:** 鸡皮疙瘩 = 大脑预测 + 等待 + 刚好兑现（还带一点意外）。
- **Length target:** 100–130 s · **Format:** 1920×1080, subtitle-only (no voice-over: the music is the subject), the owner's bgm edited live.
- **Visual direction:** new look (not the navy+gold family, not ep12's phosphor scope); something that makes sound visible and "editable" on screen; 2–3 looks for the owner to pick.
- **Must include:** the live edits with an on-screen indicator of what is being changed (a cursor/scissor/knob…), credible science on chills (frisson): anticipation and dopamine, prediction/expectation, which musical events trigger chills; a moment where the viewer can test themselves; an end-card question ("发给谁").
- **Must avoid:** claims not in facts.md; Wikipedia/百度百科/知乎; "Juno 出品"; realistic hands/faces; music that clips or pumps badly; on-screen text fighting the music at the demo moments (the ear is the hero there).
- **Owner approvals:** gate 1 (title + lines), gate 2a (look), gate 4 (the cut on a phone, with sound).
