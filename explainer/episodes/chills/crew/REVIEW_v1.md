## What works (keep)
- **The title lands on the hardest drop exactly.** The picture stamps at frame 8.100 and the audio drop is at 8.098 (extra/title_hit_7.97-8.37.png). The cart tips over the lip on the same frame.
- **Edits are on the measured bar grid.** Every edit point has a strong onset (7–27× the median onset strength), and no join has a discontinuity. The master is −14.8 LUFS with a true peak of −4.4 dBTP in the WAV.
- **Demo 1 and Demo 4 prove what they say, even on a phone.**
  - Demo 1: 2.0 s of digital silence, the cart stopped dead at the cliff, and the timecode turned to `--:--.- 静音`.
  - Demo 4: the 2–8 kHz band drops 33 dB (−31.8 → −64.9 dB) at matched loudness (−17.9 vs −18.2 dB). On a 400 Hz–8 kHz phone-speaker proxy it is only 1.5 dB quieter, so it sounds muffled rather than vanishing. The grey drained rail and the 800 Hz knob make cause and effect visible.
- **Frame 0 is a bright, full poster.** It has the subtitle, the cart on the climb, and the strip with 铺垫/吸气/高潮. The strip, timecode jumps and the ✂ n/4 counter make a clear "we are editing this" vocabulary.
- **Script discipline.** Reading pace has 0 % waiting. "约三成" is always paired with 24个人. Demos 2 and 3 end in open questions. There are no "15 秒", drugs or sex claims and no "Juno 出品". The end card has the J monogram, gold title, question and follow pill.
- **The 2 Mbps two-pass Douyin delivery holds the riso halftone.** PSNR is 38 dB on a static frame and 35 dB on the shaking late plunge, with no visible blocking (extra/bitrate_2mbps_test_*.png).
