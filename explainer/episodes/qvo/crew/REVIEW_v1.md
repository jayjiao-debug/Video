**Auto-check flags I am discarding:**
- Bitrate 6.64 Mbps "too low": this is the HD master. Delivery is the ≤ 28 MB file, per the owner notes.
- pace.py "TOO FAST": the film is voiced at ≈ 5 字/s with 0.3–0.6 s breaths, which matches the reference.
- The 0:12.4–0:16.7 subtitle gap is the title card on the drop, so it is intended.
- The `2³⁰⁰…种组合` readout running past its box at 0:58.3 is storyboarded ("pushes through its box border"). It would read better if the border visibly broke into dashes where the text exits; right now it looks like an overflow.

What works (keep):
- **One world, fully committed:** every idea is a signal on the same scope. Bits are a square wave, the qubit is an XY coin, measurement is the trigger, combinations are a wall of traces, interference is the MATH channel. Most transitions are motivated by the instrument (zoom into a step, the cursor carries over, the wall collapses into one spike).
- **Hook and structure:** the first line is the viewer's own belief about the topic, with the subtitle on frame 0. The title lands on drop 1 (0:14.24). "不是。" plus the wall collapsing into one spike lands on drop 2 (1:02.77). The ending bookends back to the hook's fan, and the end-card question invites sharing.
- **The 1:14–1:21 interference payoff** (7 rows go flat, \|101⟩ turns red and grows to 94.1 % 示意, `RESULT 101`) is the clearest picture of the takeaway. Don't touch it beyond fix 8.
- **Claim discipline:** "估计", "示意", "将来 · 可能", "Google 估计" and "测速题 · 暂无实际用途 (Google)" are all in the right places, and the film never says Willow is useless. The numbers check out on screen: 10²⁵ shows 26 digits, the dot grid is 15×7 = 105, ≈ 1 万倍 = 10⁶/105, and 2³⁰⁰ ≈ 2×10⁹⁰ against ~10⁸⁰ atoms (估算).
- **End card on brand:** J monogram, gold title, coin motif, question, follow pill, sources. No "Juno 出品", gold appears only on the end card, and there are no hands, faces or maps.
