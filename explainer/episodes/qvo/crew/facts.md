## Story-worthy

1. **"先偷后解" (harvest now, decrypt later)** — NIST: adversaries "collect encrypted data now with the goal of decrypting it once quantum technology matures." For a 20-year-old: *the chat you encrypt today might already be stored, waiting.* Strongest hook — it makes the threat personal and present, without claiming a date (rows 24, 25).
2. **10^25 years for a useless question** — Willow did in <5 min what Google estimates would take a top supercomputer 10 septillion years ("vastly exceeds the age of the universe"), and Google itself admits the task has no practical use yet. The perfect "fast at what?" twist (rows 2–4).
3. **300 coins > every atom in the universe** — describing 300 qubits takes 2^300 ≈ 10^90 numbers, more than the ~10^80 atoms in the observable universe (rows 11, 12).
4. **Colder than space, on purpose** — ~10 mK, about 100× colder than outer space (2.7 K), so the metal superconducts and thermal noise stays out (rows 6–8).
5. **The number keeps falling** — RSA-2048 estimate: 20 million qubits (2019) → under 1 million (2025); 2026 papers go lower still. Meanwhile Willow has 105 (rows 18–20).
- **Strongest human story:** Peter Shor, a mathematician (his employer at the time is not confirmed in the sources I opened, so leave it off screen), in 1994 shows on paper that a machine nobody had built could break the encryption the whole internet uses. 30 years later governments are swapping out encryption because of a machine that still doesn't exist (rows 16, 22, 23).
- **Best visual image:** noise-cancelling headphones: a noise wave and its mirror-image wave meet and flatten to a line. Then the same picture with "wrong answers" cancelling and the "right answer" growing (rows 14, 15). Second option: the gold dilution-fridge "chandelier" sinking to 10 mK beside a 2.7 K starfield thermometer.

## Risks

- **Overclaiming timelines:** never give a year for "破解你的密码". NIST: "a few years to a few decades". The 2030/2035 dates are a NIST *draft* migration plan, not a prediction.
- **"同时算所有答案"** may appear only as the myth being corrected (marked ✗), never as narration stated as true.
- **"Willow 没用"** is wrong. Only the RCS benchmark has no known use; since Oct 2025 Willow has a 13,000× "verifiable" result aimed at molecules.
- **"今天只有约100个量子比特"** is false industry-wide (IBM Condor 1,121; Caltech 6,100 atoms). Tie the 105 to Willow.
- **Frontier name** is not confirmed in the Google text I opened; on screen use "最快的超级计算机之一".
- **10^25 years is Google's estimate**, and classical-simulation researchers have challenged earlier supremacy claims (2019). Phrase it as "Google 估计".
- **Real companies/people:** Google, IBM, Bose and Aaronson/Shor/Gidney are named only with their own sourced statements. No logos needed; don't imply endorsement. No realistic faces (brief).
- **Platform:** crypto and security content is factual and defensive (NIST migration). Avoid "教你破解" framing. Bitcoin/crypto-asset mentions (Google 2026 ECDLP post) could read as financial content; leave them out.
