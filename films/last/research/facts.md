## Story-worthy (top 5)

1. **48%**: after 7 years only 48% of your confidants and helpers are still in your network, and only 30% hold the same place. The circle stays the same size, but the people in it change (Mollenhorst / Utrecht, about 1,000 Dutch adults).
2. **Why they disappear: "a lack of meeting opportunities"**, in a peer-reviewed sentence (Mollenhorst, Volker & Flap 2014). The answer is lost meeting chances, not the universe.
3. **1.95 h → 0.76 h → 0.50 h**: daily time with friends at 18, 30 and 40 (ATUS 2010–2024). It falls by about 60% between 18 and 30, while time alone climbs to about 8 h by 80.
4. **The year after high school is the most dangerous year**: friendship dissolution peaks then, and only "over one third" of high-school friendships survive it (PROSPER, about 13,000 pairs). In Roberts & Dunbar's 18-month study, closeness to friends fell while closeness to family rose, and it fell most for those who moved away to university.
5. **1/(1−r)**: if each year's meeting chance is 80% of the previous year's, the expected total of all future meetings is only 5. A "last meeting" doesn't need a cosmic plan, only a shrinking series. Pair it with **200+ hours** to build a close friend (Hall / KU): at 30 minutes a day, that is more than a year of all your friend time.

## Risks

- **Mollenhorst numbers come from a press release.** 48% and 30% come from the 2009 NWO release about the dissertation. The 2014 paper's full text could not be opened, so cite "Mollenhorst 等, 乌得勒支大学" with year 2009 for the numbers and 2014 for the "lack of meeting opportunities" quote. The scope is confidants and practical helpers, not "all friends". "每7年换掉一半朋友" is the press wording; "一半不在了" is safer than "换掉".
- **Oswald & Clark 2003: no verified number.** The primary source was not accessible. Use only the direction ("best friends became close or casual friends"), or drop it in favour of McMillan 2025.
- **ATUS is US, cross-sectional and counts physical presence only.** Don't say "你会" as if one person were tracked. Say "美国人在 18 岁 / 30 岁…". Online chatting is not counted, and a sharp-eyed 18–25 viewer may object. Pre-empt it with "同处一室的时间".
- **Roberts & Dunbar is n = 25, UK, and b is a model estimate.** Don't present −0.62 as a percentage. Describe the direction and the friend-vs-family contrast.
- **Pew: 8% (2023) and 18% (2024)** come from different question wording. Pick one and label the year, or skip.
- **The r = 0.8 decay is an assumption, not data.** Mark it "假设" on screen. The math (1/(1−r)) is exact. The parameter is illustrative.
- **Origin of the meme.** Attribution to @maelexilamb comes only from Bustle. Say "2025 年 TikTok 上流行的说法" rather than naming a creator.
- **Not opened or blocked:** ScienceDirect (Mollenhorst 2014 full text), Wiley (Oswald & Clark), Springer/PMC (Roberts & Dunbar 2015 publisher page; I used the LJMU accepted manuscript instead), NBC News (robots), Crossref and Semantic Scholar (permission timed out).
