# 《对爸妈说“我爱你”，为什么这么难》 facts

| L1 | Wu & Chao 2017, *Asian American Journal of Psychology* 8(4):323–338. 15 Chinese-American 10th graders, interviews. All 15 described parental love as expressed "quietly"; only 4 of 15 said affection (hugs, "I love you") built closeness (qin). Love shown through devotion and sacrifice (education, money, needs). Small qualitative sample, immigrant families in LA | 15个孩子都说爸妈的爱是“安静的”；只有4个提到拥抱或“我爱你” | PMC5793938 |
