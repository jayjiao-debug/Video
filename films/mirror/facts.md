# 《哪个才是真的你》 facts

| # | Claim used in the film | Source | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| M1 | 1977 年实验：给女大学生拍照，冲洗正片和左右翻转的两版；她自己更喜欢翻转版（镜子里的样子），她的好朋友更喜欢正片 | Mita, Dermer & Knight (1977), "Reversed facial images and the mere-exposure hypothesis", *JPSP* 35(8) | 33 women each paired with a close female friend (fstoppers summary). No percentages used in film. |
| M2 | 熟悉的东西看着更顺眼 = 曝光效应 | Zajonc 1968, mere-exposure effect | Mita's paper tests exactly this |
| M3 | 人脸左右不对称，翻转后分缝、痣、高低眉都换边 | general | visual only |
| M4 | 很多手机前置预览是镜像的，存下来的照片会翻回去 | phone behaviour; iOS 14+ has 「镜像前置镜头」 setting | say 「很多手机」 |
| M5 | 自拍距离约 30 厘米，和 1.5 米外比，鼻子底部被拍宽约 30% | Paskhover & Fried et al. (2018), *JAMA Facial Plastic Surgery*; Rutgers press release 2018-03-01 | 12 in vs 5 ft; nasal base ~30% wider, nasal tip ~7% |
| M6 | 30 cm 时鼻子显长 6.4%，下巴缩小 12% | Amirlak et al. (2022), *Plastic and Reconstructive Surgery*, 30 volunteers | optional |
| M7 | 从一段说话视频里截出的静帧，91% 被评为不如视频本身好看 | Post, Haberman, Iwaki & Whitney (2012), "The frozen face effect", *Frontiers in Psychology* 3:22 | 6,254 / 6,844 non-tied stills rated less flattering; attractiveness 3.9 (still) vs 4.6 (video) |
| M8 | 大多数人会把美化过的脸认成自己（约 65% 选了至少美化 10% 的版本）；对好朋友也会美化，对陌生人不会 | Epley & Whitchurch (2008), "Mirror, mirror on the wall", *PSPB* 34(9) | 65% figure from secondary summary (everydaypsych); film says 「大多数人」 |
