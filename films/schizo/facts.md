# 《身体里的两个人》 facts (checked 2026-10-20)

| # | claim | source |
|---|---|---|
| Z1 | "Several people in one body" is dissociative identity disorder, a different condition; schizophrenia is not split personality | standard psychiatry (DSM-5 / ICD-11 are separate categories) |
| Z2 | Bleuler coined "schizophrenia" in Berlin on 24 April 1908; "splitting of psychic functioning" (schizo = split, phren = mind) | American Journal of Psychiatry 2008 (psychiatryonline, doi 10.1176/appi.ajp.2008.08050714) |
| Z3 | ~24 million people worldwide, ~1 in 300 (0.32 %); 1 in 222 adults | WHO fact sheet, via ScotPHO |
| Z4 | China: schizophrenia and other psychotic disorders, weighted lifetime prevalence 0.7 % (schizophrenia alone 0.6 %) | Huang et al., Lancet Psychiatry 2019 (China Mental Health Survey), via 搜狐转载 |
| Z5 | Luhrmann (Stanford): 60 people with schizophrenia, 20 each in San Mateo (US), Chennai, Accra. US voices harsh and threatening, none mainly positive; Chennai 11 of 20 heard family members, often elders advising; Accra 10 predominantly positive, 16 heard God | Stanford News 2014-07-16; British Journal of Psychiatry 2015 |
| Z6 | Japan renamed 精神分裂病 → 統合失調症 ("integration disorder") in 2002 to reduce stigma; the old term almost vanished from newspapers after 2004, but the share of crime words in headlines did not change (~24.5 %) | Koike et al. 2015, via Asian Scientist |
| Z7 | Taiwan renamed 精神分裂症 → 思覺失調症 from 7 May 2014, to remove stigma | 卫生福利部 2014-06-24 |
| Z8 | 12356 national psychological assistance hotline, unified nationwide from 1 May 2025, round the clock | 新华网 2025-05-08 |

Rules: no claim that people with schizophrenia are dangerous (the evidence is nuanced: most are not violent; risk rises with
substance use and no treatment) — the film does not go there. No diagnosis talk, no figure acting out symptoms.
"Hearing voices is the most common hallucination" and "it often begins in late adolescence / early adulthood" are textbook
knowledge (DSM-5); phrase without numbers.
