# 《身体里住着两个人》多重人格版 · facts (checked 2026-10-21)

| # | Claim used in the film | Source |
|---|---|---|
| D1 | DID: two or more personality states (alters / identities) that alternate, with recurrent memory gaps; often one identity does not know what another experienced (asymmetric amnesia) | MSD Manual Professional, "Dissociative Identity Disorder", https://www.msdmanuals.com/professional/psychiatric-disorders/dissociative-disorders/dissociative-identity-disorder |
| D2 | Formerly called multiple personality disorder; renamed in DSM-IV (1994) to reflect fragmentation of one identity rather than separate personalities | Psychology Today, "Dissociative Identity Disorder (Multiple Personality Disorder)", https://www.psychologytoday.com/us/conditions/dissociative-identity-disorder-multiple-personality-disorder ; DSM-IV completed 1994 (AJP book forum, doi 10.1176/ajp.156.10.1655) |
| D3 | DID and schizophrenia are different conditions | standard psychiatry (separate DSM-5 / ICD-11 categories) |
| D4 | Prevalence about 1–1.5 % of the general population (US); one small US study 1.5 % | MSD Manual (as D1); Cleveland Clinic, https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/diseases/9792-dissociative-identity-disorder-multiple-personality-disorder |
| D5 | Signs often appear between ages 5 and 10; usually not diagnosed until adulthood | Cleveland Clinic (as D4) |
| D6 | Chronic, severe childhood abuse or neglect reported by >70 % (up to 100 %) of patients | MSD Manual (as D1) |
| D7 | Dissociation is a way the mind copes with too much stress / trauma; "a normal defence mechanism" during trauma | NHS, "Dissociative disorders", https://www.nhs.uk/mental-health/conditions/dissociative-disorders/ |
| D8 | The trauma model ("the separated parts develop into identities") is the main explanation; some experts argue symptoms can be partly iatrogenic — phrased in the film as 一种主流解释 | Psychology Today (as D2); MSD Manual (as D1) |
| D9 | Switching is often not overt: in the common non-possession form, switches are "less overt" and hard for others to notice | MSD Manual (as D1); Cleveland Clinic (as D4) |
| D10 | Suicidal behaviour and self-injury are common; DSM-5: more than 70 % of outpatients with DID have attempted suicide | MSD Manual (as D1); Psychology Today citing DSM-5 (as D2) |
| D11 | Treatment: long-term psychotherapy, safety and stabilisation first; most desirable outcome is integration of identity states, otherwise cooperation among them; many improve | MSD Manual (as D1); Psychology Today (as D2) |
