# 《两个我》 shot list v2 (for visual-director)

## Look (art bible in one paragraph)
- **Mood:** melancholic, quiet, restrained. Night, rain, fog, empty rooms. Long slow camera moves, held frames that
  stay alive (rain, steam, lamp flicker, a string still trembling). Silence is part of the picture. Not horror: no
  sudden scares, no distorted faces, no shadows that move on their own, no red flashes.
- **Palette:** desaturated blue-grey night (ink `#0b1018` → fog `#5d6b7a`), wet surfaces with cold rim light; **one warm
  amber lamp** per shot (the only warm source until the drop). Gold only on answer keywords, the title, the 调 reveal
  and the end card; red only on {两个人} / {威胁}. Grain + light fog layer on every set.
- **Recurring motif:** the lamp and **the strings (弦)** — fine silver strings from the puppet's head, wrists and chest rising
  into the dark to a wooden peg-head (like a violin scroll) with four pegs. No hand ever holds them (they are the mind's
  own coordination, never a puppeteer). Title/end-card motif in gold: one ball joint with a single string rising from it.
- **Cast (all wooden mannequins: wood grain, ball joints at neck/shoulders/elbows/wrists/hips/knees/ankles, no face):**
  **他** — pale ash wood, the protagonist; **companion** — darker walnut, appears only at the end;
  **Bleuler** — round wire spectacles + dark frock coat; **Luhrmann** — person-card bust with a scarf;
  window figures in the three cities and the apartment block — small, mostly still.
- **Sets:** ① 他的房间 (small rented room, rain-streaked double-glazed window, wooden table, desk lamp, cup, old radio,
  peg-head in the dark above) ② the apartment block in rain (20 × 15 = 300 windows) ③ 1908 Berlin lecture hall
  (gas lamp, tall wet windows, chalkboard) ④ map table under a lamp ⑤ one long night street "around the world" with three
  windows (San Mateo, Chennai, Accra) ⑥ hospital corridor at night (enamel sign, three brass plates). Every cut carries
  something across: glass, chalk dust, waveform → thread, string, 调 → peg, window.
- **Puppet acting:** one clear action per shot, from motion reference (`character-motion`); everyone else gets idle
  breathing only. Puppets never act out symptoms; voices live in the radio, data in objects and numbers.

★ = highlight (should work as a still / cover).

## Highlights (stills / cover candidates)
1. **Shot 10** — the rainy apartment block, 300 windows, one warm lamp: the 1-in-300 number as a place.
2. **Shot 8 / 24** — the puppet under the lamp with four silver strings rising to the peg-head (24: the strings in tune, the joint back in place).
3. **Shot 22** — three brass plates in a dark corridor, the three 調 lighting gold and merging into one 调.
4. **Shot 27** — dawn at the window, the doubled reflection fading, the lamp still on (callback to the opening frame).

## Hit budget (edit-rhythm)
Tier 1: 0.334 / 4.404 / 8.473 / 12.54 (cold open, one gentle change each), 16.613 title, 20.683, 49.172, 65.451,
82.239, 108.184, 111.745. Tier 2: shot 3 focus, shot 21 plates (one gesture), shot 22 gold, shot 24 click.
