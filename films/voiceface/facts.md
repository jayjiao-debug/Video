# 《声音好听的人，是不是长得丑？》 facts

| # | Finding | On screen | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| V1 | Early small studies (30–42 voices/faces, mostly women, vowels or single words) found face–voice attractiveness r = 0.37–0.60 | 早年：好听的声音配好看的脸 | Zäske, Skuk & Schweinberger 2020, *R. Soc. Open Sci.* 7:201244, summarising Collins & Missing 2003 and others |
| V2 | Zäske et al. 2020: 64 speakers (32 f) + 21 speakers, 20 raters per task; vowels and natural German sentences. Only vowel r = 0.30 reached p<.05 (not after correction); sentences r = 0.09–0.24 n.s., experiment 5 r = 0.06 | 听整句话：几乎没关系 | same |
| V3 | Valentova, Varella & Havlíček 2017, *Behavioural Processes*: 86 women, 81 men (Brazil, Czech Republic). Positive face–voice attractiveness correlation in women, none in men | (note: women weakly yes, men no) | abstract via Charles Univ. repository |
| V4 | Kordsmeyer, Rasokat, Stern, Schild & Penke 2026, *Evolution and Human Behavior* 47(2):106818: 165 men, 155 women, 400 raters, each rater judged one modality. Face–body attractiveness: medium positive; voice: very few associations, "voices may communicate partly distinct information" | 320人、400个评分者：脸和身材对得上，声音自成一派 | HBES summary; PsyPost report (exact r not retrieved, so no number on screen) |
| V5 | Hughes & Miller 2016, *J. Soc. Pers. Relat.* 33(7): listeners pair attractive voices with attractive faces and, even more strongly, unattractive with unattractive ("what sounds beautiful looks beautiful" stereotype) | 听到好声音，你会脑补一张好看的脸 | journal abstract |
| V6 | Krumpholz et al. 2022, *Front. Psychol.*: raising women's pitch made voices sound more feminine and younger but did not change attractiveness ratings in natural speech | (backup) | journal |
| V7 | If face and voice are uncorrelated, a top-1 % voice has an average face (50th percentile); at r = 0.1, 59th percentile | 百里挑一的声音，配的多半是普通的脸 | arithmetic, bivariate-normal model |
| V8 | Selection: people with both a great face and voice can choose on-camera work, so voice-only professions may hold fewer of them | 还有一种可能 (stated as a possibility, not a finding) | reasoning, same logic as our Berkson episode |
