# The owner's own ideas (2026-10-25)

The new direction, in the owner's words: "I don't think the videos I'm currently doing are good enough. I'm just copying others. I want to do something unique, where people can feel the passion and the thinking, and memorable for someone."

What the ideas have in common: a question simple enough that most people understand it, one they have probably wondered about at some point but never looked up. The viewer *takes part first*: they listen, look or feel, guess, and are usually right. Then the film shows why they already knew. From now on, use voices and sound effects.

| # | Idea | Hook | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Swearing**: why can you tell which clip is swearing, in a language you don't speak? | "Listen to these 2 clips. Which one is swearing?" | Started as ep49 (`episodes/49-zanghua/`). Lev-Ari & McKay 2022, Psychonomic Bulletin & Review: swear words avoid approximants (l, r, w, y); 63 % picked the approximant-free pseudo-word. Test 1 is tone (easy); test 2 uses a calm voice (the sound of the word itself). Risk: Douyin moderation of profanity on screen. |
| 2 | **Spot the AI drawing** | "Here are 2 drawings. Which one is AI?" Start with a 2024 AI image vs a real drawing, then a recent one that is much harder or impossible (or both AI). | Can AI still tell? Then photos, then video ("give it a few years"). Be honest: detectors are unreliable; the clues you used (hands, text, smoothness) are exactly what the models learned to fix, so the tell moves from the picture to provenance (watermarks, C2PA). Dates fast: make it soon. |
| 3 | **Flow**: the deciding game of the 英雄联盟 Worlds BO5 | 「现在是英雄联盟世界赛BO5的最后一场」: the crowd is very loud, the heartbeat is very loud, you close your eyes and the world slowly goes quiet. Only you, the screen, the mouse and the keyboard are left. You enter the flow. | The passion piece. Sound-led and immersive (second person). Why does time disappear? (Csikszentmihalyi; challenge = skill.) Riot fan-content rules apply; draw our own version of the game. |
