# Quality bar: check every frame before the owner sees it

The owner's notes over ep41–ep48, turned into a checklist. The producer checks every still at full size (and a 25 % copy for phone size) before sending anything: key frames, demos, fixes, covers. A frame that fails any line goes back, it is not sent "for review". If it still isn't good, say so plainly instead of sending it.
Reference for "good": `ep44_final_sheet.jpg` (approved 2026-10-25).

## People and hands
- Hands simple and readable; no claws, no outlined fingertips, no drawn nails ("crab hands", "nails disturbing").
- Size the prop first, then fit the hand to it; a child's hand ≈ 0.7 × head length; a child ≈ 60 % of an adult.
- Behaviour is plausible: eyes on what they look at (the eclipse observer looks through the eyepiece), hands touch what they hold (the pen nib on the signature line), feet on the ground, the doctor beside the bed (not inside it), runners actually run.
- For hard poses/hands/props: make or find a reference first, then draw. Dignified and stylised beats fussy detail.

## Text and layout
- Captions readable on every background (contrast); key words not too hard to read on light or dark grounds.
- No text spilling outside its paper, sign or panel; headlines fit the newspaper.
- Corner items (counter, stamp, seal) ≥ 120 px from the sides and ≥ 58 px from top/bottom (iPhone crops the sides). Nothing important in the right column (Douyin buttons).
- The title holds long enough to read (≥ 2.5 s at full size), it never flashes by.
- Subtitle on screen at frame 0; the first frame is never dark or empty; readable in 2 s at phone size.

## Picture
- One background family per film; never mix AI painterly plates with flat vector/code-drawn sets. Not "too bland": layered set dressing.
- Glows stay inside their shape (masked), never trail or float behind a head.
- No two copies of the same prop during a cross-dissolve (one compass, not two).
- No unmotivated or prop-less floating elements (an eclipse disc in a night street, papers pinned to a lamppost).
- Era tags, stamps and the counter agree with the line on screen.

## Motion
- Camera ≤ ~25 px per frame; no whip pans (they strobe on phones and read as flicker).
- No flicker frames: check transitions frame by frame (0:15–0:17 and 0:22 flickers were owner-found).
- Nothing static: every shot drifts and every line is acted out.

## Process
- The same artist carries a film's visual work start to finish when possible; every brief links this file.
- Fewer, stronger options (3 good > 6 average).
- Run stripsig / glitchscan / a contact sheet on every render; look at the flagged frames.
