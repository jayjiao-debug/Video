# Douyin analytics: what to design for (from the owner's analysis chats, 5 Oct review and 25 Oct "path forward")

- **The first 2 s decide the push.** Films with a 2 s drop-off ≤ 29 % were pushed (24 h > 100k); 35–40 % were not (r = −0.63 over 8 films). Winners open on a concrete thing plus a question, with a subtitle from frame 0. Losers open on near-black, starry or abstract frames.
- **Our openings beat comparable videos** for the first ~10 s, then fall below Douyin's 同类 average at **20–40 s** and never recover.
- **The two biggest leaks:**
  - **The title card.** It holds several seconds right where loss is fastest (《第几个人》 lost 7–8 % per 2 s at 18–24 s, against 4–5 % for comparable videos). Keep it ≤ 2 s or lay it over the picture.
  - **The end card.** In the last 10 s we lose 12–27 % per 2 s, against 4–13 % for comparable videos. Say the payoff and the question before it, and keep the end card to 3–4 s.
- **Average watch time is 13–24 s whatever the length.** 164 s films completed at 3.5–3.7 %. Aim for ≤ 120 s where possible (owner's cap for ep45: 2:30).
- **"You and other people" topics** (第几个人, 邓巴数, 缘分方程) get a 2.1–3.2 % share rate, against 0.5–0.7 % for history and maths. They also draw more women viewers and get more likes. Shares are what break a film out (《第几个人》: 4.9万 shares, 211万 plays).
- **Audience:** mostly men aged 18–23 (with a notable share under 18), interests 游戏 / 随拍 / 二次元. 87–94 % of plays come from 推荐; people search "vibe知识大赏" directly.
- **Comments:** viewers notice "these AI videos are everywhere". The edge is research that is right and topics worth sending to a friend, not visuals alone.
- **Owner (25 Oct):** "the start can hold people and draw people in". Protect the hook and teaser; cut from the middle.
