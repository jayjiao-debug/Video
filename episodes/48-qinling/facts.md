## Corrections to the brief (read first)

1. **"70多倍" cannot be traced to a measurement.**
   - What the sources say: 吴永琪 said it (stdaily/CCTV4, 2026-09-21: 「目前已经通过物探手段测出皇帝陵确实有水银，封土的汞含量是大地本体的70多倍」). The article gives no institution, no date and no absolute value. 新京报 (2026-09-22) repeats it ("1981 and 2002 measurements… more than 70 times") with no origin either.
   - Every published number I could open gives a much smaller multiple. Chemistry World (Ball): background about 30 ppb, mound centre average 250 ppb, up to 1500 ppb. Sci. Rep. 2020: 205 ppb average, up to 1440 ppb. The ratios are about 7-8x for the average and about 50x for the single peak (估算, M2).
   - So **"70多倍" is a quote of an expert, not a reproducible measurement.** On screen it must be attributed to him (M1), or replaced by the safe line in M3.
2. **When did Wu say it?** The brief says a Sept 2026 CCTV interview. The stdaily page only says 「据此前央视采访报道」 with no date. The Star (Malaysia, 2026-10-03) calls it a "2024 CCTV interview". I could not find the original CCTV programme. Don't write "最近" or "今年" next to his quote.
3. **The 70万 number does not belong to the tomb alone.** 《史记》 has it twice (S3, S4). The first clause is about conscripts sent to the tomb. The second says 70万 convicts were split between 阿房宫 and the tomb. Say 「《史记》记载」, and don't add "修陵70万人 + 修了38年".
4. **Age at death.** 《史记》 gives no age. By East Asian count (虚岁, which is what 《史记》's 「年十三岁」 uses) he was 50. By full years he was 49. Use 虚岁 for every counter value, and 50 at the end.
5. **Construction length: 36, 37 or 38 years.** Use 「三十多年」 or 「约37年」. See C1.
6. **"项羽 dug up the tomb".** The line 「项羽烧秦宫室，掘始皇帝冢」 is in 《史记·高祖本纪》 as Liu Bang's list of charges against his rival, not the historian's own verdict. Archaeology finds fire damage and a dug-open passage in the terracotta pits (申茂盛's reading), and no sign of a breach in the burial chamber. See section E.
7. **"国家文物局 原则上不主动发掘" is a paraphrase.** The only official wording I could open is the State Council notice of 30 Mar 1997: 「对大型帝王陵寝暂不进行主动发掘」 (F1). "原则上" and "只做抢救性发掘" appear in the press, not in that sentence. Use the 1997 wording.
8. **"水银来源被找到了" (the 旬阳 cinnabar story, Sept 2026) is not established.** Experts quoted in The Star and 封面新闻 say it is only a lead. Keep it off screen.
9. **ctext.org was not reachable** (proxy blocks the host). The 《史记》 text comes from 原古诗文网 (m.gushiwen.cn); every quote below is copied from the page I opened. Before publishing, a human can check the same sentences on ctext.org.

## Story-worthy

**The 5 most surprising facts (all ✓):**
1. **15 seconds.** The colours on a terracotta warrior begin to change 15 seconds after the soil comes off, and are flaking within 4 minutes (F3: 秦始皇帝陵博物院文物保护部主任夏寅, 新华社/人民日报 2024). The fastest way to make "why not dig" physical.
2. **Two shafts missed the chamber by 200 m.** Two small shafts (under 1 m wide) were dug into the mound in the past, and both stop more than 200 m from the centre of the 地宫 (X7). No sign of a breach in the burial walls (X8).
3. **They found the army by digging a well in a drought** (1974), 1,500 m from the mound, villagers calling the broken figures 「瓦盆爷」 (T1, T2). Fifty years later, only about a fifth of Pit 1 is dug (T5).
4. **《史记》 ends the tomb paragraph by planting it to look like a hill** (S9), after sealing the craftsmen in (S8). The most famous tomb in China was made to disappear into a mountain.
5. **A 13-year-old started it, a 50-year-old ended it, and the Chinese state's rule is "not yet".** 《史记》: from the day he took the throne (S2); the 1997 State Council wording says 暂不 ("not for now") and ties it to technology (F1).

Runners-up (all ✓): the corpse carriage hidden under a stone of salted fish (S11); the expert who preserved the colours for decades says "don't dig" (F12); the archaeologist's own line, "只有挖开以后才知道" (X8); the empty Pit 4 (T6); mercury at 12,000 m² under a mound that looks like a hill (M3).

**Strongest human story:** the **well-diggers of 西杨村** in the 1974 drought: they struck brick and bronze, took the pieces for gods ("瓦盆爷"), and started the world's most famous dig. Today the man who built the colour-saving method (吴永琪) is the one saying "能不挖就不要挖". Second choice: the nameless craftsmen who built the traps and were sealed in (S8), told as one door closing.

**Best visual image:** a warrior painted red, green and blue, with a clock from 0 to 15 s and the colours draining to grey on the last second (F3). For the main set-piece: the stepped mound under changing skies, with the counter running 封存 2235 年 (P2, section I). Another strong one: two thin shafts drawn into the mound, ending far short of a glowing chamber (X7).

## Risks

- **Fact-check risk (Douyin 抖音小安).** The hook number 70多倍 has no origin. If used, it must be attributed on screen to the 名誉院长 and the sources cited (M1). The wording 「汞含量异常偏高，范围约1.2万平方米」 holds up against every source (M3).
- **The question is a hot search (2026-09-20).** Every video on it is competing; any claim that 国家 "will open it" or "已经决定" is ✗. The state position is F1.
- **Sensitive history and real people.**
  - 秦始皇 and 项羽 are historical figures, fine as silhouettes.
  - 吴永琪, 夏寅, 申茂盛, 段清波, 袁仲一 are real people. Quote only their published words, with title. Not as cartoon faces.
  - 赵康民 (d. 2018) and the 西杨村 villagers: don't name or draw recognisable portraits.
  - 殉葬 (S12) and 刑徒: no bodies, no skeletons, no gore (brief). Sealed craftsmen = a door closing and lamps going out. The 鲍鱼 line (S11) is text only.
- **Maps.** No bordered map. The "mercury matches China's rivers" idea (M6) invites a China map: leave it out. Use a plan sketch of the site or Lintong as a dot.
- **No real photos.** Museum and press photos of the figures are copyrighted. Use silhouettes (brief).
- **Superstition / 盗墓 flags.** Avoid "curse", "盗墓", "诅咒", "尸体不腐" and how-to-rob content; talk about 盗洞 as 'holes found by archaeologists'.
- **Mercury toxicity.** Fine as a topic. Don't say the mound is dangerous to visitors, and don't say it is safe: lidar air readings were at most 27 ng/m³ against a local background of 5-10 (R4), and I have no sourced health threshold.
- **Age convention.** 虚岁 vs full years. The counter uses 虚岁 and says 「《史记》记载」 for 13. A pedant could say "he was 12": keep the plain 《史记》 wording.
