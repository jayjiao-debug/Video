## Story-worthy: 5 most surprising facts (for 2:30)
1. **His mum's first words after 28 years: 「你去哪儿了？」** He hadn't seen her in ten years; she wept under a "Welcome Home Son" banner (rows 47, 48, 46, 51). (Rows 52–53 add that she saw him 3 times in 27 years and bought him a Christmas present every year, but those are single-source BBC: ?.)
2. **Rule: no wheels, so where the land ends, he swims.** He calls himself "definitely not a swimmer", yet swam nearly 300 km across the Caspian in about a month by "floatimming", then the Channel in 3 days (~20 h in the water) because Eurotunnel wouldn't let him walk the tunnel (rows 11, 32–34, 37–39).
3. **Pause, fly out, come back to the exact spot.** Visas gave him 90 days in 180; each time he left, he returned to where he had stopped. Only about half of the 28 years was spent walking (rows 10, 13, 23).
