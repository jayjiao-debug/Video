## Hook and spine
- **Frame-0 line:** 「两千多年前，有人列了7个世界奇迹。」 Then: 「今天，你还能去看几个？」
- **Frame-0 picture:** seven lit silhouettes on one horizon, one per wonder. No map.
- **Spine:** the viewer travels to each wonder in turn. Each one appears in its glory, then the count drops, each with one striking fact (all to source):
  - 罗德岛巨像: stood only about 54 years before an earthquake brought it down.
  - 阿尔忒弥斯神庙: burned by a man who wanted to be remembered, and the city banned his name.
  - 空中花园: no trace of it has been found in Babylon's ruins or texts; it may have been in Nineveh instead. **This is the second question: 「它可能根本不在巴比伦」.**
  - 亚历山大灯塔, 宙斯神像, 摩索拉斯陵墓: earthquakes, fire, stones carried off for other buildings (one line each).
  - **The reveal: one is left, the Great Pyramid, the oldest of the seven.** It was already ancient when the list was made.
- **Coda (conflict mark):** in 2007 nearly 100 million votes chose a "new seven", and 长城 was on it. End question: 「如果让你选，你会选哪7个？」
- **Counter:** 「还剩 N 个」 bottom-left, 7 → 1. It must match the picture at every cut.
