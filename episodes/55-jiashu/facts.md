## Corrections to the brief (read first)

1. **「大哥/长兄衷」 is the media's framing, not the letters'.** 新华社 and 中国日报网 call 衷 「兄长」/「长兄」. The scholar 李天虹 says only 「兄弟“中”」. The letters address 衷 together with the mother and ask him to teach 惊's daughter, which fits an older brother, but no source I opened shows the birth order. Safe wording: **「写给家里的衷和妈妈」** or 「家里的兄弟衷」 (row C3).
2. **「新婚妻子」 is not safe.** The text says 「新负」, read 「新妇」 = 惊's wife (李天虹, 陈伟). 「新妇」 in this period need not mean newly married. 惊 also has a daughter (妴) old enough to be warned about going far to fetch firewood, which suggests he had been married for some time (my inference). Safe wording: **「妻子」**, with no 「新婚」 (row A1-17).
3. **The rank (爵) question is the mainstream reading, but one scholarly translation disagrees.** Chinese museum and official sources read 「报必言相家爵来未来」 as asking whether the rank granted to the family has arrived. The University of Washington anthology translation (based on 《秦简牍合集》 2016) reads 「相家爵」 as a person's name, "Xiang Jiajue". Safe wording: **「信里还问：家里的爵位，到了没有？」**, attributed as 「一般认为」 or kept as a question. Don't build a whole scene on it (row A1-9).
4. **「急急急」 is real.** It is in 惊's letter right after 「即死矣」: 李天虹 (2025) quotes 「弗遗，即死矣。急急急」. Older transcriptions (大众日报 2020, press) have a **second** 「急急急」 at the very end, after the warning about 新地, but the two scholarly quotations of that sentence I opened end without it. On screen, use only the first one (rows A2-5, A2-11).
5. **衷 or 中?** Newer readings (李天虹 2025; the 湖北省博物馆 2024 exhibition text 「问中（衷）」) write the name **中**. The older transcriptions and most media write **衷**. It is the same person (both zhōng). Use **衷** on screen, the familiar form, and note 「（又释“中”）」 in the sources card if there is room (row A1-1).
6. **The date is 「秦王政二十四年（公元前223年）」 per 陈伟.** Barbieri-Low gives "campaigns of 224–223 BCE". Safe wording: **「约公元前223年」 / 「两千两百多年前」**. Note that the 湖北省博物馆 web page labels the object 「秦（221 BC—207 BC)」, a period heading. The letter itself predates unification, so don't print "秦朝" as the date. Use 「秦灭楚前夕/秦统一前夕」 (rows B1–B3).
7. **Excavated 1975, not 1975–76.** The canal-digging find was in mid-November 1975 and the 12 tombs were excavated in December 1975. Safe: **「1975年12月」 / 「1975年底」** (rows C5–C7). No source I opened gives the exact day 4号墓 was opened.
8. **Where the originals are:** the 湖北省博物馆 page says 「湖北省博物馆藏」. 新华社 (2024) says 云梦县博物馆 shows **复制品**. 人民日报 (2024-11) says the two 木牍 are 「云梦县博物馆内展出」. Safe: **「现藏湖北省博物馆」** (museum's own wording), or leave the location out (row C12).
9. **「最早的家书」 needs 「目前发现」 / 「实物」.** Older letters survive in transmitted texts. The claim in every official source is about **archaeologically found letters** (section D).

## A. The two letters: 释文 and 白话

Transcription conventions: （） = the editors' reading of a variant or loan character; 〔 〕 = a character the editors supply; …… = lacuna (damaged or unreadable). The base text is the standard (1981-type) transcription. I could not open the 1981 report itself, so the line-by-line base is the near-complete text printed by 大众日报 (2020, press, S11). Every line is cross-checked against the scholarly sources available (李天虹 2025 S2, Barbieri-Low 2019 S9, the UW translation of the 《秦简牍合集》 text S10) and official press (S3, S5). Newer readings from those scholars are given in the notes.

Numbering: 黑夫 and 惊's joint letter = **M4:11 (11号木牍)**; 惊's own letter = **M4:6 (6号木牍)**. ## Story-worthy

**Five most surprising facts**
1. The earliest family letter found in China ends a plea for money with **「即死矣。急急急」** (S2).
2. **Only two pre-Qin letters are known for sure, and both were in one man's tomb**, the brother they were written to (S2; C2).
3. **The soldiers paid for their own uniforms**: 黑夫 asks his mother to compare **安陆 cloth prices** and either sew the clothes or just send cash (S2, S3, S13).
4. The letters lay **beside an ink stone and ink**, in the middle of the floor of the head box. The man who received them could read, and perhaps wrote back (S2, S7).
5. **Nobody knows if 黑夫 and 惊 came home**. What we know is that the letters did (S4, S17).

**Strongest human story:** 惊's letter. He is broke: he has used money that belongs to someone called 垣柏, and he writes 「家里再不寄来，我就要死了。急急急」. In the same letter he asks after his wife and daughter, tells his wife to look after the old folks, asks his brother to make sure the girl doesn't wander far to gather firewood, and warns his brother to stay out of the new lands because of bandits. The brother at home kept both letters until he died and took them into his grave. 2,200 years later the letter home says what letters home still say: send money, it's getting hot, how is everyone, look after Mum, be careful.

**Best visual image:** December 1975, a small wooden-chambered tomb in 云梦 is opened. In the middle of the floor of the head box lie two thin wooden tablets, one 尺 long (about 23 cm), columns of ink on them, next to a stone ink-slab and a piece of ink (S2, S7). That is the hardest-drop shot: 「你，到家了」. A second image: the route 淮阳 → 安陆, drawn as a line across unbordered terrain (no borders, per the owner's rule), about 320 km (估算).

## Risks

- **Quoting the letters:** use only ✓ lines, and the safe wording for disputed ones. Avoid on screen: 相家爵 as a certain "rank" (A1-9), 子彦/子產 (A2-10), 益就 (A1-4), the 祠祀 sentence (A2-9), the second 急急急 (A2-11), 「借」 as certain (A2-5), 「新婚妻子」 (A1-17), 「大哥」 as fact (C3).
- **Deaths:** 「即死矣」 is a plea, not a report of death. Don't show the brothers dying. Their fate is unknown (E1). War: no battle gore. Show the 反城 as a distant walled silhouette.
- **Maps:** owner rule, no maps with borders. The 淮阳–安陆 line can sit on unbordered relief with two place labels (今 names in small type).
- **Real people:** 黑夫, 惊, 衷 are real, but nothing is known of their faces. Use readable generic silhouettes and no "portrait" framing. Living scholars and officials: don't depict.
- **Political:** the letter is tied to a 2024 visit by 习近平 to 云梦县博物馆 (S20: 「展陈中有迄今考古发现最早的家书」; S21: 「习近平总书记逐字逐句读了出来」). Don't reference it. Keep the film about the family.
- **Platform / title truthfulness:** 「中国最早的家书，竟然是在跟妈妈要钱？」 holds: 「黑夫和惊都向母亲要钱」 (S6), and 「最早」 is in D1 to D5. Keep 「目前发现」 in the body. Don't add 「第一封」 as an absolute.
- **Era props** (owner note, ep41): wooden 牍 with brush and ink. No paper, envelopes or wax seals. Clothes: 襌裙、襦. Coins: Qin coins are plausible, but no coin type is named in the letters, so don't label them.
- **Dating label:** don't write 「秦朝」 for 223 BC. Write 「秦统一前夕」 (correction 6).
