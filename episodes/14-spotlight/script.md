# Script: 你以为大家都在看你  (v1, first draft for gate 1)

**Logline:** 你进教室绊了一跤，觉得至少一半的人记住了。康奈尔的T恤实验给出了答案：猜一半，实际不到四分之一，换成得意的T恤也只有8%。另外两个实验发现：出了糗，别人给你的印象分比你以为的高；聊完天，对方也比你以为的更喜欢你。最后镜头拉远，屋里每个人都站在自己的聚光灯下。
**Takeaway:** 别人没你想的那么在意你，这是好消息。（#1 #7 #9 #13 #17）
**Length:** the body is **100.6 s** (31 lines plus the title gap). With a Juno end card of **≤ 4.0 s** the film is **≈ 104.6 s**, inside 95–105. If the card has to be longer, cut L9 (2.8 s, see "Cut list"). pace.py: 0.0 s waiting (0 %), no TOO FAST. Its only CHECK is the planned 2.8 s title gap at 12.4–15.2 s.
**Music anchors:** the title plaque lands on the hardest drop (12.4 s). The pull-back reveal (L29) starts on the 2nd drop at **90.5 s**.

## Revision notes
This is the first draft, so there are no gate notes yet. The producer's story decisions were applied as follows:
- **Cold open = the viewer's own moment** (owner rows 22, 35, 36). L1 is the viewer tripping in the classroom doorway, with the subtitle on frame 0. The emotional turn is L2 (脸烧了一整天，半夜还在回放). The guess comes in L3 (至少一半, the hook number by 6.6 s), and L4 asks the question before the drop. I chose *one* moment instead of a list (摔倒/发错消息/答错), because it is a single story and it happens at a **door into a room of seated people**, which is exactly the Study 1 layout (#4). The cold open and the hero 3D world are the same place.
- **"It's not you, it's X" + what they'll get** (row 35): L5 不怪你敏感，心理学叫它聚光灯效应 · L6 看完这条，也许能少很多内耗. "也许" keeps the promise from overclaiming (#13: "fewer regrets").
- **WHY before every study** (row 35): L7 (2000, #2), L19 (2001: 万一被看见了，会不会被看扁, #14), L25 (2018: 对方多喜欢你, #19).
- **Evidence beats, each NEW:**
  1. The embarrassing shirt (L8–14): you overestimate how many people notice.
  2. The twist (L15–18): your good moments are noticed even less, at 8 %.
  3. Even when they do notice (L19–23), the harm is smaller than you fear, and the line says why (#17).
  4. The liking gap (L24–28): after a chat you underestimate how much you are liked. It lasts for months and starts at age 5.
- **Bookend:** 回放 in L2 comes back as 回放最多的人是你自己 in L30, and the door in L1 is the door in L30.
- **Caveats:** the word 实验 is on screen in L7, L20 and L25. Nothing says 科学证明, 所有人 or 没人在看你. The singer is "一位歌星" and has no name or likeness on screen. The "proud" shirts are 有面子的T恤, with no names (Risks in facts.md).

## Title options
1. **你以为大家都在看你** (9 字), **recommended for the cover, the feed and the in-film plaque.** A 20-year-old reads it and thinks "这说的就是我" (row 45). It names the feeling, not the paper, and the word 你以为 already promises the twist without stating it. It cannot overclaim, because it asserts nothing about other people, and the film shows exactly that gap (46→23, 48→8). As the plaque on the 12.4 s drop it answers L4's "到底有多少人记得？" with a jab.
2. **你的糗，别人记得吗？** (8 字). It is a real question, and the film answers it with numbers (L13–14). It is also the most concrete (糗). But it covers only beats 1–3; the liking gap (L24–28) is not about 糗, so the title promises a narrower film than the one we made.
3. **别人没那么在意你** (8 字). This is the takeaway, stated honestly; it is L31 shortened and its plain reading is what the film shows. But in the feed it reads like a self-help quote or a put-down, and it gives away the ending, so there is less reason to watch.

**Recommended pairing:** cover = plaque = **你以为大家都在看你**.

## Lines
**Camera plan: one 3D hero world runs through the film.**
- **The world:** a small seminar room at night, lit low-key. There is one tall window with blue night outside and a warm practical lamp, and a theatre spotlight hangs on a ceiling bar. A door is on one wall. 4–6 stylised figures sit at a table facing the door (rounded, matte, no faces, no hands), with questionnaire sheets on the table. The student is the same kind of figure.
- **Data on the model:** a physical gauge floats above the student's head, with a warm needle **你以为** and a cool needle **实际**. It is the one recurring infographic (46 → 23, 48 → 8). At L19 it turns into a 0–100 rating bar.
- **2.5D beats inside the same world:** the ceiling replay in L2, the shirt in the corridor in L8–9, the score cards over the table in L20–23, and the chat bubbles at the window in L24–28. 2D overlays sit only inside the room's space, never on a separate slide.
- **Transitions (all motivated):**
  - the spotlight circle → the round light on a dorm ceiling (L1→2)
  - dive into the replay → back into the room (L2→3)
  - the beam → its fixture (L4→5)
  - follow the dust down the beam to the door (L6→7)
  - through the door gap into the corridor (L7→8)
  - the door opens (L10), and we follow the student out (L11)
  - back through the door to check the answers (L14)
  - the gauge dial flattens into a rating bar (L19)
  - the table → the window (L24)
  - the window → a dorm door calendar (L27)
  - out through the window into the big pull-back (L29)
  - back to the doorway (L30)
- **The first second:** the door bangs open on the first beat, the figure stumbles, and the spotlight slams on. The subtitle is already on frame 0.

**Notes for the art director, editor and animator:**
- People are rounded stylised figures with **no faces and no hands** (owner: "干脆别画手了"). Every action that would need a hand is a cut on motion: the shirt in L10, the door, the pencils standing in cups.
- The singer is never named, drawn or suggested by a likeness. The "proud" shirt is an abstract gold star block. Don't show Bob Marley, Seinfeld or MLK.
- The 小秘密 in L20 stays padlocked and blurred. Never write or hint at the actual detail (#18).
- No numbers on the liking-gap bars (L25–28). The calendar in L27 shows no end month (#20: the gap was there at every time point except the last, May).
- The gauge (L3–17) is the key infographic. Its number must be big enough to read on a phone (row 44: data on the model). The ×2 / ×6 brackets are the only secondary labels.
- 他 in L10–14 means the one student figure. The study had men and women (#7), and the figure stays gender-neutral.
- 3D vs 2.5D per shot goes in medium_plan.md (row 42). The script only says where the hero world is used: L1, L3–5, L7, L10–23, L29–31. L2, L8–9 and L24–28 can be 2.5D.
