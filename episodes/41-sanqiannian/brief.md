- **The walking person and the eras are the hero.** Imagery only supports.

**Title.** Chosen by the owner after three rounds ("not catchy", "不够浪漫?"):
- Title: 《为了跟你说一句话，人类努力了三千年》
- Core: 人类发明的每一种传话方式，都是为了离想见的人近一点。
- Viewer's question for the comments: 「你最想对谁说一句话？」

**Format**

- Label top-left: red dash, year, name and place.
- Native-language line, with the Chinese line under it (ElevenLabs voice).
- Hand-off object: the "sentence" itself — carved bone → letter → wire → wave → light → star.

## Candidate scenes (researcher: verify every one with primary sources; find better ones)
1. 商 甲骨卜辞 (~1200 BC): the four-directions rain divination 「其自东来雨…」. Our earliest writing is a question.
2. 杜甫《春望》757: 「烽火连三月，家书抵万金」. A letter home in wartime.
3. Samuel Morse.
   - 1825: his wife Lucretia died while he was away; the letter arrived too late, after the burial.
   - 1844: first telegraph message, "What hath God wrought". Who chose the words: Annie Ellsworth?
4. Édouard-Léon Scott de Martinville, 1860 phonautogram 「Au clair de la lune」.
   - Never playable until First Sounds, 2008.
   - First thought to be a girl, later re-sped and heard as a man, probably Scott himself.
   - Is the restored audio usable, and under what licence?
5. Alexander Graham Bell, 10 March 1876: "Mr. Watson — come here — I want to see you." Exact wording in his notebook.
6. Marconi, 12 Dec 1901, Signal Hill: the letter "S". Did he ask Kemp "Can you hear anything?"
7. 1987-09-14 China's first email: "Across the Great Wall we can reach every corner in the world."
   - Who sent it, from where, and the exact date sent vs. arrived.
8. 1992-12-03 first SMS, "Merry Christmas" (Neil Papworth → Richard Jarvis).
9. Finale: Voyager Golden Record, 1977.
   - Ann Druyan's EEG while thinking of falling in love (Carl Sagan).
   - The Mandarin greeting 「各位都好吧？我们都很想念你们，有空请到这儿来玩。」 (exact text).
   - Voyager 1's current distance.
- Also consider:
  - 1969 ARPANET "LO".
  - 1969 Armstrong.
  - Earliest known love letter or postal letter.
  - 鸿雁传书 (苏武): legend vs. history.
  - The first telephone call in China.
  - Any romantic story behind one of these firsts.
