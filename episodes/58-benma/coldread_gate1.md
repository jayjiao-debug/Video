# Cold read, gate 1 (subtitles only, v1, 179.5 s)

Reader: 20-year-old on Douyin in the evening. I know the bronze horse from the tourism sign and nothing else. I judged from the words only and could not see the pictures.

## 1. Would I keep watching at 2 s and 5 s?

- 2 s (line 1, "旅游标志上这匹马，三只脚悬空，只靠一只撑着"): Yes, but only just. I know this horse and had never noticed the three raised feet, so I get a small "huh, true" reaction. It is a statement, not a question, and there is no conflict yet. If the picture on screen is weak or slow, I swipe here.
- 5 s (line 2, "7公斤多的铜马，全压在一只鸟的背上"): Yes. "一只鸟" nudges me because I only know the name 马踏飞燕, so I want to know what bird it is. "7公斤多" lands flat, since 7 kg doesn't sound heavy. The part that works is "全压在一只鸟的背上".
- The real hook is the title card at 9 s ("脚下踩的到底是什么鸟？"). That comes after my usual 3-second decision. For the first 9 s I am held by the image and a mild "huh", not by a promise.

## 2. Where interest drops and where it spikes

Spikes:
- 22.4 s, line 6: "生产队的两匹耕马刚死，地下却挖出了39匹铜马". Two dead real horses against 39 bronze ones is a sharp image.
- 26.9 s, line 7: villagers want to sell the relics to buy real horses. Funny and human, and I would stay for it.
- 35.8 s, line 9: "一只脚，怎么站得住？" This is the first real question. "三招" at 38 s is a good countdown device.
- 49.1 s, line 13: "X光一照，马腿里有铁骨头". This is the best fact in the film. "铁骨铜腿" is memorable.
- 58 s, line 15: "顺拐". I know this word as the awkward walk, and it is funny to see it applied to a horse.
- 93.7 s, line 23: the green plush horse, 20,000 sold in a week. This is the only moment that connects to my own world. I know that plush.
- 98.2 s, line 24: "可它脚下，真的是燕子吗？" This is the biggest spike, a proper re-hook.
- 102.6 s, line 25: swallow's tail is a scissor, this bird's tail is flat. I can check it with my own eyes, which makes it satisfying.
- 111.5 s, line 27: the small dent in the tail may be where a bell was tied. This feels like detective work.
- 149.4 s, lines 36-37: the horse's real name 铜奔马 has no bird in it, and the man who named it is the "猎鹰" guy. This is the payoff beat for me.

Drops:
- 26.9–35.8 s (lines 7-8): "党寿山" is a name I have to hold onto with no payoff, and "国家的文物，不能卖" sounds like a slogan. Mild dip.
- 62–71 s (lines 16-17): "对侧步", "汉代河西良马", "甘肃至今还有这样的走马". Right after the funny "顺拐" it turns into a lecture. Mild to medium dip.
- 71.4–93.7 s (lines 18-22) is the main swipe-away zone, about 22 s. It is a list: 郭沫若 1971, Paris 1973, London, 十几个国家, 1983 logo, 2002 ban. These are many dates and places with no tension. Worse, by line 20 I have forgotten the title question about the bird (asked at 9 s, answered from 98 s). If I was ever going to leave, it would be around 75–85 s.
- 124.9–133.8 s (lines 29-31): 龙雀, 张衡《东京赋》, 牛龙菲, "马超龙雀". Four proper nouns in 9 s, and I can't keep them apart.

## 3. Unclear, confusing, or words I don't know

- Line 19 (75.9 s): "'马踏飞燕'这个名字，从此传开。" It is not clear who named it. Did 郭沫若 say 天马行空 and that somehow became 马踏飞燕? I lose the cause and effect.
- Line 27: "猎鹰系铃". I don't know that falconers tie bells on hawks, so the little dent means nothing to me until I guess.
- Line 26: "鹰隼类猛禽". I don't know how to read 隼, so I skim it. "猛禽" is fine.
- Line 29: "龙雀 / 风神". What is a 龙雀? Is it a real creature or a myth? Nothing in the film makes it concrete for me.
- Line 30: "'龙雀'和'天马'，写在同一句". So what? The line does not say why that supports the 风神 idea.
- Lines 16-17: "对侧步 / 走马 / 河西". I can get the idea from "骑着稳，颠得少", but 河西 is a place name I have to guess at.
- Line 5: "仪仗队". Vaguely fine. Line 4: "生产队" I only half know from history class.
- Line 40: "鸟刚回头，马已经冲过去". This probably describes the picture, but with words only I can't follow it.
- Line 38: "说不准的，就先不写进名字" sounds like the real reason, but I can't tell whether it is documented or just the film's tidy spin. I would half believe it.

## 4. 「答案是—— / 至今，没有定论」: cheated, or paid off?

Honest split. My first reaction at 145 s is an eye-roll: "哈？ 就这？" A 2.2 s dramatic pause followed by "no conclusion" is a classic bait-and-nothing move, and the title card promised an answer at 9 s.

I do not feel fully cheated, for three reasons:
1. The film lines up four real candidates (燕子, 猎鹰, 乌鸦, 风神) and gives a reason for each. By then I am invested in the debate, not the verdict.
2. "连甘肃省博物馆，也这么说" gives the no-answer an authority, so it reads as honest and not lazy.
3. The twist at 149 s works. The proper name is 铜奔马 and it has no bird in it, and the person who named it is the 猎鹰 guy. I did not know the horse had another name, and that is the fact I'd keep.

Without lines 36-38, I would feel cheated. With them, it is a 7 out of 10 payoff.

Would I comment? Yes, because the last line asks me to pick one. I would write:
- "我选猎鹰。燕子尾巴是剪刀，这只是平的，初世宾说得对。"
- or: "所以我们喊了这么多年的名字其实是个没定论的猜测？？小学课本骗我"
- or: "那个绿色玩偶我也想要"

## 5. Takeaway and who I'd forward it to

One sentence for a friend: "你知道马踏飞燕脚下那只鸟其实没人确定是燕子吗？它真名叫铜奔马，名字里没有鸟，而且给它起这个名字的专家自己觉得那是只猎鹰。"

Forward to: my roommate who bought the green plush horse; my friend who does history or museum stuff, to start an argument about which bird it is; and my dad, who loves "原来你一直叫错了" facts.

## 6. Slow, preachy, or history-class lines

- Line 8 (31.3 s): "国家的文物，不能卖。" Slogan voice, plus a named person I never see again.
- Line 16 (62.5 s): "这叫'对侧步'，是汉代河西良马练出来的跑法。" Textbook phrasing.
- Lines 18-22 (71.4–93.7 s): the dated timeline, the heaviest history-class stretch.
- Lines 28-31 (116–133.8 s): the scholar name-dropping (初世宾, 牛龙菲, 张衡). Names come faster than ideas.
- Line 38 (158.4 s): "说不准的，就先不写进名字。" It reads a little like a life lesson, but it is short and I like it.
- Line 39 (162.8 s): "不管它是什么鸟——没有它，这匹马就站不住。" Slightly sentimental, but I accept it as an ending.
- Line 40 (167.3 s): "是它，让这匹马飞了起来。" Poetic. It also pulls against the earlier "站稳" idea, because "飞" seems to contradict it. Fine as a closing line, but I would not remember it.

## Structural point in one sentence

The film asks "what bird?" at 9 s and does not touch it again until 98 s. The engineering section (35.8–62 s) is strong enough to hold me, but the 71–94 s timeline is where the promise gets forgotten.
