# Script: 薄肌圣典（注释版） — draft v1 (gate 1)

**Logline:** A crypto founder (孙宇晨) and an English-speaking-content creator (邵艾伦) spent 4 h 22 min talking about 「薄肌」. The internet laughed. We take the meme dead seriously: we annotate the 「薄肌圣典」 line by line with real papers, like a professor marking up scripture. Ironic look, real science.
**Takeaway:** 薄肌的科学版只有一句：肌肉是能存的。30 岁以后它每十年流失 3–8%，所以趁年轻，往身体里存一点。它是保险，不是彩票。
**Length target:** ~2:10 · subtitle-only (no voice-over), except the opening montage, which uses real voices · ~38 lines.
**Tone:** deadpan academic. The more absurd the quote, the more serious the footnote. The irony comes from the format, never from mocking anyone's body.

## Title options
1. **薄肌圣典** (4 字): recommended. It's the meme's own name, so it's instantly searchable and clickable. The film delivers on it literally as an annotated edition of a "holy book".
2. **薄肌，是玄学吗？** (7 字): a clear question with a clear answer. Less funny.

## Look: "注释版圣典"
- A dark, warm parchment page lit like a museum case. The 「圣典」 verses (verified quotes only) are set in a big gold serif with verse numbers (第一章 · 第一节).
- Our annotations arrive in the margin in a red "professor's pen": underlines, circles, arrows and footnote numbers ¹ ² ³. Each footnote expands into a clean data graphic: a grid, a curve or a bar.
- One recurring diagram is the 「薄肌坐标系」: x = 体脂率, y = 肌肉量 (FFMI). Every claim moves a dot on it.
- Music: a mock-solemn organ/choir sting for each verse, under a modern trap beat. The beat drops when a footnote proves the verse right.
