## Notes
- ⚠ 「薄肌之父」: use what the clip actually says (coverage says 「薄肌教父」).
- Lines 33–35 (Nedeltcheva 2010): n = 10 overweight adults, 14 days. "瘦肉" here means fat-free mass (muscle plus water, organs and so on), which is why line 35 says 「大半不是脂肪」 rather than 「大半是肌肉」. Small study: fine as a striking finding, not as a law. The source line credits it.
- Line 28 (Vispute 2012): 24 sedentary adults; 7 ab exercises, 2×10, 5 days a week, 6 weeks; no change in abdominal fat.
- Line 9 uses 孙's own words; the film never comments on anyone's body. 币 / 挖矿 / 上链 are our metaphors. Line 39 is attributed to 「网传」.
- Rights: the clips are short (≤ 1 s each, ~8 s total), with the source credited in the end-card line. The owner's call.
- Visible-abs body-fat numbers and "how many months" are gym rules of thumb with no solid study, so they are left out on purpose.
