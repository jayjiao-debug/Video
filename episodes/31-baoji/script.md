## Notes
- ⚠ 「薄肌之父」: use what the clip actually says (coverage says 「薄肌教父」).
- Line 14 ("拆旧、盖新", muscle protein turnover) is textbook physiology; there are no numbers on screen.
- Line 16 (R14): the +109% at 24 h comes from Chesley 1992, cited by MacDougall 1995, which measured the 36 h return to near baseline. The screen says 「升高一天多」 and doesn't show the percentage.
- Line 19 (R1): Janssen 2000 says *relative* muscle mass declines from the 3rd decade, but an absolute drop is only noticeable by the end of the 5th. The "3–8% per decade after 30" figure (Volpi 2004) is the common estimate; keep it as 「约」 in the source line.
- Lines 34–36: Nedeltcheva 2010 has n = 10. "瘦肉" means fat-free mass, so the film doesn't claim 「全是肌肉」.
- Line 39 is attributed to 「网传」, not to either person.
- Rights: the clips are short (≤ 1 s each, ~9 s total), with the source credited in the end-card line. The owner's call.
