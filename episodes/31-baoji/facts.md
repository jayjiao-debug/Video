## Science check
| # | Claim | Verdict | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| R1 | Muscle is a store you lose with age | ✓ | After about 30, muscle mass falls about 3–8% per decade, faster after 60. Volpi, Nazemi & Fujita 2004, *Curr Opin Clin Nutr Metab Care* (a widely cited estimate). |
| R2 | Muscle and strength protect health | ✓ strong | PURE study, Leong et al., *Lancet* 2015: 139,691 adults in 17 countries. Each 5 kg lower grip strength meant 16% higher all-cause mortality, a stronger predictor than systolic blood pressure [9]. |
| R3 | "Eat enough protein" | ✓, with a number | Morton et al., *BJSM* 2018 (49 RCTs, 1,863 people): gains from resistance training plateau at about **1.62 g/kg/day** of total protein [10]. |
| R4 | 薄肌 is the natural, achievable level; 厚肌 has a ceiling | ✓ | Kouri et al. 1995 (157 men): no drug-free lifter exceeded FFMI 25 [11]. |
| R5 | 薄肌 → confidence / control | ~ partly | Exercise does improve mood and self-efficacy (a large literature; check a specific meta-analysis before putting this on screen). But "control" is a feeling, not a measured outcome of 薄肌 in particular. |
| R6 | 薄肌 → success, wealth, women | ✗ no evidence | No study supports the causal chain. Critics' point: it reverses cause and effect, since successful people can afford time and trainers [6]. Any "positive habit" can be dressed up the same way (the critic's own parody: 「薄书理论」). |
| R8 | Volume: every muscle about 10+ hard sets a week | ✓ | Schoenfeld, Ogborn & Krieger 2017, *J Sports Sci* (meta-regression): growth rises roughly linearly with weekly sets; 10+ beat 5–9, which beat <5 [12] |
| R9 | Light weights work if taken near failure | ✓ | Schoenfeld et al. 2017 meta-analysis (summarised in Grgic & Schoenfeld 2018): low load (≤60% 1RM) to failure gives similar whole-muscle growth to high load; not to failure, high load is better [13] |
| R10 | Crunches don't burn belly fat | ✓ | Vispute et al. 2012, *JSCR*: 24 sedentary adults, 7 ab exercises 2×10, 5 days/week for 6 weeks: no change in abdominal fat (only endurance improved) [14] |
| R11 | Sleep decides what you lose | ✓ (small study) | Nedeltcheva et al. 2010, *Ann Intern Med*: 10 overweight adults, 2 × 14 days of the same diet, 8.5 h vs 5.5 h in bed. Weight loss was the same (~3 kg), but fat lost was 1.4 vs 0.6 kg (−55%) and fat-free mass lost 1.5 vs 2.4 kg (+60%) [15] |
| R12 | Beginners (and even trained people) can lose fat and gain muscle at once | ✓ | Body-recomposition review, *Strength Cond J* 2020; 42(5): 7–21 [16] |
| R13 | Muscle is about 38% of body mass in men, 31% in women | ✓ | Janssen et al. 2000, *J Appl Physiol* (MRI, 468 adults aged 18–88): 38.4% men / 30.6% women; relative mass declines from the 3rd decade, absolute loss noticeable by the end of the 5th [17] |
| R14 | One workout raises muscle protein synthesis for about 1–1.5 days | ✓ | MacDougall et al. 1995 (6 men, arm curls): +50% at 4 h and +109% at 24 h (cited from earlier work), back within 14% of control by 36 h [18] |
| R15 | Early soreness and damage ≠ growth | ✓ | Damas et al. 2016, *J Physiol* (10 men, 10 weeks): early synthesis goes to repairing damage; fibre size grew only by week 10; synthesis correlated with growth (r ≈ 0.9) only once damage had faded (weeks 3 and 10) [19] |
| R16 | "70岁再练来不及" is wrong: even 90-year-olds gain | ✓ | Fiatarone et al. 1990, *JAMA*: 10 frail nursing-home residents aged 90 ± 1 (9 finished), 8 weeks of high-intensity resistance training: strength +174% ± 31%, mid-thigh muscle area +9.0%, tandem gait speed +48% [20] |
| R17 | Women rate "toned", not the most muscular, as most attractive | ✓ | Frederick & Haselton 2007, *Pers Soc Psychol Bull* 33(8): 141 women, six male silhouettes; "toned" (two steps below "brawny") rated most sexually attractive; the most muscular seen as more volatile and less likely to commit [21] |
| R7 | 孙's "start young" | ✓ | Follows from R1: muscle built before the decline is the "deposit". |

## Script corrections needed (script.md v1)
1. Line 1 and the framing: the 「圣典」 is **邵艾伦's** theory, which 孙宇晨 adopted on air (F3). The film shouldn't call it 孙's holy book. Better: 「一个口语博主写了本'圣经'，一个币圈创始人读完开始练了」.
2. Line 18: the 「碳基服务器」 quote has to be checked against the audio (S1 ⚠). If that can't be done, use S2 「肌肉是战略储备资产 / 年轻时存的肌肉，是老了能花的现金」, which is better sourced and fits the "保险" ending even better.
3. Line 36: 「财富、名望、江山美人」 is the media's summary (T5). Attribute it to 「网上的薄肌圣经」 and don't put it in either man's mouth.
4. New beat worth using: 孙 logs his training with Claude Code (S6). This is a funny, verifiable footnote: 「碳基服务器，用硅基来管理」.
