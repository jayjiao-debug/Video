# Brief: 走进房间就忘了 (ep33, working title)

- **Episode:** ep33 · **Series:** VIBE知识大赏 (Juno)
- **Idea in one sentence:** Walking through a doorway makes you forget more than walking the same distance inside one room, because the brain files experience into "episodes" (event boundaries) and a doorway tells it one episode has ended, so what you came for gets filed away with the old room.
- **Why a 18–25 viewer cares:** everyone has walked into the kitchen / bedroom and forgotten why. It happens daily; it feels like a personal flaw, and the film says it's how the brain is built.
- **Takeaway (one line the viewer should remember):** 不是你记性差，是门把你的记忆"翻了页"。(screenwriter to sharpen; it must hold up against the research, including the limits.)
- **Length target:** ~100 s (owner prefers ~1:40 or less, no filler after the answer) · **Format:** 1920×1080 landscape, subtitle-only, music, ~2 s Juno end card.
- **Visual direction:** Juno series engine; the look is new. Set-piece idea from the pitch: **one continuous camera (一镜到底) following "you" through a flat**, where each doorway works like a page turning and the thing you came for falls behind. The owner likes long moving shots that flow, few hard cuts, reveals and transitions on the beat, music doing the emotion.
- **Comment hook / 发给谁:** end-card question 你今天在门口忘过什么？ (or a sharper variant that invites people to share their own story).
- **Must include:**
  - Open on the viewer's own situation in the first second (walking into a room, blank), subtitle on frame 0.
  - The real experiments (Radvansky and colleagues: virtual-room and real-room studies, ~2006–2011) told as what was done, with the actual numbers, verified from the papers.
  - Why: event boundaries / event-model updating, in everyday words, explained on the set-piece itself (no detour analogies).
  - A practical payoff the viewer can use (e.g. going back to the room helps; saying the task out loud / holding it in mind), only if the research supports it.
- **Must avoid:** realistic human faces (use a figure/silhouette or first-person camera); overclaiming (the effect is about memory for what was just handled/seen, measured in lab tasks; note any failed replications or limits); jargon on screen (事件边界 only if the next line says what it means in daily words); textbook framing; the last 3 episodes' looks (爆机 ep31, 刷脸 ep32's dots/depth maps).
- **Owner approvals required:** gate 1 (title + script), gate 2a (look), gate 4 (cut).
