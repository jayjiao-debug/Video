## Checks asked for, and their results
- **Music:** the song is sample-identical. mix_v3 − s130f = 0 exactly (24-bit) on every sample outside the SFX windows. The SFX are added on top with gain 1.0, except the frost, which is pulled to 0.78 where the sum would clip (by mix17.py's clip guard). Master vs mix: 0-sample lag, −0.03 dB, residual −30 dB (AAC). The master is 7.9 ms shorter (AAC framing).
- **SFX:** 11 events, three types. Ignites sit 12.4–18 dB under the music, whooshes ~21 dB, frost 13.5 dB (peak −3.8 dBFS). None collides with the drop: the 73.3 whoosh ends 8 s before it.
- **Beat sync:**
  - Title plaque on f498 = 16.60 (b8 16.58).
  - Drop on f2442 = 81.40 (first frame after 81.375). The luma rise is +13, from the line flare only; no whole-frame flash.
  - Transition cuts at 20.633 / 49.00 / 73.30 / 97.60 / 105.70 are on bars 10 / 24 / 36 / 48 / 52, within ≤ 1 frame.
  - 95 % starts on b14 (28.73) and is fully in by 29.1. 60 % counts from b18 and lands about 37.4 (b18 beat 2). 20 % counts from b20 and lands about 41.5. 48 % counts from b30 and lands about 61.6 (beat 2). 85 %: see #5.

- **Contour crawl / flicker:** none found. Consecutive-frame crops at 41, 62, 75, 86.5 and 99 show stable isotherms (evidence/crawl.png), and static holds measure diff ≈ 0.1. The only strobing is during the fast moves in #4 and #7.

  - facts.py: no problems. No banned sources.
