https://news.emory.edu/emory-report-archive/erarchive/2003/September/September29/9_29_03monkeys.html (Emory Report, 29 Sep 2003)
- female brown capuchin monkeys tested in pairs; "First they gave study subjects lower-value rewards of cucumbers if the subjects would exchange tokens."
- grapes = higher-value reward; subjects "refused to accept a lower-value reward if their partners received a higher-value reward."
- equity test: "a 95 percent completed exchange rate"; inequity test (partner got grapes for same work): "fell to 60 percent"; effort control (partner got grapes for less/no work): 20 percent; food control: 55 percent.
