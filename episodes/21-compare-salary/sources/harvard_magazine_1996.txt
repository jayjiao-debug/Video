https://harvardmagazine.com/sites/default/files/html/1996/mj96/right.up.html (Harvard Magazine, May–June 1996) on Hemenway & Solnick's "Two World Study" (published as Solnick & Hemenway 1998, JEBO 37(3):373–383)
- "distributed last year to 257 staff and faculty members and students at the School of Public Health"
- "If prices are identical in both World A (in which your yearly income is $50,000, while others earn $25,000) and World B (in which your yearly income is $100,000, while others earn $200,000) ... in which world would you prefer to live?"
- "48 percent of respondents rejected the prospect of everyone, including themselves, becoming richer"
- "Eighty-five percent of the respondents chose to maximize their weeks of vacation, even if others had more."
