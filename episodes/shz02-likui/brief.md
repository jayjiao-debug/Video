# 水浒人物志 · 贰 · 李逵接母 (shz02)

- **Owner pick (2026-11-11 21:48):** 《武松打一只虎成了英雄，李逵杀了四只，为什么没人记得？》. Resonance: you finally do well enough to bring your mother to live with you, and you are one step too late.
- **Owner's instructions:** same BGM as 林冲 (《笛子戏》, 148.5 s, same anchors); evaluate 林冲 and improve the visuals, the animation and the finish (完成度).
- **Format:** as ep1 — narrator only, third person, the same native zh narrator, subtitles, end card without a question, ≈2:28.
- **Source:** 《水浒传》一百二十回本, 第四十二回 (end) and 第四十三回; 武松 from 第二十三回. Working copy: the public transcription in github.com/hunterhug/china-literary. No Wikipedia / 百度百科 / 知乎.

## What ep1 (林冲 v5) got wrong, and the rule for ep2  (review 2026-11-11, contact sheet every 2 s)
1. **Nine different looks in one film** (wood-table letter, ink title, flat drill ground, red shadow wall, blue cartoon close-ups, sunset silhouettes, flat snow village, fire silhouettes, misty lake). → **One look for the whole film**, designed before anything is animated: ink-wash landscape in depth + solid ink silhouettes with a thin rim light + one accent colour.
2. **The letter is on screen ~35 s of 148** and several shots hold one pose for 4–5 s. → No shot longer than ~4 s without a new action; no location used more than twice.
3. **Figures are cut-outs that slide, scale or fade**; only the stone push and one thrust really move. → Every figure is a jointed rig driven by motion capture (walk, carry, crouch, strike); nothing slides.
4. **Flat fills, little air.** → Every frame has depth layers (far/mid/near), atmosphere (mist, dust, leaves, moonlight), a light source you can point at.
5. **Faces and hands kept failing.** → No faces, no hands at all: silhouettes in profile; emotion from posture, distance and light.
6. **Colour jumps at random.** → A colour script that follows the story: lantern gold (the feast) → red autumn dusk (the road) → amber lamp (home) → moon blue (沂岭) → blue + ember red (the tigers) → grey dawn (the grave) → pale morning (the village).
7. **Finish:** plain end card, sound thin. → Title, stamps, end card and sound designed as one set; ambient sound in every scene.

## Safety
No gore (the mother's death is never shown: the empty stone, the spilled water; the cubs are not shown being killed; no blood beyond a dark trail in the grass), no minors, no faces of side characters, the cannibalism in the 李鬼 episode is left out, 李鬼's death is left out.
