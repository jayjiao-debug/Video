**Logline:** 假如说，你是一棵柏树。相传汉武帝种下你，你在泰山脚下、岱庙的院子里一步没动。皇帝上山顶祭天，又走了。赤眉的斧头、乾隆的画笔、民国的炊烟都到过你身边。你半边身子被火烧死，靠一条32厘米的树皮活到今天。2024年，你的孩子被送去汉武帝的茂陵，替你守那个"相传种下你的人"。

**Takeaway:** 来的人都走了，留下的是一棵谁也说不清来历的树。最早的书只说"盖"（大概）是汉武帝种的，可两千年后，是它的孩子去守汉武帝。

## Device
- **「你」 = 汉柏连理** (rows 4, 49): one root, two trunks. The west trunk is charred black and the east trunk is green. It is the only glowing colour. **The camera never leaves it.** Only what is behind it changes.
- **Counter (bottom-left): 「相传 · 你 X 岁」.** It lands at A1 with the 相传 tag and the small print 「按汉武帝首次封禅（前110年）推算 · 估算」 (row 13), and the small print returns at G3. 110 BC = 0, so an AD year + 109 (56 → 165, 1008 → 1117, 1928 → 2037, 2026 → 2135). During the 赤眉 legend it stays on screen, dimmed (no date, row 10).
- **Seal counter (D1–D6):** six seals for the 封禅泰山的皇帝 (row 22).
  - Seal 1 (秦始皇 · 前219) greys out (before you, 估算).
  - Seal 2 (汉武帝 · 前110) is dashed (legend).
  - Seals 3–6 light on the beat with name and year (rows 18–21).
  - A 7th outline appears and stays empty.
- **Legend style:** every 相传 / 传说 is drawn as a translucent dashed outline, and facts are solid.
- **Repeated set-piece: the torch line.** It climbs the mountain behind the tree to the summit altar and goes out. The rite is at the top (row 51) and you stay at the foot. No procession passes you (row 24).
  - Wu Di ×8 at A3.
  - Then shorter lines under the seals (D3–D4), ending for good at D6.
  - The tourists on the mountain path at G1–G2 are its last version.
- **Text:** big lines **top-left that stack and stay** (`TL`; `TL new` = clear and start a new block). **Punch lines centred and bigger** (`C`). Stamps (year · source) sit top-right, small.

## Title options (≤ 10 字; owner picks)
1. **汉柏的孩子，去守汉武帝** (10 字). **Lead (director #1, cold reader's click).**
   - **Why:** it makes you ask "wait, how?", it reverses who serves whom, and it promises exactly the film's peak. It is true as read: in 2024 six seedlings grown from 汉柏连理's seeds went to 茂陵 (row 43). It does not claim Wu Di planted the tree. 汉柏 names the subject for the feed and search.
   - **Weak spot:** it spends the peak. The cold reader says that's fine: "makes me want to see how it gets there".
2. **皇帝走了，这棵树还在** (9 字).
   - **Why:** the safest. True as read (rows 1, 14, 18–22) and states the device.
   - **Weak spot:** a familiar "the dynasty falls, the mountain stays" mood. It raises no question.
3. **它真是汉武帝种的吗** (9 字). New, replacing v1's 乾隆 title, which the cold reader found misleading.
   - **Why:** a question title that matches the end card and the 盖 turn (the cold reader's #1 surprise). It overclaims nothing, since the film's answer is 「大概」 (rows 9, 12).
   - **Weak spot:** without the cover tree it doesn't say which tree. Pair it with a cover reading 汉柏 / 泰山岱庙.
