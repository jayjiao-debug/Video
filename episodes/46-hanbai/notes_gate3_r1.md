# Gate 3 · round 1 · ep46 泰山汉柏 (look 「岱庙 · 同一个机位」)

Director, 2026-10-25. Material reviewed: all 38 key frames (`review/qa/frames/kf/`), all 41 people stills (`review/qa/frames/people/`, read as three contact sheets with spot zooms), `lines.json` v3 and `make_lines_v3.py`, `script.md` v2, `brief.md`, `crew_log.md`, and the facts.md rows behind every on-screen stamp. No gate 2a or 2b was run (owner asleep, single look, logged), so this is the first review of the look itself. I judged it against the brief, the rubric and every owner-notes row. Coordinates are in 1920×1080 frame pixels.

## Verdict: **REVISE**

The core is good and worth keeping. The tree is a real design: two trunks, layered pads, a glowing bark strip. The counter, stamps and subtitles all work, and 41 of 42 on-screen facts trace to verified rows. As it stands, though, the owner would send it back for five reasons:

1. **The peak is the cheapest-looking section of the film.** The emotional peak at 118.3 s (kf31), and the drive that leads into it (kf28–kf30), look like clip-art: lollipop trees, a toy truck and a road with poplars. 「你」 is not in the peak frame at all.
2. **Visible render faults.** There are dark rectangular boxes around props in the night grade, the flames are built from blocks, a stele shows as a half-transparent ghost, and the frame washes white at 124.2 s.
3. **The dead west trunk keeps changing state.** It is whole at frame 0 under a 2026 stamp. In 1762 it is dead in one shot and whole in the next. It then catches fire right after the 民国 smoke, which brings back the gate-1 High note 1 (it implies the 1928 cooking fires killed it).
4. **Frame 0 is a calm, wide establishing shot**, not the striking opening the owner rows ask for.
5. **The steles (in ~25 frames) and the mountain (in ~30 frames) are the wrong shapes.** The steles are domed cylinders that risk a phallic reading in screenshots. The mountain is a symmetric cone that reads as Mount Fuji, not 泰山.

## Don't change
- **The hero tree's design:** one root, two leaning trunks, layered flat pads, warm outline (kf00, kf04, kf17). It is the best drawing in the film.
- **kf04 as the cold-open payoff:** the push-in to the glowing living strip on 「今天，你只靠一条树皮活着」.
- **The counter:** 「相传 · 你 N 岁」, the dial icon, the small print 「按汉武帝首次封禅（前110年）推算 · 估算」, 「?」 during the fire, and every value (21, 1117, 1871, 1880, 2037, 2133, 2135).
- **The stamp system** (big year · place, small detail line, right bar), the corner mark, and the top-left stacking subtitles with the feathered scrim (once lightened on skies, note 18).
- **The six-seal idea:** 秦始皇 grey, 汉武帝 dashed, four lit with years 56 / 666 / 725 / 1008. Only the colour and the position change (note 11).
- **The eight 《汉书》 slip entries** and the empty 种树 slot idea (kf08); the 水经注 text with 盖 circled (kf09). Only their support and position change.
- **The Qianlong stele beats:** the dashed sight-line from the carved tree to the real one on 「望着」 (kf17), and the vertical poem 「名實賓主誰是乎」 on the stele (kf18, 故宫 reading).
- **The people:** one-tone, profile silhouettes that stand on the floor. Qing hats with red tassel; 民国 caps with no insignia; the visitor with a lit phone.
- **The seasons/snow time-lapse** (kf13); the night hold before the end question (kf35), once note 2 is fixed.
- **The 茂陵 覆斗 mound shape** with trees on it (kf29–kf31).
- **The Juno end card** (kf37): J monogram, gold title, question, 关注 Juno · 一起看懂世界, sources line. No "出品".
- **The timing:** title on the 13.588 crash, the empty breath bar, F6 on the 118.307 drop, and the v3 cut of the 赤眉 block.
