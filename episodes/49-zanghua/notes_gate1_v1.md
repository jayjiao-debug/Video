# Gate 1 notes: 听不懂，为啥知道在骂人？ script v1 — verdict: REVISE (one pass, all notes small)

Scores (gate 1 covers the script only): script 4. Frame 0 is the challenge, the twist is real (「都不存在」 is the
paper's own method) and the narrator is honest about the limits. Three things keep it from a 5: the ending makes
the wrong sound the hero, one line contradicts how the film was made (S1), and the middle 35 s is a list of facts.

**Against the owner's new ambition.** Will a 20-year-old remember it tomorrow? Yes, for two moments: 「这两个词，都不存在」
and saying 来/拆 out loud. Will they feel a person who cares? Only in places: R5, T2, T3 and X4 have a point of view.
T4–W6 is a fact list with 「我」 added. Note 8 gives that stretch a narrator who thinks.

## Your questions, answered
- **Facts.** R4 is right: Scherer 2001, listeners in 9 countries, anger 76 % overall (64–86 %), so 「平均七成多」 is fine
  (T2). 「十次里有六次多」 is an honest rendering of 63 % of trials (L6, the researcher's safe wording), but see note 5.
  W4 has a row: L10 quotes the paper itself ("harsh, abrasive sounds when distressed"). E3 has no row (note 1b).
  E5 is wrong in one word (note 6). Every other number traces to a ✓ row: 215 (L3), 37 (C1), 20 languages (L7),
  12/29 (L11, but see note 10), 1861/tan (B1).
- **Platform.** No real swear word is heard, spelled or hinted, and there are no bleeps. Tan's oath is a blank bar
  and the original swear words are blank tiles. There are two 谐音 risks (notes 3 and 4).
- **H1 then T2.** This is fair, not a cheat. H1 is true: clip A really is 骂人 (scolding), just in invented words.
  The only false statement is S2 「有一个是脏话」. That is the paper's own deception (L4), and T1–T2 confess it 11 s
  later. Keep it.
- **Participation beats.** The 3 s and 4 s countdowns are enough to commit to a choice between two. The 2.6 s pause
  in E2 is right for saying two words. T10 「你刚才选的，是B吗？」 guesses rather than judges, and the ~4 in 10 who
  chose A will want to comment. Keep it.
- **Tan.** Cut it (note 7), which brings the film to about 2:26. Spend about 7 s of that on note 8, for about 2:33.
- **Titles.** Ranked 2 > 1 > 3.
  - 「哪个词，更像脏话？」 is the new format itself: the viewer plays. It promises a test, not a result, and it is
    the safest cover.
  - 1 is searchable, but the obvious answer to it is 「语气」, which the film settles by 0:25. So it undersells round 2.
  - 3: 「它」 is vague (owner row "titles: concrete").
- **Ending.** Keep 来/拆 as a device, but change what it proves (note 1a).
- **Pacing.** About 6 % of the film is waiting. All six CHECK gaps are named participation beats. The owner is fine
  with up to about 20 % waiting, so don't trim the pauses.
