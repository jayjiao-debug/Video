# Brief: 你们互相喜欢，却都在等对方先开口

- **Episode:** ep53 · **Series:** VIBE知识大赏 (Juno) / free experiment
- **Idea in one sentence:** Pluralistic ignorance + the illusion of transparency mean two people who both like each other each wait for the other to make the first move, because each wrongly assumes their own interest is obvious and reads the other's silence as disinterest.
- **Why a 18–25 viewer cares:** Directly describes a situation most students have lived through — liking someone, assuming it's obvious, and staying stuck in mutual silence; forwarding this is itself a way to nudge a real crush or a friend stuck in the same spot.
- **Takeaway (one line the viewer should remember):** 你以为自己很明显，其实对方什么都没看出来——所以别等了。
