## Story-worthy

**5 most surprising facts:**
1. 撒谎时，你以为快一半的人会看穿你（48.8%），实际上只有四分之一（25.6%）看穿——和瞎猜没区别。(Gilovich et al. 1998, Study 1a)
2. 哪怕你拼命憋住表情喝下难喝的饮料，观察者也看不出来：你觉得会有近5个人（满分10）看出你的嫌弃，其实只有3.56个，和随机瞎猜差不多。(Gilovich et al. 1998, Study 2a)
3. 两个陌生人聊完5分钟天，双方都觉得对方没那么喜欢自己——这个"好感差距"甚至能在同住一年的室友之间持续到第二年2月才消失。(Boothby et al. 2018, Study 5)
4. 人一辈子回头看，最后悔的不是做错的事，而是没做的事——84%的人长期后悔清单里，"没做"的事比"做错"的事更让人耿耿于怀。(Gilovich & Medvec 1994)
5. 核心机制本身很"对称"又很荒谬：你把自己的沉默解释成"怕被拒绝"，却把对方一模一样的沉默解释成"对方没兴趣"——这是一种可以在实验室里反复测出来的认知错位（pluralistic ignorance）。(Vorauer & Ratner 1996)

**Strongest human story:** The "mutual pluralistic ignorance" pattern — two people who are both interested, both afraid, both reading the other's silence as the final answer, when the silence is actually the exact same fear mirrored back. The twist is that the barrier is not rejection itself but a *symmetrical misreading* — a problem that in principle resolves the instant either party gets one piece of missing information (as shown by the Stinson/Holmes experiment where simply telling a man his nervous date-partner was "anxious, not disinterested" made him act warmer).

**Best visual image:** Two identical speech bubbles/thought balloons facing each other, each person privately labeling their own silence "怕" (fear) but labeling the other person's identical silence "没兴趣" (not interested) — a literal mirror-image misattribution. Alternative: a split screen where the same silent beat plays twice, with different subtitle captions underneath depending on whose head we're inside.

## Risks

- **Core-study age and reach:** Vorauer & Ratner (1996) is the foundational paper for the entire film's claim, but it is almost 30 years old, uses undergraduate convenience samples (typical of the era), and we could not get full-text access to confirm the exact percentages beyond the qualitative mechanism (self-attribution to fear vs. partner-attribution to disinterest), which is independently corroborated by a 2010s review chapter (Stinson, Holmes & He) citing the same study. We found no large-scale direct replication of Vorauer & Ratner specifically. Treat the exact number in row 2/3 (544 participants, 76%/54%) as unverified-strength and avoid putting those specific digits on screen; the qualitative mechanism itself is well-established enough (consistently cited across 2+ decades of relationship-science literature, and structurally identical to the independently-very-well-replicated illusion of transparency / signal amplification literature) to carry the film's central claim.
- **Vorauer et al. 2003 (signal amplification bias):** We could not access the full text (ResearchGate/Academia.edu both returned 403). Only the qualitative finding is confirmed via multiple independent secondary descriptions. Do not put any specific percentage or beta from this paper on screen — use only the plain-language mechanism ("你发的信号，你自己觉得比实际更明显").
- **Illusion of transparency and liking gap:** These are the strongest-sourced claims in the table — verified directly from full primary PDFs with exact Ns, means and p-values, and the liking gap in particular is a well-powered, multi-study, pre-registered-adjacent 2018 Psychological Science paper with open data. Safe to lean on these for the hardest numbers in the script.
- **Regret study:** The 1994 original is strong but old; flag the 2023 large-N museum replication which shows a weaker/non-significant long-term effect — use honest, hedged phrasing ("更可能后悔没做的事", not "84%的人都会后悔").
- **No Chinese-population data:** Nothing credible found specific to China/暗恋 confession rates. Do not imply the numbers are China-specific; frame as a general human-psychology finding from lab studies (mostly North American undergraduates), which is an honest limitation to disclose if asked.
- **Unverified listicle stats:** Generic "暗恋成功率" style numbers are common online but have zero credible sourcing — explicitly excluded from use (see row 10/11).
- **Sensitive topics:** None — no real identifiable people, no deaths, no political content, no maps. Low platform risk. The main truthfulness risk is overstating the exact percentages from Vorauer & Ratner 1996 / Vorauer et al. 2003 beyond what we could verify; recommend the screenwriter uses only the rows marked as directly-verified (5, 6, 7, 8) for any on-screen numeral, and keeps rows 1–4 as qualitative mechanism-only language.
- **Overall verdict:** The mechanism the film is built on (pluralistic ignorance about first moves) is real, named, and has been cited continuously in the relationship-science literature since 1996, and is structurally reinforced by two very robust, independently-replicated sibling phenomena (illusion of transparency: Gilovich et al. 1998; liking gap: Boothby et al. 2018). It is robust enough to build the film's central claim on, provided the script states the *mechanism* with confidence but keeps any *exact percentage* confined to the directly-verified rows.
