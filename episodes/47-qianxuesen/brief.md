- **Episode:** ep47 · **Series:** VIBE知识大赏 (Juno).
- **Idea in one sentence:** the most famous rocket scientist in America buys a ticket home in 1950 and is locked up instead. For five years he is watched. Then a letter written on a cigarette paper, addressed in his wife's disguised left hand, goes through Belgium to Shanghai to Beijing to a negotiating table in Geneva, and the question is whether he gets home.
- **Suspense device:** a running counter, 「被困第 X 天」, from 6 Sep 1950 (Terminal Island) to 4 Aug 1955 (ban lifted, day 1,793) and 17 Sep 1955 (the ship sails, day 1,837).

The same US official said both 「宁可枪毙他」 and, later, "the stupidest thing this country ever did".
