# Script: 七年后，你还是你吗？ — v1 (2026-10-20)

**Spine:** the "7-year" myth → the ship of Theseus asks the real question → the bomb carbon clock dates your cells: much is new, but the parts you see and think with never change → twist: the cells stay, yet *you* change (63-year study) → and you never notice (end of history illusion) → answer: you are the voyage, not the planks.
**Takeaway:** 身体最核心的部分一直都在；变的是你，只是你自己察觉不到。
**Tone:** quiet, warm, a little melancholic; the music should breathe (piano/strings), not drive.
**Length:** ~2:00 (times provisional until the music is chosen).

## Notes
- Line 11: sources differ on the size of the 1960s ¹⁴C spike, so the line says only 猛增 (no multiple).
- Line 13: Frisén's lab, Karolinska Institutet, Stockholm.
- Line 15: "几天" = the gut-surface lining (~5 days); the deeper gut cells average 15.9 years — we don't claim the whole gut renews in days.
- Line 17: rib muscle = intercostal muscle, 15.1 years average (in adults in their late 30s); keep "平均15岁".
- Line 28–29: "as if from different people" is the press wording; the paper's simple correlations showed no significant stability; a corrected model kept some for mood stability. The line says 几乎对不上 (not 完全).
- Line 32: Quoidbach (then Harvard), Gilbert (Harvard), Wilson (UVA).
