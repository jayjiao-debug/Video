# Script: 假如你是清明上河图  (v1)

**Logline:** 你就是《清明上河图》。九百年里，人人都想留住你，所以你一直在被带走：被调包、被藏、被抄、被太监和末代皇帝带出宫，在乱中下落不明，最后在一堆残次品里被认了出来。
**Takeaway:** 人人都想据为己有的东西，直到不再属于任何一个人，才终于留住了。代价是：它很难再被看见。
**Length:** 180.0 s (3:00), the owner's cap ("within 3 minutes", ep35). 51 subtitle lines; the title card sits at 23.25–26.25.

- **Timing is nominal.** No track has been chosen yet. Every start is on a 0.25 s grid (8th notes at a nominal 120 bpm), and **all lines will be retimed to the bars of the chosen track** (see "Music and retiming" below).
- **Files:** this script, plus `lines.json` (51 lines, `[[start, end, "text"], ...]`, `[red]` = red key word). Screenwriter, 2026-10-24.
- **Counting:** 字 as in `pace.py` (a number counts as one). The ≤ 16 rule is checked on display width: punctuation counts 1, each digit 0.55. The widest line is 16.0.
- **Facts:** only ✓ rows of facts.md are used. No 相传 row and no ✗ row is used. A few lines are framing rather than fact; they are listed under "Facts used".

**Inputs taken from the gate-2a cold read** (`coldread_gate2a.json`):
- **「杨准跋」 meant nothing to the reader.**
  - The stamp now reads 「裱画匠调包（元·杨准记）」.
  - Line W3 says it plainly: 「文人杨准后来买下你，记下了调包。」 The viewer learns how we know.
- **「你约250岁」 → 二百五.**
  - No beat lands on 250. The swap beat shows 「240多岁」 (至正 swap, a few years before 1351), and W3 shows 「约251」.
  - The counter rolls through 250 for a frame or two and never stops there. The joke would undercut the theft beat, so it is avoided on purpose.
- **The dimensions line was unreadable and felt beside the point.**
  - It is gone from the stamps. 「5米多长 · 几百个人」 appear once, as picture labels, while the scroll is being painted (B1).
- **「贵官」 → 「权贵」**, the word facts.md (C9) uses.
- **r2 cold read, 「杨准」 next to 「权贵」 (is he the 权贵? before or after the swap?).** W3 is now 「文人【杨准】后来买下你，记下了调包。」: 后来 and 文人 set him apart from the 权贵. The stamps order it: W1–W2 「元 · 至正年间」, then W3 「1351」.

## Story in 3 lines
1. 九百年，人人都想留住你，所以你四次被带出皇宫、五次被送回（故宫说的"四出五进"）：调包、藏匿、抄家、太监带出宫、末代皇帝偷运出宫、下落不明、在残次品堆里被认出。
2. 第一个写下画家名字的金人张著，在你身后留了一句"藏者宜宝之"；后来的人确实都很珍惜你，珍惜到偷你、换你、藏你、抄你，宋元明清四个朝代的皇宫都没留住你。
3. 直到你不再属于任何一个人，你才终于不再被带走；代价是展一次要静养三年以上，所以排四五个小时，只能看你一两分钟。

## Title options (≤ 10 字)
1. **假如你是清明上河图** (9 字). **Recommended.**
   - It is the format's own title shape, which the owner asked us to replicate, so the series and the reference read as one family.
   - It promises the device (you are the painting) without promising any single event.
   - It can't overclaim.
2. **谁偷走过清明上河图** (9 字). The clickiest.
   - It asks a question the film answers three times, each from a ✓ row:
     - a palace mounter (C9);
     - a eunuch (C20, the Palace Museum's own word is 偷盗);
     - the last emperor (C24, 偷运).
   - It asks "who", not "how many times", because how the scroll left in 1127 is unknown (C5) and the film doesn't call that one theft.
3. **清明上河图，四出五进** (9 字 + comma).
   - It uses the Palace Museum's own four characters (O6), with concrete numbers, and it is the film's spine.
   - A viewer who doesn't know the phrase still gets "进出了什么？", and the film answers it in its first life beat.
   - Weakest of the three as a hook.

Cover note (owner row 105): the cover carries the title alone. No hook line, and nothing that spoils the 残次品 reveal.

**Why this present-day hook.**
- **Chosen: the 2025 queue (P3).** It is the viewer's own situation: they queued, or saw friends queue. It sets up a question the ending answers (P5/O9), and it feeds the comment question.

## Screen furniture (for the art director and animator)
- **Subtitle.** Top-left at the gate-2a position (x 150, first line y ≥ 132), at most 2 lines.
  - **Reveal.** Typed lines reveal in ≤ 0.4 s. Their budget includes that 0.4 s, so reading time counts from the last glyph.
  - **Cut-on lines.** Teaser lines T1–T4 and E3 appear whole on the cut.
  - **Red.** Key words in `[ ]` are red.
  - **Centred lines.** N2 and Q end in ？ and are centred, using the ½ em pull-back from owner row 97.
- **Stacking (the format's 1–2 lines per beat).**
  - `lines.json` gives each line its own reading window; windows never overlap.
  - A line marked **"line 1 (stays)"** in the table stays on screen above the next line, its line 2, until line 2 ends. Then both clear.
  - `pace.py` was run on the reading windows. Stacking only adds display time, so it can never make a line too fast. (A reviewer extracting subtitles from the render will see line 1 "waiting": that's the stack, not slack.)
- **Stamp (top-right).**
  - Line 1 `— 年份 · 地点` (56 px).
  - Line 2: event + plain-language source in brackets (40 px, ≤ 14 字). Sources are written so a viewer gets them: 故宫博物院 · 元·杨准记 · 余辉考 · 故宫装裱记录 · 《赏溥杰书画目》.
  - Line 3: an optional detail or quote, only where the stamp holds ≥ 4 s (Z2 落款, F2 冯保跋, P1/P2 dates, D1 counts, E1 seal count).
  - `〃` = unchanged from the line above. Stamps change on a beat.
- **Era chip + age counter (bottom-left).** `北宋 · 你 0 岁` … `你 900多岁`.
  - Anchor c. 1100 (facts O8, 估算).
  - "约N" where the stamp has a year; "N多岁" where the date is only an era.
  - The counter rolls between beats and is hidden in the hook, the teaser, the present day, the title and the end card.
  - Era chips: 北宋 · 金 · 元 · 明 · 清 · 民国 · 新中国. 伪满 appears only in the stamp, never as an era chip.
- **四出五进 tally.**
  - Four 出 marks and five 进 marks, labelled 「四出五进 —— 故宫博物院」.
  - Placed near the counter, or under the gate when the gate is on screen.
  - Each mark lights on its beat: 进 B2 · 出 O1 · 进 I2 · 出 W2 · 进 M3 · 出 F1 · 进 Q1 · 出 P1 · 进 R1 (complete).
- **Handover set (column "Set").** One reusable place where the in/out moments happen. Between uses only the banners (宋/元/明/清/none + museum plaque), the era chip, the counter and the tally change.
  - **Recommended: a palace gate (宫门).** The Palace Museum's sentence is literally 「四出五进宫门」 (O6). The gate also lets the teaser shots T2/T3 be the same shots that pay off at F1/P1.
  - **If the chosen look keeps its own set** (A: 虹桥 crest; B: 界画 table + seal run; C: the box), the in/out beats happen there, with the gate as a silhouette behind.
  - **Used at:** H2, T2, T3, S1, B2, O1, I2, W2, M3, F1, Q1, P1, P2, R1, E4, E5.
- **People.**
  - Whole figures only, never hands from off-screen (brief).
  - No likeness of 溥仪; at most a back-view silhouette, never comic.
  - No plane or airfield in 1945; no maps.
  - Deaths (陆完's mother, 王忬) are not shown or mentioned.

## Music and retiming (no track yet)
- **Title (23.25).** Lands on the hardest drop (owner rows 26, 84). Move the whole opening with it. If the drop comes later, hold N2 longer, but don't add a line (row 84 allows a hook question to fill).
- **Teaser T1–T4.** Hard cuts on the beat, about 2.5 s each (≈ 5 beats at 120 bpm). The year stamp slams on each cut.
- **E3 「偷你、换你、藏你、抄你」.** Four consecutive beats, one flash and one red word per beat. This is the film's rhythmic punch.
- **V3 → Y1.** The 1-s gap (116.25–117.25) is the film's one deliberate picture pause plus a music dip (pauses.md). Y4 「才是你」 lands on the next strong downbeat.
- **Tally ticks and seal slams.** On beats. Stamps change on beats.
- **Retiming rule.** Map new → old piecewise per line. Never shorten a line below its "Needs" + 0.4 s (typed lines). The end card holds the question to the song's end, at most 3:00.
