- **Painting at frame 0 and the peak (sc 01 and 28):** the A-frame mast pivots at its foot on the foredeck and folds back toward the stern. It passes through the up-left positions and comes to rest along the cabin roof (129.7–130.3). The crew stand on the deck and the roof working ropes. The bridge crowd stands behind the railing. The ropes from the bridge down to the boat match the real painting.
- **Sc 01:** the holders face each other and the scroll. The walkers face the way they move (right, 出). The lanterns go out in order from the palace side.
- **Sc 02:** the title band behaves and nothing about it is illogical.
- **Sc 03:** the swap reads. His hands are on the rollers at 12.0, 12.6 and 13.2, and the glow leaves with him.
- **Sc 05:** both arms are round the bundle, light shows through the cloth, there is snow on the rails, and the far windows are warm.
- **Sc 06:** the window shaft and the dust are fine.
- **Sc 07:** the ramp lighting falls off with distance from the door, and the walkers all go the same way.
- **Sc 08:** the lanterns light right → left, and the boat's mast and stays are upright and consistent.
- **Sc 27:** he really sits on the chair with his knees under the table. At 122.5 his hand is on the battered roll on the crate, and at 123.1 the roll is set down on the table under his hand.
- **Sc 29:** they walk left (进), their legs alternate, the two are not in lockstep, and the rims come from the sun behind them.
