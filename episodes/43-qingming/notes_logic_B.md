## Looks right

- **Scene 22:** All four officials face left, the way they walk, with the box held forward on the forearms at chest height. Only the 2nd box glows, and the glow is self-lit. The lanterns are on the near railing in front of the officials, so the layering is correct. The mast tilts away from the boatman, and his rope runs to the masthead, so he is visibly paying it out. The tilt grows steadily frame to frame (vertical at 87.6, about 20° at 88.2, about 35° at 88.8, about 70° at 89.4). The lantern light and the dusk sky are consistent.
- **Scene 23:** No seal touches a painted figure. 嘉庆御览之宝 is centred on the mounting silk, and 石渠宝笈 straddles the first join. The glyph columns of these two read right to left correctly. The handscroll layout is right: brocade on the right, then mounting, then the painting opening on the left. The seals keep their size and position through the pull-back. The bottom eave silhouette matches the far-bank roofline of scene 24.
- **Scene 24:** The young man is in profile facing right (his direction of travel), with both arms round the bundle at his chest and the near forearm under it. He is correctly behind the near railing, with posts and lanterns occluding his robe. His feet land on the snowy deck line (97.2) and alternate through the stride. The far windows are the only other warm light.
- **Scene 25 crossings:** Each figure faces its direction of travel. The 2nd figure in line is the one that glows (confirmed at 99.0 and 100.2). The warm rims on its neighbours face the glowing carrier and the nearby railing is lit, so that light has a real source. Each figure follows the arch of the deck (rising, then falling). Each one's own stride changes frame to frame, with no sliding. The footprints appear only behind walkers, in four depth lanes. The pan into the snow and the ledger transition are clean.
- **Scene 25 desk:** The man stands *behind* the desk. The desk top occludes his robe, his feet are slightly higher than the front desk-leg feet, and he does not cut through the table. The box and the grey roll both sit on the desk top. The lamp lights the ledger.
- **Scene 26 building and seasons:** There is no glow from 106.2 to the end except item 3. The interior seen through the window matches the light patches visible in that same window on the pull-back (106.2 → 106.8). The tree runs green → ochre → white → summer green (1945 Aug), and its base sits in front of the building's ground line. The door and window proportions are plausible. Both cars face right with their headlights at the front, moving right. The guards face right toward the exit and their feet are on the floor. The moonlight on the floor and the dust beam come from the open-door side, and the heap sits in that moonlight.
