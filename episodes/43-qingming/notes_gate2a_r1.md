## Rulings on the art director's two questions

**(1) B's subtitle flips to dark ink on light silk: accepted for look B.** Polarity is the only change. Font, 58 px size, position, character reveal and the red key-word role stay, and the corner mark and end card carry the series identity. On silk, white type would need a dark shade blob, which is the "lone black panel in a light world" the owner rejected (ep15, row 70).

Measured contrast:
- ink #1D140D on silk #C8AD82: 8.4 : 1;
- ink on colophon paper #D6C29A: 10.4 : 1;
- key-word red #B42E22 on bare silk: only 2.9 : 1, but 4.6 : 1 on the silk halo.

Conditions:
- **(a)** Keep the feathered silk halo under every line. It is what keeps painted linework out of the glyphs.
- **(b)** One polarity per scene. Never flip while a line is on screen.
- **(c)** In the three.js pull-backs where the scroll lies on a dark table, use the ivory version, and keep the subtitle zone entirely over dark or entirely over silk, never straddling the two.
- **(d)** The stamp and counter follow the same rule (they already do).

This is B's variant only. The series standard does not change.

**(2) Subtitles top-left instead of the lower band: accepted for this episode.** The owner asked to replicate this format, and the brief lists the top-left subtitle as format furniture. The lower band (y 820–960) remains the series default.

Conditions:
- **(a) One fixed position all film** (row 116): x 150, first line y ≥ 132 (clears the Douyin watermark), at most 2 lines, and the line ends ≥ 120 px left of the stamp block (16 字 × 58 px ends at about x 1080).
- **(b) A quiet top-left in every composition.** Each storyboard scene states it keeps the top-left ~1000 × 240 px quiet: sky in A, unpainted silk or a mist band in B, the lid lining in C.
- **(c) Fast reveal.** The character reveal completes within ≤ 0.4 s (about 25 ms per character), and the reading budget (0.5 + n/7 + 0.6 s) counts from the last glyph. Otherwise a typed line steals reading time.
- **(d) Centring rule still applies where text is centred.** Left-aligned lines need no ？ centring, but the centred title and the end-card question still use the ½ em pull-back.
- **(e) Known, accepted risk.** In Douyin's landscape full-screen mode, the avatar/title overlay covers the top-left for a few seconds; it covers 谁 in the reference at 0:00. Most viewers watch in the portrait feed (row 44), where nothing overlays the video.
