## Looks right

- **31.2–33.0, the ink-trace wipe and the painting drawing itself:** the bridge crowd walks with shoulder poles and the horse and cart sit on the deck. The boat's lowered A-frame mast and ropes make sense, and the people on the boat roof stand upright.
- **34.2, the handover:** both men face each other and each has one hand on one end of the glowing roll. The roll's glow lights both sleeves and faces, and nothing floats.
- **34.8–35.4:** the court figures face left and walk left with their feet on the deck. The roll is held in the front figure's hand and lights his robe.
- **36.6–37.2, the refugees:** they face right and walk right with alternating legs and varied, not lockstep, poses. The glow in the cart bed lights the follower's robe from below, the wheels sit on the deck, nobody is seen taking you, and the lanterns go dark when the fire comes.
- **38.4–41.0:** the corner search shows bare silk with no signature. The seam and the silk edge are plausible.
- **42.6–43.8:** the brush tip is on the paper at the position of the newest column (about (697,647) and (701,622)). 张著 sits on the stool, faces the paper and is lit from the open painting at his right.
- **44.4–52.8:** the colophon columns accumulate right to left in reading order and the glow lands character by character. 翰林张择端 (x about 1270–1400) and 藏者宜宝之 (x about 1230–1390) are inside the x 1200–1700 spec. At 52.8 the colophon is joined to the tail with its roller.
- **134.05–134.15:** the iris opens from the small lit gate passage. The 1950s figures face and walk left down the slope with their feet on the deck and the glow in the front man's hands.
- **135.0–136.8, and 141.0:** the posts become a dashed ear outline and then the donkey on its patch, with the ears lined up on the posts. After the peel the lens shows the posts standing over blank silk.
- **139.2–141.6:** the fluorescent tubes are lit. The tweezers hand is on the silk under the lens at 141.0. A hand holds the lamp arm as it swings at 141.6, so cause and effect is right there.
- **142.8–145.8:** the tail reads clearly at small size as columns with red seals at the joins. 藏者宜宝之 glows at x about 1240–1380.
