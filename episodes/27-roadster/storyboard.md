# Storyboard v1: 这辆车，凭什么能开上天花板？ (look: 看得见的空气)

Look: floodlit night test site at dusk (mid-tone blues, not black). The car is an original, generic pearl-white coupe (no brand marks), and it is always the brightest thing in frame. One key light per shot. Air is drawn as drifting particles; the cut-aways turn the car into x-ray glass. Amber is for numbers, frost-white for gas. Every label is 思源黑体 on a feathered scrim; subtitles sit on the series strip.
