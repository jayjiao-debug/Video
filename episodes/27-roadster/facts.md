## Story-worthy
- A 2-ton car hangs upside down because the air under it is only ~5% thinner than outside. The atmosphere does the holding.
- "Flying" = letting go of a balloon: a tank of compressed air blown out fast. No wings, no propellers.
- The headline stunts are on the invite-only, not-road-legal trim.

## Risks
- Tank pressure, flight height and duration unpublished: don't invent numbers.
- Whether a person drove the demo car is unreported (Electrek pre-event: could be remote-operated).
- 0–60 figures are Tesla claims.
- 0–60 mph ≈ 0–97 km/h: don't call it 百公里加速 without saying "约".
