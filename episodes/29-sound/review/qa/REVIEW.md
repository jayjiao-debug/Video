# Review: 为什么好听？ v2 (高清原片, 2:43) — verdict: BLOCKED (one audio fix), then FIX FIRST

The demos are real and land: every effect checks out on the audio, and the particle title hits the drop exactly. One audio fault blocks publishing: the "remove the middle" demo is built in exact antiphase, so on a single phone speaker the film goes silent twice. After that the main problem is the picture between ideas. Almost every scene change fades to an empty starfield and back (about 20 dips). Some labels hang over the next scene, the key labels are too small for a phone, and one citation is attached to the wrong claim.

The particles form the title in the ear colours (cyan → gold → magenta) with a live sound-wave underline. There is no white flash and no freeze, motion runs straight into the hit, and the word is centred to 0.5 px.
- **Demos match the audio:** every demo is honest and checked on the track.
  - varispeed
  - binaural 363.3 / 369.3 Hz
  - prank (one ear only under each arrow)
  - precedence (equal level, 18 ms lead that swaps sides at 1:11)

- **Clearest demos:** the live spectrum demos (low-pass, high-pass, phantom bass, age cut) and the wave pairs with Lissajous 2:1 / 3:2 / clash. They read instantly even at phone size.

- **Loudness:** −14.05 LUFS integrated, −1.1 dBTP. The drop sits about 2.5 dB over the body, and no stretch falls more than about 6 dB under it.
