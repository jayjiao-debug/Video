# Cold read, gate 4: 《为什么好听？》 (viewer POV, subtitles + frames only)

**3s verdict:** I keep watching. "你听的这首歌，被放慢过" speaks to anyone who plays 慢放 versions, and #5 "声音还能在两只耳朵间跳" is what really gets me to stay.

## Where I'd swipe
- **15.7-23.9s (#7-#9):** speaker/air/Hz textbook physics right after an ear trick was promised.
- **42.2-48.3s (#15-#16):** "主音约360赫兹，正好是鼓声的8倍" is a number with no payoff.
- **80.5-87s (title burst):** the explosion, gold ring and 3s of no subtitle look like the outro. **This is the biggest drop-off.** The second half (the actual answer to the title) would lose people here.
- **130.5-140.7s (#47-#51):** 10s of one-word recap.

## 哇 / 原来如此
- #25-#27: "骗你的！...一边只晚0.02秒 / 大脑只信先到的那只耳朵". The best moment.
- #18-#20: the wobble "两只耳朵里，都没有它，是大脑算出来的".
- #34-#35 with the 3:2 frame: "高音走三步，低音走两步，就对齐". This is where I finally understood what a ratio means.
- #41-#45: slowed = sad = how sad people talk. It closes the loop with the hook.

## Empty or confusing
- #13 asks "你还听得见鼓吗？" and never answers. The answer (brain fills in the bass) is only small text on the frame.
- #17 363/369 shows up with no setup. The 6 Hz difference is never said.
- #21-#22 "年纪越大，高音越先听不见 / 就像这样——" lasts 1.6s and I don't know if I missed a tone.
- #28 "永远在上升的音" is never explained.
- #43 "再放慢一点——" breaks the #42/#44 sentence.
- #52 "振动接力" was never set up.
- #39-#40 "和弦都对得齐，所以它好听" is too thin to answer the title.

## What I'd tell a friend
"Sound is air squeezed X times a second. Your brain makes up half of what you hear: a wobble that's in neither ear, and left/right from just a 0.02s delay. Notes sound good together in simple ratios like 2:1 and 3:2. Slowed songs sound sad because slow and low is how sad people talk."

## The one change that would make me share it
Turn #21-#22 into a **real ear test**: play the tone, give its Hz on screen, hold it 4-5s, and ask "听得见吗？听不见的扣1". That is the moment I'd forward to my roommate. Close second: put #25-#27 (the 0.02s prank) much earlier, and keep the 81s title burst from reading as an ending by putting a subtitle over it right away.
