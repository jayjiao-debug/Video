## Title ranking (final)
1. **同一段旋律，8种曲风**. It names the one thing no earlier episode had and lands literally on the 20.0 drop where the melody first turns into rock. It has a concrete number and can't overclaim. The cold reader picked it independently.
2. **4步听出曲风**. The clearest promise and the biggest type on a phone. The cold reader found it dry, and the steps aren't the film's surprise.
3. **一耳朵听出曲风**. Confident and colloquial, but "一耳朵" slightly oversells what the film teaches: 4 steps, with a quiz showing that one clue fools you.

- The one-melody device: pitches fixed, the 原版 at 0:16 ("记住这段") and again at 2:03, same FM e-piano patch both times.
- The 动 / 打 / 次 system, used consistently all film, with the drum names only as small labels.
