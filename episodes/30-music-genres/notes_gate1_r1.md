# Gate 1 notes: 一耳朵听出曲风 script.md + lines.json v1 — verdict: REVISE

Scores (rubric 1–5, only the areas this gate covers): script **3** · visual — · info — · edit — · motion — · sound (plan only, nothing heard yet) —

Script = 3. It is clear and correct and the everyday pictures are good, but there are four problems. (a) Every genre line plays over a clue that sounds for only 1 bar, and the lines run back to back at the minimum reading time. (b) Drum names are used before the viewer has heard which sound is which. (c) The 4 clues are promised at 0:11 but not named again until the recap at 1:47, so the middle plays as a parade of genres. (d) The end question can't be answered with what the film teaches. All four are fixable inside the script. First round, so there is nothing to compare with yet. No cold-read file was in the folder when I wrote this, so these notes rest on my own read.

Pace check (pace.py, copied to scratch): 44 lines, 0 % waiting, no TOO FAST. The only flag is the 18.5–22.0 title gap, which is fine. Lines hold 2.2–3.9 s, most 2.4–2.8 s. Longest displayed lines: 16 字 (l.8, l.29). The 16-字 limit is met.

## Titles (provisional ranking, so round 2 doesn't need another pass; confirm at APPROVE)
1. **同一段旋律，8种曲风**. It names the one thing no earlier episode had, and it lands literally on the 20.0 drop where the melody first turns into rock. It has a concrete number and can't overclaim. Keep "8" only if all 8 genres stay.
2. **4步听出曲风**. The clearest promise and the biggest type on a phone. Fair as long as the quiz still shows that one step alone fools you. Close second.
3. **你怎么听出是爵士？**. It reads as a jazz film when jazz is 1 of 8 (row 11), and the trailing ？ needs optical centring.

## What must not change (it works)
- The device: **one original melody**, pitches and octave fixed, across 8 genres, so "旋律一个音都没改" is true by construction. The bare theme as the control at 0:16 and again at 1:41.
- Jazz at the **same 120 as rock**: a clean A/B where the only difference is straight vs swing.
- The everyday pictures. They are the best part of the script: 像走路 (house = footsteps, row 7), 叮——叮哒 / 蹦蹦跳跳, 沙沙 like an old record, 下楼梯一脚踩空, 吉他“嚓、嚓”, 踩镲像拉拉链, 吉他声被“弄脏” / 糊成一团, 又快又慢.
- The **quiz**: the steppers lure (kick on every beat, row 26), the 3-2-1 countdown on the beats, the reveal on the downbeat, and the lesson that one clue alone fools you. It is the best "aha" in the film.
- Metal's three-step A/B (normal → muddy → clean) with the *why* (row 32).
- The hook mechanics: subtitle and sound on frame 0, the 0.25秒 number living on the picture (stopwatch), line 3's non-musician reassurance, and the promise in lines 5–6 before the title.
- The title slams on the 20.0 drop; the end card fades over the final bar to a downbeat.
- Careful wording, all of which should stay: no % in the hook (row 2); reggae "常见" so the quiz doesn't contradict it; trap "隔好久" (counting-safe); "复杂的和弦"; "懒懒散散", not "拖"; 走路 as a comparison, not a cause.
- Risk avoidance: no real songs, artists, brands, flags, maps, smoke, leaves or skulls; no trap etymology; no dembow lawsuit.
