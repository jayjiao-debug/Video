The 4 clues promised at 11s aren't labeled during the genre section, so I couldn't tell which clue each genre was teaching.

**What I'd tell a friend:** genre is the costume, not the song. Same tune, but change where the drums hit, swung vs. straight, faster or slower than walking, and which instrument is loudest, and you get rock, jazz, reggae or metal. Dance music is basically walking speed.

**Title pick:** 同一段旋律，8种曲风. It's concrete, promises something I can hear, and matches the video. 你怎么听出是爵士？ misleads, because jazz is only about 8s. 4步听出曲风 sounds like a dry tutorial, and the steps only appear at 107s.
