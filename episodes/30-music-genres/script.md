# Script: 一耳朵听出曲风  (v2, gate 1 round 2)

**Logline:** You can tell a genre from a quarter of a second of sound, but you can't say why. We play ONE melody of our own in 8 genres. Only the style changes, so you can hear the 4 clues that give a genre away, unlock them one by one, and then use them.
**Takeaway:** 听歌认曲风，问4个问题：鼓点在哪一拍？直的还是摇摆？比走路快还是慢？谁的声音最显眼？
**Length:** 2:27.5 (147.53 s) · subtitle-only · 43 lines · pace.py: 0 % waiting, no TOO FAST · **listen-only time 24.9 s = 16.9 %** (the listen bars below) · median line hold 2.9 s.

## Picture system (the look is the art director's)
One **beat grid** stays on screen all film: 4 rows × 16 steps, filling most of the frame. Each drum row is labelled by its **syllable in big type (动 / 打 / 次)**, with the small name under it (底鼓 / 军鼓 / 踩镲). The 4th row, 招牌, is renamed per genre (电吉他, 合成器, 叮叮镲, 黑胶, 吉他嚓, 尼龙吉他, 808, 失真吉他). Cells light on their hits; a playhead sweeps; big beat numbers 1 2 3 4 sit over steps 1/5/9/13. Also on screen: a **genre tag**; a **tempo needle** against a 走路 footprint mark (numbers only at lines 12–13 and 17); and **4 clue slots** under the grid, locked at 0:11, each unlocking on a downbeat with a ≤ 5-字 label (鼓点在哪拍 / 直·摇摆 / 快·慢 / 招牌声). After the quiz, a "bend" is marked on a slot as a small badge (slot 1: guitar icon; slot 3: two needles + "又快又慢"; slot 4: "为什么？" → two dots). No real songs, brands, flags, maps, smoke, leaves or skulls, and no people's appearance as genre stereotypes.
