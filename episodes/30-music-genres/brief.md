# Brief: 一耳朵听出曲风

- **Episode:** ep30 · **Series:** VIBE知识大赏 (Juno)
- **Owner's request (his words):** "create a video about different genres of music and how normal people without background knowledge in music can use to identify what type of music it is, give fun and intuitive examples, try keep the audio and video illustration clear."
- **Idea in one sentence:** You don't need music theory to name a genre — listen for 3–4 physical clues (where the drums hit, straight vs swing feel, how fast, one signature sound), and the SAME melody becomes rock, jazz, EDM, hip-hop, reggae… in front of your ears.

- **Takeaway:** "听鼓点在哪一拍、直还是摇摆、快还是慢、哪件乐器最显眼——四步听出曲风。"
