## The one change that would most make me share it
**Put the payoff and the challenge in the first 3 seconds.** Open with a 2–3s rapid audio montage of the same melody flipping through about four genres. Then say "看完你也能分出来，结尾有一题". Right now I hear the first transformation at 24s and learn about the final question at 126s. The quiz is what makes me comment and send it to a friend ("你猜是啥"). If I know about it from the start, I stay through 波萨诺瓦 and the recap, and I share it as a challenge rather than a lesson.
