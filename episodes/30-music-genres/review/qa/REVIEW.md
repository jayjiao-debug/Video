## Spot checks the brief asked for
- **Pads light exactly on the sound: PASS.** I measured the luma of every pad on every frame (4425 frames) and compared it with all K/S/H events in events30.json, excluding scan, bossa and bonus. Of 692 events, 654 show a clear pad jump. **639 jump on exactly the expected frame** (the first frame with t ≥ event time). The 15 "early" ones are trap rolls and one lo-fi pickup at 51.656: that pad was already lit by an earlier hit in the same step, or the event falls before the segment's t0, so it belongs to bar −1 and is never drawn (1 hat, negligible). The 38 with no clear jump are mostly trap roll repeats on an already-lit pad. On the audio side, all 269 K and 116 S events have an onset within ±60 ms of their time, with a median of −10 ms (analysis-window bias) and 90 % within 20 ms.
- **One hero per line:** this holds for most lines because of the 30 % dimming. It fails at l.18 and l.29 (row 4), l.22 (row 5) and l.6 (row 6). It is also undermined everywhere after 0:31.5 by the ghost (row 1).
- **Label sizes:** the drum syllables (88), beat numbers (62/72), VFD (84), 招牌 label (56), keys (52), answer keys (58), 空 (60), 5个音 / 2个音 / 上：摇摆 / 拇指 → 动 (52) are fine. The tags at 40–42 (招牌声, 底鼓/军鼓/踩镲, 5种曲风里选, 差一点, TEMPO) meet the ≥ 40 floor. Two labels fail the ≥ 52 floor (row 6).
- **Dips / blinks / flashes:** there is no white flash (the 20.0 burst is row-coloured spill, frame-diff 9.0 max), no dip to black, and no whole-frame pulse. The blinks are the VFD hole at the title (row 3), the subtitle blink (row 8) and "undefined?" (row 2).

## Script pass
The chain reads well: you can tell genres apart → why → 4 clues → one genre per clue → a quiz with a lure → the recap as a checklist → a final question. The hook puts the viewer's own ability in line 1, on frame 0, with music in the first second. The numbers match facts.md (row 1 above-chance at 250 ms, row 5 54 % at 125 ms vs a 20 % chance, row 7 walking 120 steps/min, row 8 the 120 bpm peak, row 11 lo-fi 70–80). l.1 "就猜得出" is the strongest reading of "above chance", but l.2 immediately puts a real number on it, so it does not mislead. No line needs the next one to decode it. One repeat to watch: l.12 and l.13 both land on "120", which is fine as a payoff. facts.py found no source problems, and the brand grep found no "Juno 出品".
