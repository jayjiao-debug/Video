## Why REVISE and not APPROVE
The look, the picture system and 16 of the 19 transitions are right. Built as written, though, the film would get sent back for two kinds of fault:
- **The picture contradicts the v2 track in three places.** The storyboard has a double kick on trap's last beat, but that beat is a 32nd hat zipper. It has a metal "choke", but the kick plays all 16 steps up to 122.72. It also cites the v1 m4a.
- **The context row (y 660–780) has several occupants at once, with no timeline.** In the quiz it has to hold the answer keys, the tempo strip and "slot 1's key", and that key does not exist while the clues are collapsed. The bend badges sit on bezel LEDs at the edge of the subtitle band.
- Smaller items: a geometry collision (the title VFD overlaps the corner mark), a 200 px countdown inside a 104 px VFD, and one fast pan.

All of these are specification fixes, roughly one hour for the art director. **The animator can start the core now:** Panel, PadGrid driven by events30.json, RowLabels, BeatNumbers, StepLeds, Vfd tag mode, Camera. No note below changes those.
