## Ranking (one line each)
1. **A 鼓机面板.** It is the brief's "every hit lights up" taken literally: hits are light, so the beat is seen in time, not just marked. It has the real light, glow and material the owner asks for (row 72 炫丽). A pad grid is a recognisable object on frame 0 for Douyin viewers who know 打击垫 videos, and it fills the frame. Its risks (expected, one faceplate for 2½ minutes, a busy key frame where beat 3 outshines the empty beat 1: luma 136–167 vs 90) are all fixable in the build. Notes 2–6 cover them.
2. **C 鼓手草稿本.** It has the clearest key frame (the red-circled 空 + red ① is the only red in the grid) and the only natural home for the everyday analogies as margin doodles. But it is a flat, light, paper world, and owner rows 72/34 (flat clip-art "cheap", flat game-UI "太low") say that style is the biggest taste risk. The ticked-box strip reads as a worksheet / bullet journal, which the art director's own "must not" list warns against. Highlighter marks also stay on the page, so "now" depends on the pencil arrow alone.
3. **B 唱片封套.** It is the most distinctive, and spin speed = tempo is a lovely mapping. But the circle caps the hero at ≈ 30 % of the frame. The signature-sound clue sits on the smallest (inner) ring, and the key 空 lands at the top edge. The linear analogies (zipper, swing ruler, footprints) fight the ring. With no grooves and no visible sleeve edge, the disc reads closer to a roulette wheel or dartboard than a record. The art director lists that as a must-not.
