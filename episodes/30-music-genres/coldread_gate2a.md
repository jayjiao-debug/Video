## Things that confused me across all looks
- **次 / 打 / 动.** In the hook frames these have no labels, so they read as a nonsense word ("次打动" in A/C, "动打次" in B). I only understood they're hi-hat / snare / kick when I saw the key frames. If the hook said "动次打次" (the meme everyone knows), I'd get it instantly.
- **Outline-only guitar cells** (pink outline in A/B, dashed in C). I can't tell if they mean "plays here", "plays lightly" or "doesn't play".
- **"走路" on the speed ruler.** I guess it means "about walking speed", but the marker is tiny and I wouldn't get it without voiceover.
- **The bottom subtitle band** takes up about a quarter of the frame as empty dark/wood space. On a phone that's wasted height while the grid is squeezed.

## The one look I'd most want to keep watching: C (notebook)

Why: it's the only one where I can actually *read the beat* in 5 seconds. Coloured blocks on white paper, the red-circled 空 on reggae, the red arrow on the current beat. The video's promise is "you can learn to hear this", and C makes me feel I could. The key frame is the best "oh, I get it" moment of all six.

Runner-up: A. It looks the coolest and most "music-y", and pops best in dark mode, but the grey pads blur together when small. B is the best thumb-stopper (the record/wheel shape is distinctive), but I couldn't read any rhythm from it, so it would lose me after the first look.

If C is chosen, I'd still want: drum labels on the hook frame (or 动次打次 order), a darker/warmer paper tone for night viewing, and the ticks to appear one at a time instead of all at once.
