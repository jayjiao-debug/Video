## Story-worthy (ranked)
1. Same day, opposite houses: a log cabin with no glass in the windows vs a grandson of Wedgwood, the potter who made the anti-slavery medallion (L1, D2–D3).
2. Both lose their mothers as small boys, at 8 and 9, a year apart (D3, L2).
3. Both see slavery as young men and never forget it: Darwin's 「再也不去奴隶国家」 (1832/36) vs Lincoln's 「一种持续的折磨」 (1841) (D4, L4).
4. Darwin nearly loses his place on the Beagle for arguing with the captain about slavery (D5).
5. Both lose a child of 10 and 11 (D6, L6).
6. 1859 / 1860: one publishes the book, the next November the other is elected (D7, L5).
7. **The only time their lines touch:** 18 days after the Proclamation, Darwin writes 「你们的总统」 (D8). He never writes the name.
8. Both die in April (X3). They never met (X1).

## Risks
- Don't claim Darwin wrote about Lincoln by name, or that they corresponded.
- Don't merge Darwin's 1832 Brazil scenes into one date without checking (D4).
- The election date (L5) and Willie's age (L6) are to be confirmed before going on screen.
- Avoid "both were heroes of equality" overreach. Darwin's and Lincoln's racial views are debated; we show only what they did and wrote about slavery.
