What patch 26.20 changed

From Riot's 26.20 notes, the Worlds patch.

Buffed: Lillia, Lucian, Vayne, Neeko, Diana, Kennen, Kindred, Mordekaiser, Smolder, Tahm Kench, Swain.

Nerfed: Ashe, Yunara, K'Sante, Ambessa, Cassiopeia. Hextech Rocketbelt reworked; Hexplate weaker on ranged champions.

Pulled before release: the planned Varus and Sylas changes.

Nocturne stayed near-permanently banned through the play-in; K'Sante went winless there after the nerf, while Vi, Olaf and Seraphine went unbeaten when picked.

How the odds are worked out

The bracket is played out — times per model with the real Worlds 2026 format: a Swiss stage (three wins to advance, three losses out; deciding matches best-of-three, the rest best-of-one), then best-of-five quarterfinals, semifinals and final. Round 1 pairs pot 1 with pot 4 and pot 2 with pot 3 and keeps regions apart; later rounds pair equal records without rematches. Karmine Corp holds the play-in spot after beating MVK in the play-in final.

Each game is won with the Elo chance from the two ratings. Before every simulated tournament each rating gets a random shift of about — points, so form swings are part of the result.

  • Riot power ranking: Riot's Global Power Rankings snapshot of 18 October 2026, an Elo built from results with extra weight on playoffs and international games.
  • 2026 results, raw: a rating fitted to every 2026 game of the Worlds teams (a Bradley-Terry model), weighting recent patches more heavily and international games one and a half times. It spreads teams wider than Riot does because domestic leagues are only linked by a few international games.
  • 2026 results, ranking spread: the same order, squeezed to the spread of Riot's ranking.
  • Blend: the average of the Riot ranking and the raw results rating, the headline model.

Not modelled: the Round 1 draw (still to be made), draft and Fearless rules, travel and stage nerves. All four models agree on the shape: the LPL sits around one title in five, almost all of it through Bilibili Gaming.
