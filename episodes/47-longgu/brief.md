# Brief: 假如说你是一块龙骨 (ep47, working title)

- **Owner pick (2026-10-24 22:27):** idea 3 「假如说你是一块"龙骨"」 from the ideas list, reworked: 「front dinosaur, 从世界的霸占到xxx 咋了的hook，然后从生活，灭绝，一步一步」.
- **Episode:** ep47 · **Series:** VIBE知识大赏 (Juno), history-video-style format (iwanau 《假如你是传国玉玺》 rhythm, our own look).
- **Idea in one sentence:** the life of one dinosaur bone told in the second person: you belong to a ruler of the Earth, you die, your kind goes extinct, you turn to stone underground for tens of millions of years, and then people dig you up, call you 「龙骨」, grind you into medicine and drink you.
- **Hook direction (owner):** open on the dinosaur. 「假如说你是…」 line, then a fast fall: 从地球的霸主，到 [a pharmacy drawer / a pot of soup / 一包药粉]. The xxx is the punch and must be a verified fact, not a joke.
- **Story order (owner):** 生活 → 死亡 / 灭绝 → 一步一步 (buried, turning to stone, mountains rising, exposed) → found by people → 龙骨 → what happens to you now.
- **Why a 18–25 viewer cares:** everyone loves dinosaurs and has heard of 龙骨 as a 中药 name; almost nobody knows the "dragon bones" in medicine were real fossils, and that in our own century dinosaur bones were still being dug up and boiled.
- **Counter:** 「你 X 岁」 counting in years (it runs from days to 亿年), or the time you've spent underground. The screenwriter picks one.
- **Takeaway:** to be found by the screenwriter. Direction: a ruler of the Earth for 1.6 亿年 is now a drawer label; what we call 「龙」 was real all along, just not what we thought.
- **Length target:** ≈2:30, max 3:00 · **Format:** 1920×1080, subtitle-first, music only (bgm2.mp3 at episodes/41-sanqiannian/audio/, cut with episodes/42-yiyi/music/ scripts; never committed). No voice unless the owner asks.

- **Must get right (logic):**
  - If our animal is from before the end-Cretaceous (e.g. Early Cretaceous), it did **not** die in the asteroid. Its death and its kind's extinction are two different moments; don't merge them.
  - Most 龙骨 in the pharmacopoeia are fossil **mammals** (not dinosaurs). Only claim dinosaur bones were sold/eaten where a source says so.
  - Deep time on screen must be in the right order and roughly to scale where numbers are shown.
  - No humans with dinosaurs.
- **Must avoid:** presenting legends as fact (label 相传 or drop); a specific dinosaur species unless the source ties it to the bones that were dug up; mocking 中医 or the villagers (stick to what happened); maps with borders; hands reaching in from off-screen; copying the reference's look.
