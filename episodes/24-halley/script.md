**Logline:**
- This comet comes back about once every 76 years, so you'll probably see it once.
- The man who predicted it would have been 102 when it came back.
- In 1705 Halley "dared venture to foretell" 1758. He reasoned that the comets of 1531, 1607 and 1682 were one, and blamed Jupiter for the uneven gaps.
- He died in 1742. In Paris, Clairaut's method, worked through by Lalande and Lepaute, made this lap 618 days longer than the last, giving mid-April 1759, give or take a month.
- On 25 Dec 1758 a farmer saw it, 16 years after Halley's death. Perihelion came on 13 March, about a month early, right at the edge of their margin.
- Two thousand years of records show no lap is the same (74–79 years), so 2061 was worked out the same way.
- Halley never saw it. The viewer probably will.

**Takeaway:** 规律能跨过一个人的一生：算对了，自己等不到的那一天，也会准时来。（哈雷没等到的，2061年，轮到你。）

## Title options (≤ 10 字)

1. **死后16年，它回来了** (recommended; both the director and the cold reader ranked it first)
   - A concrete number and an emotional turn. "Who died? What came back?" makes people tap.
   - L24 answers it word for word. It is exactly true (#12–#14) and doesn't claim the prediction was exact.
   - Risk: cold, it can read as 灵异/鸡汤. The comet swinging round the Sun on drop 1 settles the genre in about 1 s.
   - **Producer check:** if ep22 went out as 死后会变慢的钟, take #2 so the 死后 opener isn't reused.
2. **他没等到的那颗彗星**
   - Honest, names the subject and carries the ending ("他没等到 / 轮到你").
   - No number, and some viewers will think "Halley's comet, I know this" and scroll on (cold reader).
3. **斗胆预言：1758年** (new; replaces 写给身后的预言, which both reviewers ranked last)
   - Halley's own hedged words (#7) plus a concrete year. It sets up a bet ("what did he predict, and was he right?") without saying the answer, and it doesn't name the comet.
   - It can't overclaim: 斗胆 is the faithful "dare venture".
   - Weakness: it has no emotion, and 1758 means nothing yet to a cold viewer.

**Title-card sub-line:** 1705年，一个写给身后的预言 (unchanged). If option 3 is chosen, use 他没等到的那颗彗星 as the sub-line, so the words don't repeat.
