## Corrections to the brief (read first)

1. **「钱、财、货、贵，为什么都带"贝"？」 is wrong. 钱 has the 金 radical (钅), in 简体 and 繁體 (錢) alike.** The owner confirmed it. That dropped hook turns into a better one: 「财、货、贵都带"贝"，偏偏"钱"是金字旁。」 It is ✓ (rows B5, C1-C4). The answer is a good twist: 钱 was first a **farm tool (a spade)**.
2. **The "人民日报 2024" source for 交子 is 《人民周刊》 (人民日报社 magazine), 2024-06-17.** It says: 「公元1024年，北宋政府设立益州交子务并发行官方交子，世界上最早的纸币由此诞生。」 (H1). The date "1024年1月12日" is in 中新网 2024-12-13 and 光明日报 2024-02-05, not in that article by day. Both agree it is 天圣元年十一月二十八日 = 公元1024年1月12日.
3. **「祖宗」 in the hook 「你每天扫的码，祖宗是1000年前的一张纸。」 is figurative.** The line is true in the sense "paper money began in Chengdu in 1024" (1002 years ago, 估算), but a QR payment is not a descendant of the jiaozi in any chain a source states. Safe versions are in section 0.
4. **"世界上最早的纸币" is a claim with a qualifier.** 《人民周刊》 says it flat. 光明日报 (任双伟, 中国钱币学会会员) says 「世界上最早的纸币"之一"」. 《宋史》 itself says the system 「盖有取于唐之飞钱」 (modelled on Tang 飞钱, a remittance voucher). Use 「最早的纸币之一」 or 「最早由官府发行的纸币之一」.
5. **"移动支付" is not "扫码支付".** 人民银行's 移动支付 counts all bank-processed mobile payments (NFC, app, code). On screen: 「移动支付」, never 「扫码×××亿笔」.
6. **The 2025 full-year mobile payment figure** (2314.64亿笔, 571.97万亿元) is in the PBOC report 《2025年支付体系运行总体情况》 (released 2026-02-26), but I could only open a reprint (移动支付网). pbc.gov.cn blocks the fetcher. The Q1-Q3 2025 and Q3 2025 numbers I did open in primary form (A2, A3). Before publishing, a human should check the full-year line on pbc.gov.cn.
7. **「买卖」「宝」 do not carry 贝 in 简体** (買賣寶 did). Don't draw 贝 into them (B6).
8. **"最早的金属铸币 = 铜贝" is the 国家博物馆 exhibition's wording**, a "first" claim. Attribute it (B4).
9. **ctext.org was not reachable.** Classical quotes come from 原古诗文网. Spot-check on ctext before publishing.

## Story-worthy

**The 5 most surprising facts (all ✓):**
1. **钱 was a spade, and 钱 has no 贝.** The most common character for money started as a farm tool (《说文》 via 《太平御览》: 钱，铫也，古田器也; 《诗经》 「庤乃钱镈」; 国博: the 布币 took the name). Rows C2-C4.
2. **半两 and 五铢 are weights; 开元通宝 is a name.** The coin's written value stopped being its weight in 621 (G4), and "钱" money then lived on state trust.
3. **A string of 1000 iron coins weighed over 25 斤 (《宋史》: 贯二十五斤八两).** That is why Sichuan invented paper (H4, H5).
4. **The first 贯 of Ming paper = 1000 coins = 1 两 of silver; later it "不能直钱一文".** One line of 《明史》 holds the whole inflation story (I1, I2).
5. **In 2025's first three quarters, banks processed 1703.82亿 mobile payments (about 7,200 a second, 估算), and 10.17亿 people use online payment.** (A1, A2)

Runners-up: the Shang used 六千多枚 shells in one tomb (B2); five-shell strings and the 朋 (B7); the Song 交子 failed because 16 rich households couldn't pay (K1); the 五铢 lasted 700+ years (F2); in 2016-17 mobile payment offline went from 50.3% to 65.5% of netizens (A8).

**Strongest human story:** the Chengdu merchant who had to hire a cart for a bolt of cloth (H6 ⚠, backed by H5), then the 16 rich families who ran the first paper money until they could not pay (K1). Money as a promise, shown through people hauling iron.

**Best visual images:**
- The hook: three characters (财, 货, 贵) lit by a 贝, and 钱 beside them with a spade-shaped 金 (C1-C4).
- A cart sagging under a stack of iron coins vs a single slip of paper (H5/H6).
- A single hand holding each thing in turn, the shape changing: cowrie → bronze cowrie → spade → knife → round coin with a square hole → paper slip → silver ingot → phone (brief's signature shot).
- A balance: 1000 Tang coins (6斤4两) vs 1000 Sichuan iron coins (25斤8两) (G2/H5).
