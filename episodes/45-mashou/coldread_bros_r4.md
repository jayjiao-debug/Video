# Cold read, round 4

## 1. bros_blind.png, top row, left to right (11 heads)

1. Rat / mouse: sure (pointy snout, whiskers, round ear)
2. Ox / cow: guess (boxy head with two horns; could also be a goat or a horse)
3. Tiger: guess, fairly confident (stripes, round ears)
4. Rabbit: sure (long ears)
5. Deer / stag: guess (antlers, spiky neck; I would not read it as a dragon)
6. Snake: sure (coiled neck, tongue)
7. Sheep / ram: sure (curled horn)
8. Monkey: sure (tan face, round ear)
9. Rooster: guess, fairly confident (spiky comb, beak, wattle)
10. Dog: guess (pointed ear, long snout, tongue; could be a wolf or fox)
11. Pig: guess (blobby head, flat snout, one ear; could be a seal or a bear)

Person or different animal:
- The monkey's tan face reads a bit like a human face or a mask.
- The rat's round ball on top reads as a person's head with a pointy mask or hat.
- #5 reads as deer, not dragon.
- #10 reads as wolf or fox as much as dog.
- #2 reads as goat or horse as much as ox.

## 2. c1_hook_400.png

A horse head statue, side view, on a black marble pedestal in a dim room at night. It looks like bronze or copper: brown-gold, shiny, metallic. The caption says "if you were the horse head of Yuanmingyuan", and the corner says 2007 Hong Kong. At this size I read it as a metal sculpture, and the mane looks like swirly cast metal.
