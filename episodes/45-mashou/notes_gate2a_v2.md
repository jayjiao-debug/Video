## Ranking (not a pick)
1. **L1 铜灯影.** Buildable now with ep43's proven engine. It has the strongest phone read: the cold read rates the fire frame 5/5 and would watch the whole film for it. Its costs are sameness (4th one-tone silhouette look in five) and darkness (luma 51, against ep43's 86).
2. **L3 铜壁.** The most original world, and the one where the hero reads best: a bronze story told in bronze. The cold read put it 2nd and its key frame has the line they'd remember. It can't be confirmed buildable until a moving beat (a walk or carry) is proven in relief, and the brown mud and teal fringe must go.
3. **L2 正午倒影.** The best idea on paper (the mirror is "home", the water clock is water), but the weakest frames. The hall and palace are flat clip-art (row 72) and the hook's mirror reads as a glitch on a phone (cold-read swipe point). The statues sit on water, and no daylight figure or night shot is proven.

looks.md has no 2s跳出/完播 numbers for ep41–47, and the older rows track the opening line, not the look. So the data cannot separate these three. The ranking rests on build risk, the cold read and the owner rows.
