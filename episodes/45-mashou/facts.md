**Corrections to the brief (read first)**
1. **「回到原址」 is not exact.** The horse is in 圆明园, but not at 海晏堂 (a ruin in 长春园). It stands in **正觉寺 文殊亭** (in 绮春园). Officials say 「第一件回归圆明园的流失海外重要文物」 (F18). On screen: 「第一个回到圆明园的兽首」, never 「回到原来的位置」.
2. **「第一个回家」 depends on what home means.** Six heads were back in China before the horse reached the garden (ox, monkey, tiger 2000; pig 2003; rat, rabbit 2013), and the horse itself was bought in 2007. All seven are in Beijing, but the other six are in museums in the city (保利艺术博物馆, 国家博物馆). **This is the film's twist: 回国 7/12, 回园 1/12** (F17–F20).
3. **Five not back = 龙、蛇、羊、鸡、狗.** But a *suspected* dragon head was auctioned in France on 17 Dec 2018; experts said it can't be confirmed 100 % (F23). Safe line: 「还有五个，没有回来」. Don't say 「五个都不知道在哪」 flat; say 「四个下落不明，一个真假未定」 or just 「没有回来」.
4. **Ho's price:** HK$6,910 万 (Sotheby's: HK$69.1 m, US$8.84 m), a **private sale brokered by Sotheby's**, not a hammer price. The auction was stopped (F12).
5. **Ho never saw it in the garden:** he died 26 May 2020; the horse entered 圆明园 on 1 Dec 2020 (F16, F18). True, and strong; handle gently.
6. **No record of where the horse was 1860–1989.** Our sources first place it at Sotheby's London in 1989. Say 「一百多年后，你出现在伦敦拍卖行」, not 「129年没人知道你在哪」 (we can't prove a negative).
7. **The noon detail:** at 午时 the horse spouts; at exactly 12:00 the other eleven spout together with it (F3). The horse is the hour when all twelve are together. Use carefully (it's the film's emotional key).
