# Review: 马首回家，用了160年 v1 — verdict: FIX FIRST (1 BLOCKER, 9 MAJOR)

The story works. The hook is strong, the 回国 7 / 回家 1 twist reads plainly on the counter, and almost every fact is clean. But the cold open shows the 6910万 purchase as a live auction, which is false and contradicts the film's own 1:12–1:19. Several pictures also don't match their lines: no fire on 「一把火」, no curled ear on 「耳朵卷了」, and the counter drains while the heads are still seated. On top of that, the canvas edge shows in about a third of the film.

Inputs: `ep45_film_v1.mp4` (154.18 s), lines.json v3, script.md (Script v3), facts.md, src `qq45/`. I viewed all 46 sheets (1 s apart plus both sides of every cut), pulled 40+ full-resolution frames, scanned the frame edges at 4 fps (`evidence/edge_scan.txt`), measured the audio loudness curve, and re-ran pace.py and facts.py.

- **The title lands on the music:** it slams in at 0:18.13 on the measured crash (18.115). It's built from the film's own fountain arcs and head, inside a framed plaque (rows 26, 38, 90).
- **The counter device:** C7's 回国 7/12 with 7 heads and 5 dashed outlines in zodiac order is a countable number on the picture. At 2:05 the second row 回家 1/12 plus the museums appearing land the twist plainly, without a correction.
