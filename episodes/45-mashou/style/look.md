# ep45 look: 铜版 · 午时 (key frames v1, 2026-10-24)

- **World:** an engraved copper plate. The only pictures of 海晏堂 intact are the 1780s 西洋楼 copperplate views, so every set is drawn as an engraving in ONE slate family (deep #24323A, plate #34464E, mid #4D6168, soft #6F858A, line #AEC1C2, pale #D3DFDC): skies in horizontal engraving lines, shade by hatching, figures one tone #18232A with a pale rim.
- **「你」:** the red-copper horse head (core #FFE6CC, mid #EE8F58, deep #A84E2A, halo #FF8A50), drawn as a coloured engraving (copper fill, dark-copper hatching). The only colour and the only glow in the film; fire is shown only as smoke and as its flicker on your copper.
- **Skin (episode-specific, replaces the reference's red chip / red key words / serif seal):** heavy sans story text top-left; key words water-jade #9FE6D8, no glow; stamp = plate caption with a double rule; counter = the water clock's 12-hour dial (子 at top, 午 = you at the bottom, lit / ghost dots) with 在家 12/12 → 回国 x/12 → second row 回家 1/12 at H4.
- **Luma:** frame means 63–118 / 255 (owner: not dark).
- **Code:** `episodes/37-qiqing/remotion/src/qq45/` (kit45 = sets + horse head, skin45 = furniture, frames45 = 12 frames, KF45 = composition). Render: `ENTRY=qq45/KF45 CID=KF45 REMOTION_BROWSER=<chromium> node scripts/frame.mjs stills <dir> 0 1 … 11`. A local Chromium comes from `npm i @sparticuz/chromium` (`executablePath()` unpacks to /tmp/chromium).
- **Known v1 limits:** soldiers' carrying arms are a quick pose; bidders sit under the counter in the auction frames; 文殊亭 is a simplified pavilion.
