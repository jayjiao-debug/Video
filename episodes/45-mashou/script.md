# Script: 圆明园马首 (v1 · before gate 1)

Screenwriter (producer), 2026-10-24. Files: this script + `lines.json` (51 lines, `[[start, end, "text"], ...]`, `[ ]` = key word).
**Length:** 153.75 s (2:34) on the reading budget (0.5 + 字/7 + 0.6; typed lines +0.4), 0.25 s grid. The retime to bars comes with the music (bgm2), so expect ±8 %.
**Title card:** 18.0–21.0, on the drop (to be moved onto bgm2's hardest drop after measuring). **Widest line:** 15.3. **Pace check:** waiting 5 % (target ≤ 12 %); the four MINOR holds are named picture beats (the noon fountain, the counter going dark, the twist, the end card).

**Logline:** 你是圆明园的马首。你是水力钟的午时：只有到你，十二个兄弟才一起喷水。1860年你被带走，在伦敦、香港的拍卖台上被一次次估价；2007年锤子没有落下，有人买下你，又把你送了出去。2020年你回到圆明园，是十二个里第一个，也是唯一一个。
**Takeaway:** 「回家」有两种：回到中国的有七个，回到圆明园的只有一个。正午本该十二个一起喷水，现在还差十一个。

## The device: 「你」 + a counter that changes its meaning
- **「你」 = the horse head.** Never 「它」 in the lines; other heads are 兄弟 (牛、猴、虎、猪、鼠、兔 by name).
- **Counter (top right, owner's option 2):**
  - 乾隆 → 1860: 「在家 12/12」 (twelve small slots lit, yours glowing).
  - 1860 (B4): the slots go dark one by one → 「回家 0/12」.
  - 2000 → 3/12 · 2003 → 4/12 · 2007 → 5/12 (your slot relights) · 2013 → 7/12.
  - **H3–H4 (the twist):** 「回家 7/12」 splits into 「回国 7/12」 and, under it, 「回园 1/12」; only your slot stays lit in the second row.
  - K2: 「回园 1/12」 = 「还差十一个」.
  - Why not 「你 X 岁」: the birth year rests on one compiled source (F7, caution); 1759 → 267 岁 is a big number with no story in it, while 0/12 → 7/12 → 1/12 is the story. Kept as the fallback.
- **Year · place stamp** under the subtitle block on every dated line (e.g. 「— 1989 · 伦敦」). Dates live in the stamp when the line doesn't need them.
- **Repeated set-piece: the auction hammer** (5 times, faster each time, each with a different result): 1989 falls → you're sold · 2000 falls → brothers bought back · 2007 **doesn't fall** · 2009 falls → nobody pays · (2013 no hammer: a gift). It is the reference's "one set that varies and speeds up".

## Title options (owner picks; ≤ 10 字)
1. **马首回家，用了160年** (recommended). True as plain reading (F21: 1860 → 2020), promises a journey, and doesn't give away the twist (回国 ≠ 回园) or the gift. Cover = title alone.
2. **回到圆明园的，只有它** (10 字). The strongest curiosity ("only it? I thought seven were back"), but it is the H4 twist itself, so it spoils the film's best beat; use only if the owner prefers curiosity over the reveal.
3. **6910万买下，又送了出去**. Leads with Ho's purchase + gift (F13, F15), the most "share-able" fact. **Against:** makes the film sound like a story about money/a tycoon, and the price lands at 80 s.
- Dropped: 「假如你是圆明园马首」 (the reference's own title shape; the 假如说 lives in the first line instead), 「十二兽首，还差十一个」 (its plain reading is "eleven still lost", which is false: five are).
