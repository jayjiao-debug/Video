# Script: 《人活着为了什么？人类问了近四千年》 (v1)

**Logline:** At 3 a.m. you type 「人活着到底为了什么」. Someone wrote the same question nearly 4,000 years ago. We run a relay through seven people who answered it, and hold back one answer, on a clay tablet, until the end. That answer turns out to be the one Harvard's 88-year study reached: the people you love.
**Takeaway:** The question isn't new, and neither is the answer: it lies not at the end of the world but with the people next to you. 「你活着，是为了谁？」
**Length:** about 154 s (2:34) on a provisional grid: 7 stops, a quiet coda and a today-ending.
**Files:** this script, plus `lines.json` (the same 35 subtitles with display times).
**pace.py:** 0 too fast, 1 % waiting (one MINOR wait of 1.3 s at H1). Every gap is a named visual beat (listed under "Scene map").

Screenwriter, 2026-10-24, from brief.md, facts.md, ep41 `script_v3.md` and the coordinator's two course corrections:
- Length is under 3:00, ideally about 2:30.
- 6–7 stops of 2–3 short lines each.
- Same song (バケモノの唄 instrumental), re-cut to the ep41 shape.

**Timing assumptions.** All times are film seconds on a provisional grid:
- 106 BPM: beat 0.566 s, bar 2.264 s.
- The cold-open hit is at 9.30, as in ep41. Every scene is a whole number of bars from that hit, and every line starts on a beat (except O1, which starts on frame 0).
- When the music cut is ready, shift each scene block as a unit. Line offsets inside a scene stay valid.

**How lines are counted.**
- 字 = Chinese characters. A number counts as one, as in pace.py. Punctuation doesn't count.
- No line is over 16 字.
- `[gold]` marks a key word.
- The Fact column cites facts.md rows.

## 1. Hook, throughline, ending

**Hook (first 5 s).**
- Frame 0 is the viewer's own phone at 03:07, with 「人活着到底为了什么」 being typed. The subtitle is already up: 「凌晨三点，你也搜过这句吧？」
- At 3.07 s, the number arrives: 「[近四千年前]，有人写下了它。」
- At 6.47 s, the paradox that keeps people watching: 「泥板上的答案，和[哈佛]一样。」 The film doesn't say what the answer is.

**Throughline.** The same question is asked again in seven places, and each era hands the next a new answer:
1. Is it worth it? (Egypt)
2. You can't escape death (Babylon; the rest of her answer is held back)
3. Understand life first (Lu)
4. Examining your life is the point (Athens)
5. Get up and do a human's work (Danube)
6. With a why you can bear anything, and the why was love (Nietzsche → Frankl, on the drop)
7. Science agrees: relationships (Harvard)

Then the held-back answer is read out at last: the little one holding your hand, the one you love.

The 3 a.m. viewer is written into the relay twice:
- Socrates: 「你凌晨三点那一问，问得好」
- Marcus: 「管着帝国的人，也不想起床」

**Ending.**
- The tablet's hidden lines are revealed: 「看看那个牵着你手的孩子 / 常常拥抱你的妻子 / 这，就是人的命运」.
- The child's hand match-cuts to the viewer's hand on the phone: 「三千多年前的答案，和哈佛一样。」
- The thumb changes 「为了什么」 to 「为了谁」 and messages a friend: 「明天一起吃饭？」
- On the hit, the friend's voice note: 「你也没睡？明天吃火锅！」
- Then dawn: 「答案不在世界尽头，就在身边。」 This echoes Gilgamesh running to the end of the world in stop 2.
- Title, then the comment question 「你活着，是为了谁？」.

## Title options (≤ 30 characters, the owner's hook-question style)
1. **《人活着为了什么？人类问了近四千年》** (16 字), recommended.
   - It is the shape the owner liked in ep41 (《为了跟你说一句话，人类努力了三千年》): the viewer's own question, then the length of the human effort.
   - 「近」 keeps it exact: the oldest copy is c. 1850 BC, about 3,876 years ago (EG1).
   - It promises a journey without giving the answer away.
2. **《人活着为了什么？三千多年前的泥板和哈佛，想到了一起》** (24 字)
   - The oddest possible pair (clay and Harvard) makes you want to know the answer, and the title doesn't say it.
   - 「三千多年前」 is the safe date for the tablet (GB1).
   - 「想到了一起」 is used instead of 「同一个答案」 because the tablet also lists food, clean clothes and dancing, so it would overclaim.
3. **《你凌晨三点搜的问题，近四千年前就有人写下了》** (21 字)
   - It starts from a moment the 18–25 viewer has lived (the "这说的就是我" test, owner notes row 45).
   - The plain reading is exactly what the film shows (the papyrus, EG1).
   - The tone is "you're in good company", not alarm.

The skill's ≤ 10 字 default is replaced here by the producer's ≤ 30 limit and the owner's long hook-title style.
