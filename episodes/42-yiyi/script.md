# Script: 《人活着为了什么？人类问了近四千年》 (v2, timed to the music re-cut A_valley)

**Logline:** At 3 a.m. you type 「人活着，到底为了什么？」. Someone wrote the same question nearly 4,000 years ago. We run a relay through the people who answered it, and hold back one answer, on a clay tablet, until the end. That answer turns out to be the one Harvard's study reached: good relationships.
**Takeaway:** The question is old, and so is the answer: the people who hold your hand, and the friend who replies at 3 a.m.
**Length:** 151.916 s (2:32), the length of the A_valley re-cut (`scratchpad/music42/music_map_ep42.json`).

## 1. Hook, throughline, ending

**Hook (first 5 s).**
- **Frame 0:** the viewer's own phone at 03:07. The query is typed and heard in an inner voice.
- **2.27 s:** 「凌晨三点，你也搜过吧？」
- **5.10 s:** the number arrives, 「[近四千年前]，就有人写下了这个问题。」.
- **On the HIT (9.06):** the paradox over the rewind, 「而泥板上的答案，和[哈佛]一样。」.

**Throughline.** Seven eras answer the same question:
1. Is life like this worth it? (Egypt)
2. You can't escape death; the rest of her answer is held back. (Babylon)
3. Understand life first. (Lu, turning on the song's own break and re-entry)
4. Examining your life is the point. (Athens)
5. Get up and do a human's work. (Danube, the quiet valley)
6. Love is the highest goal, and it holds even after the beloved is gone. (Frankl: the crash, the build, the real silence, the drop)
7. Good relationships make people happier and healthier. (Harvard, after the drop)

The quiet coda then reads the held-back answer: the little one holding your hand, the one you embrace.

**Ending.**
- The dead stop rings out: 「泥板上的答案，真的和哈佛一样。」
- The intro returns on the viewer's phone. The thumb turns 「为了什么」 into 「为了谁」, and the narrator says so.
- The thumb messages a friend, 「明天一起吃饭？」.
- On the FINAL HIT, the friend's voice note: 「你也没睡？明天吃火锅！」
- The title, then 「你活着，是为了谁？」.

## Title options (≤ 30 characters, hook-question style)
1. **《人活着为了什么？人类问了近四千年》** (16 字), recommended.
   - It has the ep41 shape.
   - It uses the film's one number, kept exact by 「近」 (EG1 c. 1850 BC).
   - It promises the journey, not the answer. TT reads it aloud; if another title is picked, TT changes with it.
2. **《人活着为了什么？泥板和哈佛，想到了一起》** (18 字)
   - An odd pair makes you want the answer.
   - It has no year count, so it can't contradict 近四千年.
   - It says 「想到了一起」, not 「同一个答案」, because the tablet also lists other things (food, clean clothes).
3. **《你凌晨三点搜的问题，近四千年前就有人写下了》** (21 字)
   - It starts from the viewer's own moment.
   - The plain reading is exactly what the film shows (EG1).
