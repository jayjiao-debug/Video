# Script: 谁按错了  (v1, first draft for gate 1)

**Logline:** 切尔诺贝利按下紧急停堆，反应堆却爆炸了，当年的调查怪操作员。二战飞行员和夏威夷导弹警报给出了答案：同一个错一再发生、一犯就成灾难，背后往往是设计。最后回到切尔诺贝利，"刹车"本身就是"油门"，而这个缺陷 1983 年就被发现过。
**Takeaway:** 错是人犯的；但让同一个错一再发生、一犯就成灾难的，往往是设计。（#16 #25 #26 #38 #40）
**Length:** the body runs 105.0 s (33 lines plus the title gap). With a ~4 s Juno end card that is **≈ 109 s**. pace.py: 0.0 s waiting (0 %), no TOO FAST. The only CHECK is the planned 2.8 s title gap at 12.4–15.2 s.

## Revision notes
First draft, so no gate notes yet. The research decisions are all applied:
- **Thesis narrowed** → L28–30 put the researcher's line into three subtitles: "错，确实是人犯的 / 可让同一个错一再发生的，往往是设计 / 让一个错酿成灾难的，也往往是设计".
- **Structure like 《应该没事吧》** → Chernobyl opens as the question (L2–4 plus the plaque "谁按错了？"). WWII (L6–14) and Hawaii (L15–21) answer it, then Chernobyl comes back with the answer (L22–28), and the film ends in the viewer's own life with the door (L1 ↔ L33).
- **Chernobyl wording** → it is always 停堆**棒**, never 按钮 (L23, L24). The AZ-5 button in L2 is only what was pressed. The hedge "很可能是决定性因素" appears on screen (L25, #12). There is no "改口": the line is "同一机构修正结论" (#9, #10). The fairness beat is L26 (#17). 1983 and "似乎被忘了" are L27–28 (#16). 18 s is L24 (#13). There is no death toll on screen: "接着爆炸" (#21) carries the hook without one (allowed: "当晚2人，数月内28人", #22, if the director wants a dimmed count in the L3 after-image). No blue glow, no 核爆.
- **WWII** → the error is the wheels instead of the flaps *after landing* (#23). The controls were identical and side by side (#24). C-47 is the control group (#25). The fix is the wheel and wedge (#26). There are no B-17 crash counts and no Fitts & Jones percentages (#27, #29).
- **Hawaii** → "手滑" appears only to be crossed out (L17, #32). The officer believed it was real (#32). The drill text said THIS IS NOT A DRILL (#33). Test and live shared the menu and the confirmation box (#34, #35). It took 38 min (#31) and there was no correction plan (#37). The conclusion is two-sided (#38). No geopolitics, and the officer is neither named nor shown.
- **Viewer** → L1 is the viewer's own slip plus the topic (self-blame for mistakes), with the subtitle on frame 0. The emotional turn is 骂自己蠢 (L1) → 先别急着骂自己蠢 (L33). Why it matters and what they'll get is L5.

## Title options
1. **切尔诺贝利，谁按错了** (9 字), **recommended for the feed and cover.** It carries the famous name (the 《应该没事吧》/Titanic lesson: the known topic brings the traffic), and it is a real question the film answers. The answer is a twist: pressing stop was the *right* move, and the rods did the wrong thing. It doesn't overclaim, because it asserts nothing, and L26 still gives the operators' share.
2. **谁按错了？** (5 字), **recommended as the in-film plaque on the 12.4 s drop.** It is short enough to slam in, and it lands right after "主要怪操作员。真的吗？". It also works for all three cases (switch, menu, rods). On a feed alone it lacks a topic signal, which is why it is not the cover title.
3. **总犯同一个错？** (6 字), backup. It speaks to the viewer's own situation and matches the takeaway exactly. But it gives no hint of Chernobyl or the cases, so it may read as a self-help clip, and it pulls less traffic from the famous topic.

**Recommended pairing:** cover **切尔诺贝利，谁按错了** · plaque **谁按错了？** (lands at 12.4 s on the hardest drop).

## Lines
Camera plan: two 3D set-pieces carry the film. **Set-piece 1** is the reactor hall at night: low-key, warm practical lamps, big readable reactor-top and rod model, data on the model. It covers L2–4, the title, and L22–27. **Set-piece 2** is the WWII cockpit pedestal at dusk, from L6–14. Everything else is 2.5D/2D. Each transition is motivated by a shape or action:
- door push → AZ-5 press
- plaque shatters → identical switches
- panel lamp → phone glow
- 发送 button → AZ-5 → reactor
- 18 s counter spins back → 1983
- pull back → three dioramas
- back to the frame-0 door

There are no maps with borders, the island is shown as a silhouette with a place label only, and no slideshow cards.

Notes for the art director / editor:
- **Plaque wording** = 谁按错了？ (title option 2). The cover uses option 1.
- **L1 "推错门"** is used colloquially here: pushing or pulling the wrong way. The frame-0 door is a classic Norman door (a pull-bar on a push door). L33's fix is the bar becoming a flat plate, which echoes the knob coding in L13 (#39, #40).
- **L23 "刹车 / 油门"** is our plain-language gloss of #11 ("a local insertion of positive reactivity in the lower part of the core"). It is not a quote. The cut-away must show the mechanism (graphite tip displaces water at the core bottom), so the metaphor isn't carrying the claim alone.
- **L25** keeps INSAG-7's hedge on screen ("may well have been"). Never "一个按钮炸了核电站". INSAG-7 names design, operators *and* regulation (#10). L26 is the fair-share line and must not be cut.
- **L27** says "同型电站" with no map. If a label is wanted, use "立陶宛·伊格纳利纳电站（1983）" as text only, no borders (#16 risks).
- **Hawaii (L17)**: the "手滑" myth appears only so it can be crossed out. No North Korea, no geopolitics, and the employee is never shown or named (#32, #38 risks). The phone is a mock-up.
- **No deaths on screen by default.** If the director wants the human cost, use a dimmed count in the L3 after-image: "当晚2人，数月内又有28人" (#22). Nothing graphic, no blue glow (#20), no 核爆 (#21).
- **People:** Chapanis, Norman are public academics (named). Chernobyl operators are never named or shown as faces.
- **WWII**: no crash counts, no Fitts & Jones percentages (#27, #29). The belly-landing in L7 is on the runway (after landing), with no casualties.
