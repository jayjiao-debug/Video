## Story-worthy

**5 most surprising facts**
1. The flaw was found three years earlier: the positive scram effect was identified at another RBMK plant in 1983; the planned rod restriction "was never imposed and apparently the matter was forgotten" (row 16).
2. The emergency-stop rods first *added* reactivity at the bottom of the core, because a graphite tip pushed out the water, and full insertion took 18 s (rows 11, 13). In 1986 the official story was only that the rods were "not fast enough" (row 7).
3. The same agency revised itself: INSAG-7 says INSAG-1's blame "almost entirely on actions of the operating staff" "is thereby lessened". Some of the 1986 "violations" were not against any rule at the time (rows 9, 15).
4. C-47 copilots "never pulled up the wheels after landing", because their controls were apart and worked differently. Same people, different design, no error (row 25).
5. Hawaii: the drill recording itself said "THIS IS NOT A DRILL", test and live sat in the same menu, and the confirmation box was word-for-word the same for both (rows 33–35). The person who sent it was not misclicking; they believed it was real (row 32).

**Strongest human story:** the B-17/P-47/B-25 crews retracting their gear on the runway, and a young lieutenant (Chapanis, 1943) who, instead of blaming them, noticed the C-47 crews never did it. He glued a small rubber wheel on one switch and a wedge on the other (rows 23–26). It is concrete, non-gory, and is the thesis in one image.

**Best cold-open line** (holds for INSAG-1 and INSAG-7): "1986年，调查说：是操作员违规。1992年，同一个机构改了说法——停堆棒本身，在落下的头几秒，反而在给反应堆加速。" Shorter version: "按下紧急停堆的那一刻，反应堆底部反而先'加速'了。" Hedge "决定性" as "很可能" (row 12). Wording note for the brief's draft: say 停堆**棒**, not 停堆**按钮**. And "改口" is too strong; INSAG-7 still says the operators' actions were "most ill judged". Better: "后来的国际调查修正了说法：设计的问题更重".

**Best 3D image:** a cut-away RBMK control rod in its channel: absorber on top, a 1.25 m water column, and a graphite displacer at the bottom. On AZ-5 the rod starts to fall, the graphite tip shoves the water out of the bottom of the core, and the bottom glows warmer while an 18 s insertion counter ticks. Second choice: a B-17 pedestal with two identical toggle switches side by side, which morph into a rubber wheel and a wedge.

**Thesis check:** "当很多人在同一个地方犯同一个错，问题不在人，在设计" is directly supported by the WWII case (many crews, same switch, C-47 control group). Chernobyl and Hawaii are mixed cases, with real human error *plus* a design that let one error become a disaster. Suggested narrower closing line: **"错是人犯的；但让同一个错一再发生、一犯就成灾难的，往往是设计。"**

## Risks

- **Chernobyl politics and maps:** say "苏联" for 1986 and avoid current Ukraine/Russia framing. Show no map. For Ignalina say "另一座同型核电站", or if named, "立陶宛的伊格纳利纳电站（1983年）" with no borders.
- **Overclaiming:** never "一个按钮炸了核电站" or "全是设计的错". INSAG-7 names design, operators *and* regulation. Keep "很可能是决定性因素" hedged. Don't call it a nuclear explosion.
- **Death toll:** INSAG-1 says 31 and BfS/UNSCEAR say 2 + 28. Use "约30人" or "当晚2人，数月内28人", as dimmed counts only. Long-term cancer estimates are contested; avoid them.
- **Real people:** don't name or blame individual Chernobyl operators (several died of ARS). The Hawaii employee is unnamed in the FCC report (later employment outcome not verified here); don't name, mock or show the person (they gave an NBC interview). Chapanis, Fitts and Norman are public academics and fine to name.
- **Hawaii context:** don't mention North Korea or any geopolitics (no-politics rule). Show the alert text as a phone mock-up, not real screenshots with personal data.
- **Unverified/myth items (don't use):** the "blue glow"; Fitts & Jones category percentages; any B-17 crash count; "clicked the wrong menu item" presented as fact.
- **Source access notes for a platform reply:** UNSCEAR's own PDF and curl downloads from iaea.org/fcc.gov were refused by the network. The texts were read through the fetch tool. The death toll is cited through BfS, a government agency, which attributes it to UNSCEAR. The Fitts & Jones 460 report is cited by title and reprint only.
- **Optional McCain case:** US military, 10 deaths. The NTSB puts training and oversight first; don't present it as "touch screens killed sailors".
