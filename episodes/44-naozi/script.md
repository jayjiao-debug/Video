# Script: 你是爱因斯坦的大脑  (v1 · working title 《爱因斯坦的大脑》)

Screenwriter, 2026-10-24. Files: this script + `lines.json` (49 lines, `[[start, end, "text"], ...]`, `[ ]` = highlighted key word).
**Length:** 160.5 s (2:41), nominal. The timing uses the reading budget only, on a 0.25 s grid. No music yet: the retime to bars comes later.
**Title card:** 12.25–14.75, inside the rewind scene. **Lines:** 49. **Widest line:** 16.0.

**Logline:** 你是爱因斯坦的大脑。你陪他看指南针、追光、写论文、出名、后悔、拒绝手术；他停在了76岁，你没有：被取出、切成约240块、在苹果酒纸箱里躺了23年、被放上显微镜一次次"找到天才"又一次次被质疑。
**Takeaway:** 找了几十年，天才没藏在大脑的哪一块里。他自己说：我只是有强烈的好奇心。

- **Timing floor.** Typed lines get Needs + 0.4 s, rounded up to 0.25 (the ep43 director's floor). Lines that appear whole on a cut get Needs, rounded up.

## Story in 3 lines
1. 你是爱因斯坦的大脑。他刚出生，后脑勺大得吓人；四五岁看指南针，你想"事物背后，一定深藏着什么"；16岁想追着光跑，1905年一年写出4篇论文，1919年你对了、他红了。1955年他拒绝手术，停在了76岁。
2. 你没有。几小时后你被取了出来（事先没有家属同意），你约1.2公斤，不比别人重，被切成约240块，然后23年外界不知道你在哪，直到记者在一只苹果酒纸箱里找到你。科学家一块块把你放上显微镜："找到了！"→ 有争议，一次比一次快；84岁的哈维还带着你坐车横穿美国，想还给他的孙女，她没有收。
3. 至少18个研究者之后，天才在哪？没有找到可信的证据。147岁的你，有一部分进了博物馆。研究你的人，和小时候看指南针的他一样，相信背后藏着什么，而他自己说：我没有特别的天赋，我只是有强烈的好奇心。

## Title options (≤ 10 字)
1. **你是爱因斯坦的大脑** (9 字). **Recommended.**
   - On the in-film card it is the right hinge. The card lands right after C5 「天才，藏在你哪一块？」 and before the rewind into his childhood. So it should name *who you are* (the reference's own move: question → 「假如你是…」), not ask a second question.
   - It answers the ep43 cold reader's main confusion ("who is 你?") the moment it is seen.
   - It says only what the film does (the whole film is told by the brain), so it can't overclaim.
   - **Against:** it is close in spirit to the 「假如你是」 shape (the brief keeps that for the reference). 「假如你是爱因斯坦的大脑」 is 11 字, so it is out on length anyway. This version drops 假如 and states it.
2. **爱因斯坦的大脑去哪了** (10 字). The clickiest in a feed. It is the brief's own direction, shortened.
   - The film pays it off fully: cut up, a cider box, a car trunk, microscopes, museums.
   - **Against, as the in-film card:** it is a second question 2 s after C5. It also promises the after-death story while the next 46 s are his life, so the card and the scene after it don't match.
   - If the owner picks it, change C5 to a present-day statement, 「今天，你147岁。」 (2.0 s). The 天才 question then first appears at G1b.
3. **苹果酒纸箱里的大脑** (9 字). The strangest true image (B19), so maximum curiosity.
   - **Against:** it doesn't say whose brain (the cover art would have to), and it promises one 1978 moment, not a life.
4. **爱因斯坦大脑漂流记** (9 字). It promises the journey, it is playful, and it can't mislead.
   - **Against:** it sounds like a children's book, and 漂流 suggests drifting by chance, when people carried you every time.

**Cover:** the title alone (owner row 105). No hook line, no photo of Einstein (likeness risk), nothing that spoils the cider box or the verdict.

## Screen furniture
- **Subtitle.** Top-left (x ≈ 150, first line y ≥ 132), heavy type at about 58 px (brief), at most 2 lines. The font is set by the chosen look.
  - Typed lines reveal in ≤ 0.4 s and their timing includes it (floor Needs + 0.4). Cut-on lines (C2–C5, D1, D2, R4, Q) appear whole on the cut.
  - **Key words** `[ ]` are in the look's highlight colour (gold or red, brief).
  - **Centred punch lines** (~75 px): C5, D2 and Q. Each ends in ？ or 。 with the ½ em pull-back (owner row 97).
- **Stacking.** A line marked **"line 1 (stays)"** stays above the next line (its line 2) until line 2 ends, then both clear. `lines.json` gives each line its own window; windows never overlap.
- **Stamp (top-right).**
  - Line 1: `— 年份 · 地点` (56 px).
  - Line 2: event + plain source in brackets, ≤ 14 字 (40 px).
  - Line 3: a detail or quote (34 px), only where the stamp holds ≥ 4 s.
  - `〃` = unchanged. Teaser cuts show line 1 only. Stamps change on a beat.
- **Counter (bottom-left): 「你 X 岁」.** Exact ages from facts.md §D (估算 from 14 Mar 1879).
  - **Cold open:** visible from frame 0 and jumps with each cut: 76 → 99 → 106 → 117 → 147.
  - **Title:** spins back ◀◀ 147 → 0 under it.
  - **Life:** runs with his life.
  - **D1:** freezes at 76.
  - **D2:** splits. A grey 「他 76 岁」 stays and fades with his silhouette, and 「你」 ticks on: 77, 78…
  - **From D2 on** it never stops. It dims (still 147) during the bookend flashback and is hidden on the end card.
- **You are the only glowing thing on screen.**
  - During his life you are a warm glow inside his head's silhouette. It flares when 你 thinks (K2, L1, P2, E1) and dims when 他 acts.
  - After his death the glow travels: jar, cubes, box, Tupperware, slides.
- **你 / 他 rule.** 你 = the brain (thinks, is carried). 他 = Einstein (acts). Everyone else is named (哈维) or described (病理医生, 记者, 一位科学家, 作者, 她 = 孙女). 他 never means Harvey.
- **Safety (facts.md Risks).**
  - The brain is a light outline, never tissue. Blocks are translucent amber cubes with numbered stickers 「CEREBRAL CORTEX · №」. Slides are glass with abstract light.
  - No autopsy picture: a closed door and a lamp switching off.
  - 1933 has no symbols: a passport on a consulate counter.
  - 1939 has no mushroom cloud: a typed letter and a pen.
  - 1997 has no map: a road, an odometer and road signs.
  - Einstein is a drawn silhouette (the hair reads), never a photo. Harvey, Evelyn and the researchers are whole-figure silhouettes with no likeness. No hands from off-screen.
