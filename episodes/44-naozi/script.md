## Story in 3 lines
1. 你是爱因斯坦的大脑。他刚出生，后脑勺大得吓人；四五岁看指南针，你想"事物背后，一定深藏着什么"；16岁你想追着光跑，想了10年，答案叫相对论。1919年日全食，星光真的弯了：你对了，他红了；可他拿诺奖，不是因为你的相对论。1939年他签了那封后悔一生的信，1955年拒绝手术，停在了76岁。
2. 你没有。几小时后哈维取出了你（事先没有家属同意，事后儿子同意：只许用于科学）。他的骨灰被悄悄撒掉，你不在里面。你约1.2公斤，不比别人重，被切成约240块，之后23年外界不知道你在哪，直到记者在一只苹果酒纸箱里找到你。科学家一次次"找到了！"，又一次次被质疑，一次比一次快；中间，84岁的哈维带着你坐车横穿美国，想还给爱因斯坦的孙女，她没有收。
3. 至少18个研究者拿到过你，没有找到可信的证据。他没有坟墓，你的切片在博物馆。几十年来，研究你的人都相信：你身上，一定深藏着什么。可他自己说：我没有特别的天赋，我只是有强烈的好奇心。如果是你，愿意把大脑留给科学吗？

## Screen furniture
- **Subtitle.** Top-left (x ≈ 150, first line y ≥ 132), heavy type at about 58 px (brief), at most 2 lines. The font is set by the chosen look.
  - Typed lines reveal in ≤ 0.4 s, and their timing includes it.
  - Cut-on lines appear whole on the cut: C2–C5, E1b, D1, D2, R4, G3a, G3b, Q.
  - **Key words** `[ ]` are in the look's highlight colour (gold or red).
  - **Centred punch lines** (~75 px) with the ½ em ？/。 pull-back (owner row 97): C5, D2, Q.
- **Stacking.** A line marked **L1** ("line 1, stays") stays above its **L2** until L2 ends, then both clear. `lines.json` windows never overlap.
- **Stamp (top-right).**
  - Line 1: `— 年份 · 地点` (56 px).
  - Line 2: event + plain source, ≤ 14 字 (40 px).
  - Line 3: a detail or quote (34 px), only where the stamp holds ≥ 4 s.
  - `〃` = unchanged. Teaser cuts show line 1 only.
- **Counter (bottom-left): 「你 X 岁」.** Exact ages from facts.md §D (估算, born 14 Mar 1879).
  - **Cold open:** jumps 76 → 99 → 106 → 117 → 147.
  - **Title:** spins back ◀◀ to 0.
  - **Life:** runs with his life.
  - **D1:** freezes at 76.
  - **D2:** splits. A grey 「他 76 岁」 fades with his silhouette, and 「你 76 岁」 unfreezes (a pulse, plus a date line under it that starts running) but stays 76 through A6.
  - **A7** rolls it 76 → 99. From then on it never stops. It dims (147) in the bookend and is hidden on the end card.
- **You are the only glowing thing on screen.**
  - During his life you are a warm glow inside his head's silhouette. It flares on 你 lines (K2, L1, P2, E1b's 你对了) and dims on 他 lines.
  - **K2 must teach this rule in the picture** (director carry-forward 2).
  - After his death the glow travels: jar, cubes, box, food box, slides.
- **你 / 他 rule.** 你 = the brain (thinks, is carried). 他 = Einstein (acts). Others are named (哈维) or described (病理医生, 记者, 一位科学家, 作者, 她 = 孙女). 他 never means Harvey. At Q, 「如果是你」 marks the one switch of 你 to the viewer.
- **Safety (facts.md Risks).**
  - The brain is a light outline, never tissue. Blocks are translucent amber cubes with stickers 「CEREBRAL CORTEX · №」 and no bare 240. Slides are glass with abstract light.
  - No autopsy picture: a closed door and a lamp switching off.
  - No Nazi-era imagery at all (Z1 cut).
  - 1939: a typed letter and a pen, no cloud.
  - 1997: no map, only a road, an odometer and signs.
  - Einstein is a silhouette, never a photo. Harvey, Evelyn and the researchers are whole-figure silhouettes. Our own logos only. No hands from off-screen.
