# Brief: 分手以后，为什么反而更放不下？ (ep36)

- **Episode:** ep36 · VIBE知识大赏 (Juno)
- **Idea in one sentence:** After a break-up the brain treats the person like something it is hooked on — reward and craving systems light up, rejection even shares circuitry with physical pain — so checking their page is "one more hit"; the system does quiet down with time, and believing you are healing helps.
- **Why a 18–25 viewer cares:** everyone has opened an ex's 朋友圈 at 2 a.m.; it reframes "我没出息" as withdrawal.
- **Takeaway:** 放不下，不是你不够坚强，是大脑在戒断；给它时间，别再给它"补一口"。
- **Length target:** ~2 min · 1920×1080, subtitle-only, music; intimate, night-time, warm at the end.
