# Script: 分手以后，为什么反而更放不下？ — v1 (2026-10-22)

**Takeaway:** 放不下，不是你不够坚强，是大脑在戒断。
**Tone:** quiet night, then a reveal; ends warm and kind.
**Length:** ~2:10 (≈ 4 s a line; timed to the music once chosen).

## Title options
1. 《分手以后，为什么反而更放不下？》 — the question everyone has asked themselves.
2. 《你不是放不下，是在戒断》 — the reveal as the title; stronger, a little blunter.
3. 《凌晨两点，你又点开了ta的朋友圈》 — pure scene, maximum recognition, less "knowledge" signal.

## Notes
- F2 wording: "和上瘾、渴求有关的脑区" / "像一种戒不掉的东西" — never "爱情就是毒品".
- K1: "一部分相同的脑区" (overlap), not "同一个地方".
- M1 is correlational → "越…越…".
- P1: the placebo group was told the spray was a strong analgesic for emotional pain; it was saline.
- "ta" keeps it gender-neutral.
