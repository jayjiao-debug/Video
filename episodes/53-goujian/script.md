**Logline:** 你家菜刀沾水不擦就会锈，越王勾践的剑在地下埋了两千多年，拔出来还是亮的。镜头一口气不停：从你家厨房滑到博物馆的展柜，钻进剑首的同心圆，落进1965年10.8米深的楚墓，顺着剑身读出"鸠浅"，放大到菱形花纹里的那层锡，然后答案揭晓：它其实也锈了，只是很轻；保住它的不是秘方，主要是那座层层封死的墓。镜头沿原路退出去，回到你家厨房。

**Takeaway:** 越王勾践剑不是"不会锈"：料好、锡多，但它几乎没锈，主要是因为剑鞘、两层棺、木椁、白膏泥和10.8米的封土把空气挡在外面；出土后，它反而在慢慢变暗。

## Story in 3 lines
1. 你家菜刀沾水就锈，这把剑埋了两千多年还是亮的；有一种流传很广的说法：它表面镀了铬。
2. 1965年，10.8米深、从没被盗过的楚墓里，它插在黑漆鞘里；十几位学者写了40多封信才认出"鸠浅"＝勾践；放大剑身，博物馆的研究说菱形花纹是热镀锡。
3. 落点：其实它也锈了，只是很轻；同墓的其他剑也几乎没锈，保住它的主要是那座层层隔绝空气的墓。出土几十年，它反而没那么亮了：它不是不会锈，是那座墓替它挡了两千多年。

## Title options (≤ 10 字)
1. **「越王勾践剑 / 为什么不锈？」** (9 字 + ？). **Recommended.** It is the searched question in the viewer's own words, it names the textbook object (zero barrier), and as a question it claims nothing. The film answers it all the way, including the honest part: 几乎没锈, and why.

3. **「埋两千多年，为何还亮？」** (9 字). A concrete image (亮 is ✓, A9), no overclaim. It doesn't name the sword, so it relies on the cover picture.

## The device: one take (rules for every Picture note)
- One continuous camera from frame 0 to the end card. **No cuts, no wipes, no dips, no foreground-occlusion "hidden cuts".**
- Place and time change only by moving the camera: glide, dolly, rise, dive, zoom, roll. Time can run in the **stamp** (years tick) and in the **world around the camera** (light changes, layers open or close, ◀◀ rewind), never by cutting.
- Allowed **match moves** inside the move, each motivated by a shape:
  1. knife edge → sword edge (H1);
  2. the 11 pommel rings → the stepped rim of the tomb shaft from above (D1);
  3. the dark of the tomb → the dark of a 1977 film set as a film light switches on (B1);
  4. a ring of rust (micro) → the ring of the sheath mouth as the zoom pulls out (T3);
  5. the topmost soil layer → the museum floor (R2);
  6. the case's front glass reflection → your kitchen window (P1).
- The sword is the brightest thing in every frame (style 「漆」). Its bronze-gold and the blue glass/turquoise are the only saturated colours. The green rust bloom at T1 is the one exception, and it is small.
