## Corrections to the brief (read first)

1. **「在地下埋了2400多年」 is not safe.** The sword is 勾践's, from 春秋末年. The **tomb** is later. 湖北省文物考古研究院 dates 望山1号墓 to **战国中期**: the owner 邵固 「当死于威王或怀王之时」. So the sword was above ground for some time before it was buried, possibly a century or more (估算). Safe wording: 「埋在地下两千多年」 (holds either way). Don't give a burial count of years.
2. **The sword's age.** 光明日报 (2026): 「勾践七年」 = 公元前490年. 新华社 (2025): 「穿越2500年」. 2026 − 490 = 2516 (估算). Safe: **「约2500年前」 or 「两千多年」**. 「2400多年」 could be wrong if the sword was made early in his reign. Avoid it.
3. **「出鞘还是亮的」 ✓, 「毫无锈蚀」 ⚠.** The museum and the press say 毫无锈蚀. The museum's own researcher 后德俊 says it was **not completely rust-free**, only very lightly corroded, and that it has dulled since 1965 (row E8). The honest line is 「几乎没锈」.
4. **The 「镀铬」 claim has no primary source for this sword.** See section E. The 1977 Fudan report talked about **sulphur**, not chromium (谭德睿, row E3). The museum's 2025 study found **热镀锡** (hot tinning) for the diamond pattern (row E5). The chromium idea comes from the 秦俑 weapons, and a 2019 *Scientific Reports* paper showed that chromium there is lacquer contamination (row E6). Note that 国博's own web page still repeats 「铬盐处理技术」 for 吴越 swords (row E7), so call it 「一种流传很广的说法」 and do not mock it as an internet rumour.
5. **The paper-cutting test was in 1977, not 「出土时」.** It was done during filming of the science film 《古剑》 (陈振裕, rows F1–F2).
6. **No source for the 「1977年送检被撞出5毫米裂痕」 story.** The documented damage is **1994, Singapore**: a 0.7 cm × 0.1 cm scratch (row G4).
7. **The kitchen-knife number** (「一个月就锈」) has no source. Use it without a number (row I1).

## Arithmetic (估算)

- Sword age: made under 勾践 (勾践七年 = 前490, I2) → about 2026 − 490 ≈ 2500 years. 「两千多年」 / 「约2500年」 hold. 新华社 says 「2500年」 (新华社 2025-04-06, https://www.news.cn/politics/20250406/cbd9169048dc411e840d165ae3553776/c.html).
- Underground: buried 战国中期 (A14), after it was made; dug up 1965. 「两千多年」 holds; an exact count needs a burial year (UNVERIFIED).
- 1965 → 2026 = 61 years out of the ground; 60th anniversary marked 2025 (B10, 新华社 2025).
- 875 g ≈ the weight of a large bottle of water (illustrative only).

## 5 most surprising verified facts (hooks)

1. **It isn't actually rust-free.** The museum's own researcher: 「出土时并不是绝对没有生锈」, and it has dulled since 1965 even in perfect storage (E8).
2. **The "secret" is also on the swords next to it.** Other swords from the same tomb are almost unrusted too (E10, E11), so it is mostly the **tomb**, not magic.
3. **10.8 metres down, sealed with clay, in a double coffin, in its sheath** (A6, A7, A9, E12): four layers between the blade and the air.
4. **It took 40-odd letters and two months between a dozen scholars** to read two characters: 鸠浅 = 勾践 (C4–C7). The first reading was wrong (邵滑, C3).
5. **The 1977 paper test was for a science film:** 30-odd sheets, 20-odd cut (F1, F2). And in 1994 a single plexiglass plate left a 0.7 cm scratch (G4); since 2013 it may not leave China (D2).

Runner-up: the 2025 study says the diamond pattern was **tin**, hot-applied, not chrome (E5), and the museum cast a working replica (B11).

## Myths to avoid on screen

- ✗ 「越王勾践剑表面镀铬，比西方早两千年」: no primary study reports chromium on this sword. The 1977 report talked about sulphur (E3). The 2025 museum study says 热镀锡 (E5). The 秦俑 chromium is lacquer contamination (E6). (国博's page still says 铬盐, E7, so present it as 「一种说法」 that the newer research doesn't support.)
- ✗ 「硫化铜保护膜让它不锈」 as fact: the 1977 report itself said there wasn't enough evidence (E3); a cross-section found no sulphur (E4).
- ✗ 「两千多年一点没锈」: lightly corroded, dulling since (E8). Say 「几乎没锈」.
- ✗ 「出土时一划就破二十多层纸」: the test was in 1977 for a film (F1, F4).
- ✗ 「1977年检测被撞出5毫米裂痕」: no source; the documented damage is 1994 Singapore, 0.7 cm (G3, G4).
- ✗ 「在地下埋了2400多年」 as an exact count: the tomb is 战国中期, later than the sword (A14). Say 「两千多年」.
- ✗ 「郭沫若破译了铭文」 alone: 唐兰 read 鸠浅 (C5); it was a group effort (C4).
- ✗ 「镶的全是绿松石」 or 「全是琉璃」: blue glass on one face, turquoise on the other (B5).
- ✗ 「越王剑是陪嫁」 as fact: hypotheses only (H1–H5).
- ✗ 「它在墓里泡在水里」: not in any source (A11).
- ✗ 「第一批禁止出境文物」: it is in the **third** batch, 2013 (D2).
- ✗ 「卧薪尝胆出自《史记》」: 《史记》 has 尝胆; 卧薪 is later (I5, ?).

## Story-worthy

**Five most surprising facts:** see the hooks list above.

**Strongest human story:** 陈振裕, a young graduate in 1965, cleared the coffin, washed the black lacquer sheath, drew the sword and found it 「确实名不虚传」 (A9, A10). Then 方壮猷 read it wrong (邵滑), mailed rubbings to more than a dozen scholars, and 40-odd letters and two months later 唐兰 settled it: 鸠浅 = 勾践 (C3–C5). 陈振裕 later disproved the 邵滑 tomb-owner theory with the bamboo slips (A13). The witness himself did the 1977 paper test (F1).
