# Script: 为什么一到深夜就emo (v1)

**Logline:** 凌晨的坏心情不是你矫情——是没睡好的杏仁核在报警、松懈的前额叶刹不住车，外加你的生物钟本身，到了生物夜就会把情绪往下拉；三份完全独立的真实数据都指向同一段深夜。
**Takeaway:** 别在深夜做决定——写下来，白天再看一遍，同一件事会不一样。
**Length:** ~123.6 s (film ends/fades 122.43 s; last line resolves into the fade)

## Title options
1. **为什么一到深夜就emo** — plain restatement of the viewer's own question, zero overclaim, matches the brief's working title; good as the safe default.
2. **深夜的你，不是真的你** — names the comforting reframe (temporary brain state vs. the real you) without medicalizing or promising a cure; curiosity without misleading.
3. **凌晨两点，脑子变了** — concrete time + a mechanism tease ("变了"), echoes the hook image; doesn't claim a precise "emo minute" (stays as 凌晨两点 the relatable scene, not a pinpointed clock claim) and doesn't overclaim like "只有我过得不好" did on an earlier episode.
