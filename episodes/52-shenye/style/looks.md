# Looks: 为什么一到深夜就emo (ep52)

Gate 2a material. Three directions, each a treatment philosophy for the WHOLE film (not one palette
reused per scene) — within a direction, scenes can still look different, but they come from the same
visual family/rulebook stated below. The engine layer is fixed across all 9 frames regardless of
direction: bold sans-serif subtitles (Noto Sans CJK SC Bold), pure white type with gold (#D4AF6A)
on key words/numbers, centered, dark rounded scrim (rgba(6,8,13,.80), radius ~18px, padding ~10/34px),
~64px at 1920×1080, lower-third (y 860–960 of 1080); and a small consistent Juno "J" corner mark, top
right, same position/style on every frame. Sheet: `style/looks_sheet.png` (3×3, direction names on the
left, frame a/b/c labelled across the top).

None of the three repeats the previous episode's main look (ep47 `对折剪纸` folded paper-cut,
ep45 `铜版·午时` copperplate, ep43 `汴河暮影` silhouette theatre). None is the navy-night-gold-dots family
this franchise has defaulted to in most published episodes (per `production-crew/references/looks.md`).

**Revision r2 (this pass), per `notes_gate2a_r1.md`:** two fixes applied across 7 of the 9 frames.
(1) All three (a) hook frames rebuilt so the phone/device is unmistakably the key light, lighting a
face/figure from below (A: lamp demoted to a secondary accent, phone now dominant; B: the figure is
back in frame holding the device; C: a face is now in frame catching the phone's glow). (2) The (b)
brain and (c) drop frames no longer reuse one Figma layout three times with a palette swap: direction
A keeps its original two-cards+cable (b) and dial+side-map (c) layouts as the series baseline; B's (b)
is now a single dual-needle gauge and its (c) is a single dial-with-data-ring instrument; C's (b) is a
single corrupted waveform (one line, two failures) and its (c) is a vertical falling ticker-tape with
no dial at all. Engine layer (subtitle treatment, corner mark) and the palette tables below are
unchanged from r1.

---

## A — 夜灯手账 (Night Ledger / warm desk-journal realism)

**Pitch**
- World/metaphor: your own desk at 2 a.m. — a warm lamp, a notebook, a pen just set down — rendered as
  lived-in bedroom/study realism, not an abstract diagram. The brain scenes are shown as ink-sketch
  diagrams pinned to a corkboard above the desk (two index cards + a string + a literal brake lever),
  keeping the same "handwritten study" world even when the content turns scientific.
- Why it fits: the topic is intensely personal and nocturnal (lying awake, your own notebook, writing
  it down the next morning is literally the film's ending) — this look makes the whole film feel like
  one continuous object the viewer already owns (a notebook on a desk), not a constructed set.
- Mood: warm, comforting, intimate — never cold or clinical. This is the safe, best-fits-the-brief pick.

**Palette per scene (hex)**
| Scene | Base / shadow | Warm light | Accent |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hook / bedroom | #140d08, #22160d | lamp glow #FFC46E → #FFAA50 | gold #D4AF6A |
| Brain / corkboard | #3C2C16 (cork), #1B1309 | card paper #F3E7C9 | alarm #FF6A3C, brake #8FA8B8 |
| Drop / watch+map | #22160A, #170F07 | brass #E8C27A | night-wedge violet #5A3CC8, map dots gold/#8FB6E8 |

**Typography:** stays fixed (the series engine subtitle, see top). No serif anywhere in-world; the only
non-sans element is the corner mark's Cormorant Garamond "J", also fixed across all 3 looks.

**Must NOT look like:** a generic "cozy aesthetic" Pinterest moodboard (no flat lifestyle icons, no
paper-cut cliché already used in ep47); the brain cards must not look like a textbook diagram dropped
onto a desk — they must read as hand-pinned, slightly askew, part of the same object world as the
notebook.

**How each frame explains its line** (r2: hook rebuilt per owner spec; b/c kept as the series baseline)
- (a) hook — REBUILT: the desk lamp is now a small, dim accent far upper-right (not the key light); the
  PHONE is the dominant light source, large and held low, its warm beam throwing a big upward glow that
  lights the silhouette's jaw/chin/underside of the head from below (a concentrated "jaw-hot" highlight at
  the very bottom of the head shape, fading out before the crown) — "2am, lit from below by your own
  screen" now reads in one glance, matching the owner's spec exactly.
- (b) brain — baseline (unchanged, kept as the strongest of the three original takes): two pinned index
  cards side by side, joined by a drawn cable; the left card's glowing orange almond ("杏仁核") has
  alarm-ray marks around it, the right card's cool node ("前额叶") has a literal lever hanging slack off
  the sagging cable between them — "alarm loud + brake loose" reads as one connected mechanism, not two
  separate facts. **Structural idea: two pinned cards + one sagging cable with a brake-lever pin** — this
  is now the series' one kept "two-objects-joined-by-a-connector" layout; B and C each use a different
  structure (see below), so this idea is no longer tripled across directions.
- (c) drop — baseline (unchanged, kept as the strongest of the three original takes): a brass pocket-watch
  dial with its "biological night" wedge lit violet and a mood-line visibly dipping as it crosses that
  wedge, feeding via a dotted arrow into a glowing world map where counted dots flip from gold (day) to
  blue (night) — the clock-causes-the-dip and the map-confirms-it-at-scale ideas sit in one continuous
  image with the 5亿条 counter directly on the map panel. **Structural idea: dial + dotted arrow + separate
  side map panel** — kept as the one example of this layout; B and C now each use a different structure.

## B — 诊疗观测室 (Lab Scope / instrument-panel diagram world)

**Pitch**
- World/metaphor: a sleep-and-neuro-monitoring instrument console — oscilloscope readouts, phosphor
  screens, a dual-channel panel — as if the viewer is watching their own measured brain signal on real
  lab equipment. Different world from A: an instrument, not a bedroom.
- Why it fits: the brief's core claim is "this is measured, not imagined" (弱睡眠剥夺实验, forced
  desynchrony, 5亿条 tweets) — a console/readout world makes "this is data, not feelings" the visual
  premise of every scene, reinforcing the de-stigmatizing message literally through the medium.
- Mood: calm-scientific and quietly glowing, not sterile-cold — kept mid-tone (phosphor green/warm
  amber glows against dark teal, never nos pure black) and warmed at the alarm/brake moment with amber
  vs cool blue rather than clinical red/green so it doesn't read as a medical-alarm palette.

**Palette per scene (hex)**
| Scene | Base / panel | Signal glow | Accent |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hook / scope device | #060a0d, #0c1a1c | phosphor #7DFFB3 | gold #D4AF6A (subtitle only) |
| Brain / dual channel | #101b1d, #070d0e | alarm amber #FF9A3C | brake cool #7DD4FF |
| Drop / dial + map | #112224, #081214 | night-wedge violet #785AE6 | map gold #FFD98A / cool #7DD4FF |

**Typography:** fixed series subtitle; in-world HUD labels use DejaVu Sans Mono (monospace, like a real
instrument readout) for timestamps/labels only — never for the subtitle itself.

**Must NOT look like:** a hospital/medical-alarm infographic (owner-notes: avoid "clinical-cold" or
anything that could read as mocking a low moment) — no red cross iconography, no ECG-flatline-death
motif, no sterile white. Keep every glow warm-adjacent and the console housing dark teal/charcoal, never
pure black or hospital white.

**How each frame explains its line** (r2: all three frames rebuilt — hook per owner spec, b/c given new
structures so B no longer reskins A's layout)
- (a) hook — REBUILT: a figure (dark silhouette, shoulders + head) holds the oscilloscope device up in
  front of the chest, arms bracketing it; the device's phosphor-green readout is now the clear key light,
  its upward glow lighting the figure's jaw/chin/underside of the head from below (same "jaw-hot"
  underlight treatment as A) — the face is back in frame and the device is unmistakably what lights it,
  fixing the original miss where the phone/device was shown with no figure at all.
- (b) brain — REBUILT, new structure: no more two-screens-plus-cable. One shared console now houses a
  **single dual-needle gauge** — one semicircular dial face with two needles pivoting from the same
  centre point: the 杏仁核 needle swings up into a red/amber "MAX" alarm zone, the 前额叶 needle sags down
  past a dashed "正常" hold-line on the opposite side. Legends ("前额叶" / "杏仁核") sit directly above
  each needle's zone. **Structural idea: one instrument, two needles on a shared dial** — reads "alarm up
  AND brake down, at the same moment, on the same meter" without any panel-plus-cable layout at all.
- (c) drop — REBUILT, new structure: no more dial-plus-side-map split. The 24h dial and the global data
  are now **one circular instrument**: the world's tweet-dots sit as a ring of lit points directly on the
  dial's own outer rim (warm gold at day-local positions, cool blue at night-local positions), with the
  mood-line dipping on the inner dial face at the same angular position as the night-wedge and the dim
  dots above it; the 5亿条 counter sits at the centre hub where dial and data-ring meet. **Structural
  idea: dial IS the map** (one ring, not two panels) — same mechanism, genuinely different geometry from
  A's dial-plus-side-panel.

## C — 信号故障 (Signal Glitch / data-corruption night) — the bold, riskier swing

**Pitch**
- World/metaphor: the brain at 2 a.m. as a corrupted data signal — chromatic aberration, scanlines,
  dropped frames, pixel-block world maps — visualizing "something in the system is glitching" (the
  amygdala signal overloading, the prefrontal signal dropping out) as literal signal corruption rather
  than a calm diagram. This is the direction that leaves the navy-night-gold family entirely (magenta/
  violet/cyan glitch palette) and is the one we would not normally pick — it risks reading as too
  stylised/trendy for a comforting mental-health topic if overdone.
- Why it fits: "it's not you, it's a signal problem in your brain" is literally what a glitch aesthetic
  shows — the alarm is an overloaded/oversaturated signal, the brake is a dropped/missing signal, the
  global data is a glitching live feed of everyone's phones at once.
- Mood: a little unsettling in small doses (used for structure/mechanism only) but kept legible and
  warm-lit rather than harsh — must stay on the "glitchy but cozy-night" side, never horror/alarm.

**Palette per scene (hex)**
| Scene | Base | Glow / signal | Accent |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hook / phone glitch | #0a0612, #150c24 | magenta #FF46A0 / violet #8C50FF | cyan ghost #00DCFF |
| Brain / dual panel | #0d0718 (panels) | alarm magenta #FF3C82 | brake cool #6FC7FF |
| Drop / dial + pixel map | #170d26, #0a0612 | night violet #8250FF | warm pixels #FFC882, cool pixels #6EBEFF |

**Typography:** fixed series subtitle (unchanged); in-world clock/HUD numerals use DejaVu Sans Mono with
a deliberate chromatic-split duplicate (red/cyan offset by a few px) as the only "glitch" applied to
type — the subtitle strip itself is never glitched, so it stays legible per owner-notes readability rule.

**Must NOT look like:** actual broken/corrupted footage or a horror-film jump-scare glitch (no violent
screen-tear, no flicker strobing that could be uncomfortable); not a generic "cyberpunk" neon-city
cliché; the glitch effect is a motif applied to specific signal elements (the alarm core, the mood line,
the world map), never applied to the whole frame or the subtitle.

**How each frame explains its line** (r2: all three frames rebuilt — hook per owner spec, b/c given new
structures so C no longer reskins A's layout)
- (a) hook — REBUILT: a silhouette figure is now in frame behind/above the phone, lit only from below —
  the phone's magenta/violet glow throws a concentrated highlight onto the jaw/chin/underside of the head
  (with the chromatic-split red/cyan ghost kept only on that lit edge, as a glitch accent, not a whole-
  frame effect), while the rest of the figure stays dark; "phone-lit face at 2am, and something about the
  light itself is corrupted" now reads in one glance, fixing the original miss where no face was present.
- (b) brain — REBUILT, new structure: no more two-panels-plus-cable. A **single corrupted signal screen**
  now carries both ideas on one continuous waveform: the left half of the trace spikes and clips violently
  (doubled by a cyan chromatic-ghost offset) under the label "杏仁核 · 信号过载", then the same line visibly
  drops into a flat "NO SIGNAL" gap with a missing-block cutout under "前额叶 · 信号丢失" on the right half
  — one signal, one failure shown happening in two places along it, with no cable or second panel needed.
  **Structural idea: one waveform, two failures (overload then dropout)**.
- (c) drop — REBUILT, new structure: the circular dial is dropped entirely for this direction. A **vertical
  falling ticker-tape** of individual tweet-pixels cascades down a 24-hour timeline (ticks printed along
  the left edge); warm/day pixels fall cleanly above a bright "NOW · 深夜" line, then visibly corrupt into
  a white glitch-band and reappear as cool/night pixels below it — the clock-causes-the-dip idea is now
  "falling through midnight," and the map-confirms-it-at-scale idea is "thousands of individual tweet-dots
  doing it at once," with the 5亿条 counter sitting inside the same feed panel, clear of the subtitle zone.
  **Structural idea: a falling vertical feed with a corruption band, no dial at all** — the furthest from
  A's layout of all three directions' c frames.
