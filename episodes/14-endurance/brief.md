# Brief: 坚忍号 (working title)

- **Episode:** ep14 · **Series:** VIBE知识大赏 (Juno)
- **Idea in one sentence:** 在看不到尽头的困境里，人不是靠"坚持到底"撑下去的，而是靠把不可能的大目标拆成下一个够得着的小目标、靠每天的规律和分工撑下去 —— the researcher must confirm (or replace) this with what the primary sources actually show.
- **Story:** Shackleton's Imperial Trans-Antarctic Expedition (1914–1917). *Endurance* trapped in Weddell Sea pack ice (Jan 1915) → crushed and sank (Nov 1915) → 28 men camp on the drifting ice → lifeboats to Elephant Island (Apr 1916) → Shackleton + 5 sail the 7 m *James Caird* ~1,300 km to South Georgia → cross its mountains → every one of the 28 rescued (Aug 1916). Wreck found March 2022, ~3,008 m, almost intact, name on the stern.
- **Why a 18–25 viewer cares:** 每个人都有"熬不过去"的时候（考研、找工作、失恋、一段很长的低谷）。看不到终点时，"再坚持一下"这句话最没用。这群人困在冰上近两年，靠的不是意志力鸡汤，而是一套具体的方法。
