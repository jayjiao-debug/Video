## Titles (ranked)
1. **497天，怎么熬**: it is concrete and puts a number on the cover, which matches the series' number-led hits (第几个人, 邓巴数). "熬" is the viewer's own word from the brief, and nothing in it overclaims, since 497 is the RGS count (#17). It is the best fit for the feed and the cover.
2. **先别看终点**: it is the best in-film plaque, because it answers L4's "不是咬牙坚持到底" exactly on the drop. In a feed it is abstract and reads like a self-help account, with no Antarctic or story signal. Owner option: use 1 as the Douyin title and cover, and 2 as the plaque inside the film.
3. **只追下一站**: there is no topic signal ("站" reads as a metro or a journey), and it overlaps 2 in meaning and is weaker.
