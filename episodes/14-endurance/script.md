## Title options
1. **497天，怎么熬** (6 字), **recommended for the Douyin title and cover.** It is concrete and extreme, and it is a question the film answers. 497 is RGS's count and checks to the day (#17). "熬" is the viewer's own word. It claims no survival, so it can't be misread as "nobody on the whole expedition died". Both reviewers and the cold reader ranked it first.
2. **先别看终点** (5 字), **recommended as the in-film plaque on the drop.** It answers L4's "不是咬牙坚持到底" directly and names the method without overclaiming (#A1). On a feed it is too abstract, which is why it is not the cover title.
3. **只追下一站** (5 字), backup only. It is active and matches the running "下一站", but it gives no Antarctic signal and gives away the takeaway.

**Recommended pairing:** feed/cover 497天，怎么熬 · plaque 先别看终点 (lands at 12.4 s on the hardest drop).

Notes for the art director / editor:
- The wreck appears twice. At frame 0 the beam catches only the **E**. The full name and star are held for L33. Both are our own build (note 3), with no FMHT imagery and no downloaded wreck model.
- Never show the dogs being shot, Blackborow's amputation, or faces of the real men. People are abstract figures, with no invented dialogue. The only spoken lines are quotes from 《South》.

- Study (L14–17): the honest limits are a small lab study (40 children aged 7–10, subtraction), with completion % measured the day after the 4th of 7 sessions (#A32). Never "科学证明".
- Wild: "每天" stands for "from a fortnight after I had left, … each day" (#A20). "副手" = second-in-command, under whose command the 22 were left (#A33).
- L8: the "new mark" line is in *South* (1919), written of the night of 27 Oct 1915 (#A1). The subtitle says the book, so the attribution is exact.
- L19 "5天多": launched 9 Apr, landed 14/15 Apr depending on source (#A35). The film doesn't claim 7 days.
