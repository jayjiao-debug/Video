# Research: the angle (researcher 2) · ep14 坚忍号

Scope: the ONE counter-intuitive idea about how the 28 men kept going, and the small concrete details that
prove it, plus 3D model licences. Dates and the 2022 wreck are covered by researcher 1 (facts.md) and are not
duplicated here.

**Primary text used:** Ernest Shackleton, *South: The Story of Shackleton's Last Expedition 1914–1917* (London:
William Heinemann, 1919). Project Gutenberg #5199, https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/5199 . Gutenberg is blocked by
this sandbox's egress proxy for bulk download, so the full text was read from the Standard Ebooks transcription of
the same Gutenberg/1919 text (CC0), https://github.com/standardebooks/ernest-shackleton_south (source declared in
its content.opf: gutenberg.org/ebooks/5199 and archive.org/details/cu31924032382529). Citations give the
**chapter**, because the e-text has no page numbers. If a platform asks for a page, use the 1919 Heinemann scan at
archive.org/details/cu31924032382529 (not opened here, since archive.org is also blocked).

Note: *South* quotes the men's diaries throughout (in quotation marks, often unattributed: "one man wrote in his
diary"). Rows say when a line is Shackleton's own and when it is a diary he quotes.

## Candidate ideas (≤16 字)

**1. 先放下终点，只追下一个小目标** (14 字) ← **recommended**
- The counter-intuitive turn: the men did NOT survive by "坚持原目标到底". The leader dropped the original goal on
  the first night ("A man must shape himself to a new mark directly the old one goes to ground", A1). Then he kept
  swapping the end for the next reachable point: Paulet Is. → Deception Is. → Elephant Is. → South Georgia (A3).
  He counted stages ("another stage of the homeward journey had been accomplished … forget for an hour the problems
  of the future", A4).
- Modern support: Bandura & Schunk 1981. Same 42 pages, but "6 pages a session" beats "all 42 by the end", and
  "Distal goals had no demonstrable effects" (A26).
- The small proofs that fill the middle: the next meal is a target (A9 "bright beacons"), the next watch (A10,
  A14), the next Saturday concert (A18), and every morning "the Boss may come today" (A20, which makes today the
  target).
- Fits the brief's takeaway "别看终点，只看下一步" and the hook "最难熬的时候，靠什么撑下去？"

**2. 撑下去靠的不是意志，是日常** (11 字)
- Evidence: fixed mealtimes in the Ritz (A11), meals kept "regular in spite of the gales" (A9), one-hour watches
  (A14), football/hockey (A12), a concert the day the sun disappeared (A13), Saturday concerts (A18), a cook rota
  and the "General Post" seat rotation (A17), Shackleton deliberately fighting "monotony in the meals" (A16).
- Strong and visual, but less surprising. "Routine helps" is a known self-help line. It works best as the
  *how* under idea 1, not as the headline.

**3. 每天都当"今天就会得救"来准备** (13 字)
- Evidence: Wild rolling up his sleeping bag every day, "Get your things ready, boys, the Boss may come today"
  (A20). His optimism was "doing", not "telling" (A23). Result: 22 alive and "very cheerful" after four-plus months
  (A21).
- Emotional ending beat. On its own it risks sounding like "保持乐观" 鸡汤. Best as the climax / final turn of
  idea 1 (the smallest goal of all is "today").

**Recommendation:** use idea 1 as the film's ONE principle. Use 2 as the middle-act evidence (each routine is a
"next small target"), and 3 as the climax line. Suggested takeaway card: "看不到终点时，把终点换成下一步。"
