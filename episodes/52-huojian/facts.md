## Corrections to the brief (read first)

1. **The launch was on the evening of 10-23, not 10-24.** 新华社太原10月23日电 (李国利、李宸): 「10月23日18时15分，我国在太原卫星发射中心使用长征六号改运载火箭，成功将遥感五十号03星发射升空，卫星顺利进入预定轨道，发射任务取得圆满成功。」 10-24 is the date of the news stories about the cloud. On screen: **「10月23日傍晚」**.
2. **The payload is confirmed:** 遥感五十号03星 (the brief's "?" can go). Its official use: 「主要用于国土普查、农作物估产和防灾减灾等领域」. Don't add any other purpose.
3. **Where it was seen: 北京、山西、山东, not 陕西.** 澎湃: Beijing, western sky, around 18:20; also 山西, and 济南、青岛、泰安 in 山东. 央视 (极目新闻): 「北京、山西、山东等多地网友都拍到了」. None of the sources I opened says 陕西. On screen: **「北京、山西、山东都看见了」** or just 「多地」.
4. **The rocket's name: use 新华社's 「长征六号改」.** Douyin searches for 「长征6号」, but 长征六号 is a different (older) rocket. Some press (川观 2026-10-12) calls the Taiyuan solid-and-liquid rocket 「长征六号甲」. I did not find an official statement that 长六改 = 长六甲. Don't write 「长征六号」 or 「长六甲」 on screen. Use 「长征六号改」 (or 「长六改」).
5. **The viral comment's numbers mostly have no source** (section L). Two of its dates fit real events: 982年前 = 1044 (《武经总要》) and 406年前 = 1620 (the Ming court looking for Western cannon; the cannon reached Beijing in 1621). But its distances (120m / 180m / 400m / 400m) are not in any source I found. **No distance from the comment may go on screen.**
6. **Bows are far older than "6000年前".** 中国国家博物馆: 峙峪 and 下川 (旧石器时代晚期) both yielded 石镞, and the 峙峪 one is 「我国目前发现的第一件石镞」. 中国科学院 (2024, Nature Ecology & Evolution) re-dated 峙峪 to 「距今4.5万年」 (main layer 45,800–43,200 years ago), with "tanged and hafted projectile points with evidence of impact fractures". The CAS release does **not** say "bow". Safe wording in row B2.
7. **突火枪's 「百五十余步」 is how far the bang was heard, not the range.** 《宋史·兵志》: 「…焰绝然后子窠发出，如炮声，远闻百五十余步。」 A 2017 科普中国 reprint turns this into 「射程可达到150步，约230米」. That is a misreading: **✗**.
8. **冯继昇 970 may not be the first true rocket.** 《宋史》 records 开宝三年 (970) 冯继升 「进火箭法」, but CNSA notes that 《玉海》 gives 开宝二年 (969). 光明日报 (关晓武, 2015) lists four competing dates for the first true (self-propelled) rocket, and 潘吉星 argues for 1161. In 宋 texts 「火箭」 often means a fire arrow shot from a bow. On screen: **「970年，有人向朝廷献"火箭法"」**. Don't say 「世界第一枚火箭」.
9. **The earliest dated bronze gun is 1298, not 1332.** 国博's own page says the 至顺三年 (1332) gun 「是现存有纪年可考的最早火炮」. But 钟少异 (军事科学院, 光明日报 2024-01-20) says a 大德二年 (1298) gun is 34 years older, 「迄今所发现中国最早的有明确纪年的铜火铳」. 科普中国 / 《中国三十大发明》 agrees. **The 阿城 gun has no inscription.** 科普中国 says 「大致为13世纪末至14世纪初」, and 钟少异 says it is judged 元代 and needs direct evidence. The "1288" date is from Wikipedia-type sources: don't use it.
10. **天问二号's "10亿公里" is distance travelled, not distance from Earth.** 新华社 (2026-07-06): 「历经约400天、约10亿千米航程」. 科技日报's headline 「十亿公里外」 is loose: the target 2016HO3 is an Earth quasi-satellite. On screen: **「飞了约10亿千米」**, never 「离地球10亿公里」.
11. **The 「1083年领用25万支火箭」 line is mis-attributed.** CNSA's page credits it to 《武经总要》, but that book was finished in 1044. UNVERIFIED until the original (probably 《续资治通鉴长编》) is checked.
12. **Not opened / blocked:** ctext.org 宋史 / 武经总要 wiki pages (bot check; only the 太平御览 page loaded); ihns.cas.cn (robots); cnsa.gov.cn 「火箭的历史」 page; people.com.cn sub-pages (redirect loop); Nature article page (provenance block; the abstract facts come from CAS's own releases). curl to news.cn is blocked by the proxy.

## Story-worthy

**The 5 most surprising facts**
1. **The 万户 story is only 81 years old in print (2026 − 1945, 估算).** It first appears in an American book in 1945 (Herbert S. Zim), with no source. Yet a crater on the Moon's far side carries the name 万户, and in 2024 China brought back the first far-side samples, 1935.3 g (I1, H3).
2. **"远闻百五十余步" is about sound.** The 1259 bamboo gun is remembered for how far its bang was heard, not how far it shot (E2).
3. **东方红一号 is still up there.** It launched 56 years ago and is still in orbit (G3). The same rocket family has now flown 672 times (A3).
4. **The comment's "982年前" is real by accident.** 1044, the year 《武经总要》 was finished, holds the earliest recorded gunpowder recipes (D1). Its "400米" is not in any source.
5. **The 火箭云 is daylight at night.** The ground is already dark; high up (no altitude in the sources), the sun is still out and lights the exhaust, which swells in the thin air "like breath on a winter day" (A6, A7).

**Strongest human story:** 1619–1621: the scholar-official 徐光启 sends men to Macau to buy Western cannon; four arrive in Beijing in 1621; in 1626 the guns are on the wall at 宁远 (F1, F2). A scientist who reached for the best "throwing machine" of his time. Keep it to a silhouette; no battle gore. Alternative: 976, 魏丕 told to push the bed crossbow from 700 to 1000 steps (C2, ⚠).

**Best visual image:** a dark town and ridge at 18:20, and in the western sky a huge glowing jellyfish lit from below the horizon by a sun nobody on the ground can see (A4, A6). Rewind ◀◀ from it to a hand holding a stone ball.
