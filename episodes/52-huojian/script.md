**Logline:** 10月23日傍晚，天已经黑了，西边天上却亮着一团发光的云：那是长征六号改火箭的尾迹。为了把东西扔这么高，人类练了几万年。同一个无脸行者从一颗石球走起，手里的东西一路在变：箭头、劲弩、床子弩、火箭、竹筒、铜管、红夷大炮，然后是东方红一号、天问一号和嫦娥六号。从几万年前扔石头，到今天把东西扔上天，还能接回来。

**Takeaway:** 几万年来，人类一直在把东西扔得更远：先是几步，然后是史书里的几百步、千步，然后一下子到了2384公里和3.2亿公里。现在扔上天的还能接回来。那团火箭云会发光，是因为地面已经天黑，天上还有太阳。

## Story in 3 lines
1. 10月23日傍晚天都黑了，一团云却在发光：那是长征六号改火箭的尾迹。为了把东西扔这么高，人类练了几万年。◀◀ 倒回一只握着石球的手：十几万年前，人能扔的可能是石球，几万年前石头变成了箭头。
2. 史书记载，2000多年前的劲弩"射六百步之外"，北宋的床子弩从七百步加到千步。同一时期有人献上"火箭法"，982年前《武经总要》写下目前最早记载的火药方。767年前大竹筒打出弹丸，史书写它"远闻百五十余步"（先猜？），那是响声传了这么远。728年前竹筒换成铜管，407年前徐光启派人去澳门买西洋大炮，整整400年前红夷大炮守住了宁远。
3. 然后数字一下子变了：56年前东方红一号飞到最高2384公里，今天还在天上飞；5年前天问一号着陆火星，离地球约3.2亿公里；2年前嫦娥六号落在月球背面，而且这一次它回来了，带回1935.3克；今年天问二号追上一颗小行星，还要采样带回地球。回到开头那团云：地面已经天黑，天上还有太阳。几万年来我们把东西越扔越远，现在扔上天的还能接回来。你见过火箭云吗？

**Why A.**
- Row 1 is the topic itself, the cloud every viewer has just scrolled past (owner rows 22 and 47), and it is a paradox you can see on frame 0: a dark sky with a glowing cloud. It is a statement, so the viewer asks 「为什么？」 by themselves.
- The question comes back word for word at R1, and R2 answers it. That is Douyin's searched 「为什么会有火箭云」, so the film holds the answer to the end and the viewer stays for it.
- The number 「几万年」 lands at 6 s, inside the 8 s rule, and the title follows on the drop.
- The rocket's name (H2) is the search term, said once and correctly.

**Why not B.** 「为了这团云」 gives the ancient throwers a purpose they didn't have. As a playful frame it's fine (the 3000年 video does the same), but frame 0 then spends its first line on a slogan instead of on the picture's mystery. It's a good fit for the Douyin 标题 or the cover instead.

**Why not C.** It is the strongest "we checked" hook, but it puts a ✗ claim (120米) on frame 0 and spends 8 s before the first real fact. The title slips to 11.0 s, off ep51's drop. A user's comment would also have to be redrawn and anonymised. Keep it for the pinned comment: 「热评里的120米、180米，我们没查到出处；片里的每个数都查得到。」

## Title
**Film title options (≤ 10 字)**, shown on the plaque at 9.50:
1. **「从扔石头，到火箭云」** (8 字, two rows 从扔石头 / 到火箭云). **Recommended.**
   - It promises the whole 「从古至今」 walk in four characters a side, and it carries the trend's search word.
   - It overclaims nothing: the film starts at a stone ball and ends at the cloud.
2. **「人类扔了几万年」** (7 字).
   - This is the 3000年 format's rhythm with a number, and it is true from the 石镞 rung (B3).

## The 「从古至今」 walk (the spine)
- **One walker:** the ep50/ep51 silhouette (faceless, one flat tone, warm rim light), walking left → right with the same gait. His clothes change behind each wipe: a hide cloak (旧石器), robes (战国 / 宋 / 元 / 明), and a modern jacket from 1970.
- **时代色 (suggestions for the art director):** each era gets its own mid-value colour field, and the hero accent is always **the launch light: the warm white-gold glow on whatever leaves his hand.**

  | Era | Colour key |
  |---|---|
  | 2026 dusk (frame 0, R0–P2) | deep blue-violet sky down to a thin orange band, a dark slate town with warm windows; the cloud luminous white-gold with a cyan edge |
  | 旧石器 | ochre grassland at dusk, umber |
  | 战国 | lacquer red-black, bronze gold |
  | 北宋 | pale celadon sky, warm wood |
  | 南宋 寿春 night | indigo, bamboo green |
  | 元 | steppe ochre, slate |
  | 明 澳门 / 宁远 | sea teal, then winter grey-blue brick under snow |
  | 1970 | steel-blue pre-dawn |
  | space | black-violet; Mars rust; the Moon cool grey |
- **What he holds** carries the launch light, and it changes on the line that names it:
  - a stone ball (H3 rewind → S1);
  - a bow with a stone-tipped arrow (S2 「箭头」);
  - a crossbow with a bronze trigger (C1 「劲弩」);
  - the windlass of a 床子弩 (C2: too big to hold, so he cranks it);
  - a fire arrow, its packet burning (F1 「火箭」);
  - the woodblock book 《武经总要》 and a paper powder packet (D1 「火药方」);
  - a big bamboo tube (E1 「大竹筒」);
  - a bronze tube with an inscription band (E4 「铜管」);
  - his palm on a long cannon (M1 cart → M2 wall);
  - the faceted ball of 东方红一号 with its antennas, lifted onto the rocket (O1);
  - nothing (O3–O4: his hand is empty and open, palm up, looking up);
  - **the return capsule landing in that open palm (O5): the first thing in the film that comes back to his hand.**
- **The range ruler (the info design):** a faint ground line along the lower third. Every throw draws a dashed arc from his hand, and its landing mark stays behind as the walk moves on, so the ladder is always visible.
  - The ancient marks have no numbers except the two 史书 figures (「史书记载 · 六百步」, 「七百步 → 千步」).
  - At E3 the ruler shows sound rings reaching 「150多步」 while the pellet lies much nearer.
  - At O1 the ruler **turns vertical**: all the ground marks shrink to one dot at the bottom, and the arc becomes an orbit (「最高 2384 公里」).
  - At O3 it stretches to Mars (「约 3.2亿公里」).
  - At O5 an arc comes **back** for the first time.
- **Set-pieces where he stops (three):**
  1. the 突火枪 night with 先猜？, 41.0–50.75;
  2. the 宁远 wall, 57.75–61.5;
  3. the catch, O5, 78.0–82.5.

  Outside these he never stands more than ~3 s.
- **The hook is the walk run backwards.** H3 rewinds from the cloud to the stone. The walk comes forward and arrives back on the same ridge at R0, in the same frame as frame 0.
- **A new visible event at least every ~4 s:** each line has its own action (a throw, a crank, a flash, a launch, a landing).
