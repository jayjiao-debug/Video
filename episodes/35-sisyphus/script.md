# Script: 推石头的人，是幸福的 — v1 (2026-10-20)

**Spine:** the viewer's own daily loop → Sisyphus, whose punishment is exactly that → a lab really built the punishment: meaninglessness, not effort, is what breaks people → Camus's answer: stop waiting for the summit, be awake in the push → psychology agrees twice: where your mind is (present) and what story you tell (calling) → the rock stays; how you push is yours.
**Takeaway:** 石头不会消失，但你可以选择怎么推、为什么推。
**Tone:** starts weary and grey, turns warm after line 16, rises at the end.
**Length:** ~2:00 (times provisional until the music is chosen).

## Notes
- Line 9 and 23 are Camus's sentences (Chinese rendering ours from the English/French; short quotes).
- Line 15: Ariely, Kamenica & Prelec 2008, N=20 per condition. "少拼了三个多" = 10.6 − 7.2 = 3.4.
- Line 18: published Oct 1942 by Gallimard; Camus born Nov 1913 → 28.
- Line 25: "哈佛的研究者" = Killingsworth & Gilbert; 74% of the sample were American.
- Line 30–33: Wrzesniewski et al. 1997, 196 employees (135 classifiable); the same-job subgroup is 24 administrative assistants — the authors call it heuristic only, so the line says 差不多 / 也一样 (no %).
- Lottery-winner study deliberately left out (contradicted by larger later data).
