## 1. Where I would swipe away

- **0:37–0:45, most likely in the first minute.** Frame 0:40 shows two thin lines of tiny soldiers far away, the counters
  at 1000 / 500, and no subtitle. Nothing is happening and nobody is speaking on screen. I'd swipe around 0:42. Frame 0:47 ("人数比 4.0 : 1", counters
  dropping fast) is good, so if I last 5 more seconds I'm fine.
- **0:31, minor risk.** The title card "为什么要'集中优势兵力'" sits on a blurry fog background with no subtitle. It feels like the
  intro is over and the class is starting. The opening already paid off (500 ✓ for 拼刺刀, 866 for guns), so some people
  will leave here, satisfied.
- **2:35 (frame 155), where I'd actually leave.** It shows one lone ship in the sunset, with no text, right after "英国，一艘船都没丢". The payoff
  has landed and the shot is pretty but empty. It's also misleading: a single ship right after "a ship lost" made me
  think "so only one survived?" for a second. Most people will drop here and miss the caveat (2:37) and the closing line (2:41).
- The opening is **not** a swipe point any more. In the 0:07–0:15 stretch with no text, the counter ticks 370 → 254 → 124 while
  "你猜：剩 500" sits in gold under "???". I'm watching numbers fall toward my guess, and that holds me.

## 2. What I understood at each moment

- **0:15, the 866:** I understood it right away. A huge white "866" sits in the middle, and the gold "你猜：剩 500" in the corner gets a red
  strike-through. That reads in under a second as "you're wrong, it's 866". This is the best frame in the film. One small thing:
  at 0:00 the subtitle says "对面500人" but the counter already reads **370**, so for a moment I thought the number was a mistake.
  (It's the battle already running, but the opening frame doesn't say so.)
- **1:00–1:15, counting the balls:** I didn't count anything. At 0:57/1:00/1:04 I see a line of glowing muzzle-flash
  balls, but nothing tells me to compare how many are on each side. The beat that landed was **1:11**: "火力 ×2" over my
  side and "挨打 ÷2" over theirs, with "人多一倍，强了不止一倍". From that I work out ×4 myself. There is still no "= 4倍" anywhere,
  and I wanted one. (One of my two missing frames may be the counting shot.)
- **1:18–1:35, the squares:** I mostly got it. 1:20 shows a red square labelled "500 人" next to a much bigger blue one with "边长 = 一排人的长度", plus
  "战斗力，看人数的平方", so I get "bigger army = way bigger square". The blue square has **no "1000 人" label**, and I had to
  guess. 1:26 ("两边掉的面积一样多") is better than it sounded in the text: red shrinks to a small inner square while blue
  loses a thin rim, and the counters (873 / 123) make it feel like the same battle. I believe "equal area" because the
  film tells me so, not because I can see it. The red loss looks much bigger than the blue rim. 1:32 makes it click:
  "1000² − 500² ≈ 866²", with 866 written on the side of the blue square, plus "英国工程师 兰彻斯特 · 1914". The callback works.
- **1:43–1:47, the two halves:** I got the idea from the text, but the picture didn't quite work. It shows a big shape whose top half is
  faded and whose bottom half is two red blocks, with "两块加起来 = 一半". Because of the tilted perspective, the two blocks look like
  rectangles, not squares, so "two half-size squares = half the area" isn't something I can see. The 1:42 "707 /
  同样打1000人，还剩707" beat before it is what convinced me. One confusion: at 1:39 the enemy is suddenly 1000 in two waves of
  500 ("两拨，各500"), when the setup said 500. Only the faint grey "同样打1000人" at 1:42 explains the switch.

## 3. One-sentence takeaway to a friend

"有枪以后人多一倍不是强一倍，是强四倍，所以千万别一拨一拨上；1805年英国船少，就是靠把船全压在敌人同一段，一艘没丢赢了特拉法加。"

## 4. What was confusing or looked cheap

- **Low-contrast text you can't read on a phone:** the grey "同样打1000人，还剩707" under the 707 (1:42), the faded subtitle at
  1:26, and the small grey "两拨，各500" under 606 (1:39). The facts that make the logic work are in the faintest type.
- **The 0:31 title card** is a blurry fog smear and looks like a placeholder transition next to the nicely rendered fields.
- **The lone ship at 2:35** reads as "one ship left" instead of "none lost".
- **Two different years:** "兰彻斯特 · 1914" at 1:32 and "分析过这一仗 · 1916" at 2:20. Probably both are right, but a viewer sees
  two years and thinks someone made a typo.
- **The 0:22 melee counter** (855 vs 380) is close to 866, and I briefly wondered if it was the gun answer.
- The blue square at 1:20 has no "1000 人" label, and there is no "×4" at 1:11.
- **2:43, "同一时刻、同一地方，比他多":** scattered blue blocks and one red block. I can't tell who's ganging up on whom,
  so the final image is weaker than the line.
- **What didn't look cheap:** the soldier close-ups and wide sunset fields look like a real strategy game. The naval map
  (2:20, 2:28) with "前队" and "对方33艘：一半以上 被俘或被毁" is clear. Naming the source (《孙子·虚实篇》 at 1:49, "1805 · 特拉法加" with
  27 vs 33 at 1:52) and listing sources on the end card makes it feel trustworthy. That fixes my biggest complaint from the
  subtitle-only read.
