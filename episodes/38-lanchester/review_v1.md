# Review: 为什么要"集中优势兵力" v1 — verdict: BLOCKED

The idea and the arithmetic are sound, and some 3D frames are the best the series has made (the ships at 2:04–2:08 and the lone ship at sunset at 2:30–2:37). But two render faults block publishing. The 孙子 inscription turns upside down halfway through its shot, and every soldier "fires" with empty hands because there are no muskets. On top of that, three of the four 原来如此 beats don't read on screen: their labels are off-frame, sit too far apart, are cut off, or are on screen for 0.5 s. The two drops (title, Trafalgar climax) are the weakest pictures at the two biggest musical moments.

## The four 原来如此 beats with the sound off
- **★1 (866, not 500; 0:15 / 0:26–0:30):** half works. 866 is big and clear, and 500 ✓ / 拼刺刀：剩 500 reads. But the viewer saw the count heading for about 870 long before the slam, and the 500 guess is a tiny grey tick (#4). The frames at 26–30 s are dark brown mush.
- **★2 (twice the shots × half the bullets; 1:00–1:15):** does not read (#5, #6). The balls are lost among the streaks, the counts sit apart, and the ×2 / ÷2 tags are off frame.
- **★3 (squares; 1:18–1:34):** partly works. ×4 is too brief, "一样大" is off frame, and the equation is overprinted (#6–#9). The 866 + 1000² − 500² ≈ 866² moment does land on the 1:30 breath-hit.
- **★4 (split = half; 1:43–1:47):** reads best. Two red squares fill half the pale 1000-square, and 两块加起来 = 一半 is clear. But the two waves before it are invisible (#16), and the 孙子 payoff right after flips upside down (#1).

## What works (keep)
- Every number on screen comes out of the sim exactly (866, 500, 707; 1000 v 1000 ends 2 : 8 at 97.5, which still reads as 两败俱伤). Every figure traces to a ✓ row in facts.md. 孙子 is verbatim (S1). ⑧ and 前队：先不理它 are the safer wordings.
- The ship models in close shots (2:04–2:08) and the lone ship at sunset (2:30–2:37) are the best 3D in the series. Light, reflections and scale all hold up.
- The music placement is right where it was measured: the title on DROP 1 (30.05), 866 = 1000² − 500² on the strongest transient (1:30.05 breath-hit), the climax on DROP 2. The BGM is untouched apart from the end fade.
- The subtitle component centres trailing ？ correctly (measured 958–960.5 px).
- ★4 (two half squares inside the ghost) is the clearest maths image in the film. Use it as the model for fixing ★2 and ★3.
