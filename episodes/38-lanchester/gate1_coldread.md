## 3. Why 1000 vs 500 leaves 866, in my own words
With guns all 1000 of you shoot at once and his few bullets are spread over twice as many people, so you're 4 times as strong as him, not 2. His whole army can only knock a quarter-sized chunk out of your strength, and what's left works out to 866 men.
(Honestly, I can explain the "4 times" part. The "866" part I can only repeat as "square minus square". I couldn't say why it's subtraction.)
