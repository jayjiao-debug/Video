# Review: 为什么要"集中优势兵力" v2 — verdict: BLOCKED

v2 is clearly better than v1. The 孙子 text is upright, every man now carries a musket with a bayonet, the ×2/÷2 working is on screen, 一样大 sits on red's ghost, the equation is no longer overprinted, the chart shot is one continuous move with the van visibly sailing off, the tally no longer sits on ㉒, and the end card has the J and the gold title. The worst problem is new and comes from the same camera code as v1's blocker 1. On the sky bridge into Trafalgar, the whole world turns upside down for 0.6 s (1:50.03–1:50.67). Second, the new "aim" pose does not read as aiming: in the bullet-time close-ups, men hold the musket at hip height with their arms locked straight out and the barrel angled down across the body. Five v1 majors are still open or only partly fixed: the hook gives its answer away, the squares look like PPT, there is no battle on DROP 2, 1000 v 1000 is never shown, and the ending is weak.
