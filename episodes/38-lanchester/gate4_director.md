- **0–3 s: likely swipe.** The first frame is the backs of six standing soldiers and a counter. Nothing fires until
  about 7.5 s, and the music sits at −24 dB. The volley on frame 0 was on my gate-1 keep list; holding fire for ② lost it.

  So the 866 slam at 15.05 shows a number that has been on screen since 14.6. The "直觉：500？" tick is about 44 px,
  grey, on bright sky, which is invisible on a phone.
- **0:20–1:16: holds.** The melee and ★1, the title drop, the volley and the bullet-time close-ups carry it.
- **1:16–1:48: second swipe point.** Flat diagrams over the music's trough for 30 s, with no men on screen.
- **2:00–2:15: let-down.** The loudest music of the film plays over two ships passing in mist and, at 2:09, one ship alone
  on an empty sea.
- **2:16–end: fine.** The chart and the van sailing off, the quiet ship, the end card.
