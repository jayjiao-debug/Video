## Notes
- The A/B/C numbers (40 % → 12 %, B 35 → 51 %, C 25 → 37 %, pd 0.8) are an illustration; on screen mark 示意.
- Palomares: say the map used the fisherman's sighting and the bomb was found where he pointed; do not claim the map alone found it.
- AF447: "人们以为，那里可以排除了" — the acoustic search likely covered the wreck site (Tech Review 2014); the beacon was later shown not to work (Stone 2014).
- Scorpion: "within 200 yards" (Stone, INFORMS) → 不到200米.
