## Notes
- Every shot has one clear subject and foreground framing (palm, door frame, gate, shoulder); no lineups.
- The hotel window in S1 is the same window whose light dies first in S3: one visual thread through the opening.
- S6 and S8 are the same set: the push-in carries across the voice line to the title.
- No body, death or harm is shown (S2 covers, S5 the dog lying down); Tutankhamun is never shown alive.
