# Gate 2b/3 notes: 定情信物 · 一人一半, key frames v3 (40 stills + contact sheet), r1. Verdict: REVISE

Scores (rubric 1–5, only the areas this gate covers): visual **3** · info **3** · motion **3** · edit (transitions, as built) **2** · script/sound: not at this gate.

**Bottom line.** The look works and is new for the series. Flat layered paper, ink figures with a paper-white rim and the token as the only metal give it one coherent world, and the split-tally stamp carries the theme. Luma is fine. Frame means run 73–211/255, nothing near the 27 that got the "too dark" note, and the darkest stretch (破镜 at night, 41–58 s) still has mid-tone indigo and lit tokens. Body logic and gait look right in every still. Three things would get it sent back:
1. **Text sits on the picture in ~12 frames**, mostly because the story block stacks three rows down to y≈390.
2. **Two hero props don't read at phone size.** The couple avatar reads as a cartoon face with two eyes. The gold hairpin prong reads as a golf flag.
3. **The DROP isn't the biggest frame, and the hand-overs repeat.** The halves start flying on 106.986 instead of landing on it. Every scene change is either the same 0.2 s wipe (×4) or a hard cut (×15).
