## Story-worthy
1. 破镜重圆 is literally a split token with a meeting time, 1,437 years before couple avatars (F4–F10).
2. 「約指一雙銀」: rings as love tokens ~1,800 years ago (F2–F3).
3. 金钗 split into two prongs, one kept, one sent (F11).
4. A bicycle ≈ three months' pay in the 三转一响 era (F14).
5. The 520-yuan red-packet day (F16).

## Risks
- 破镜 and 长恨歌 are story and poem: label 相传 / 白居易写.
- 三转一响 ≠ 定情 (F13). 520's origin is "earliest known" (F15).
