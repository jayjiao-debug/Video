## B. The body: mummification and the heart

| # | Claim | Source | ✓ |
|---|---|---|---|
| F11 | In mummification the **heart was left in the body** | **Smithsonian (SI Encyclopedia)**: "They left only the heart in place, believing it to be the center of a person's being and intelligence." **Uni Münster Archaeological Museum**: "It is therefore usually left inside the body and is often accompanied by the so-called heart scarab". **Horniman Museum**: "The heart was left within the body" | ✓ |
| F12 | The **brain** was pulled out through the nose and **thrown away** | **Horniman**: "The brains were removed through the nose and discarded". **Smithsonian**: "removed by carefully inserting special hooked instruments up through the nostrils". **Münster**: "No further attention is paid to the brain, as the heart is regarded as the seat of thought and emotion." | ✓ |
| F13 | The other organs were dried in natron and put in **four canopic jars** | **Münster**: organs "placed in four vessels known as 'canopic jars'". (Second source not fetched; museum source.) | ✓ |
| F14 | Mummification took **70 days** | **Smithsonian**: "The mummification process took seventy days." **Herodotus, *Histories* 2.86** (primary; seventy days). Note: Münster gives 35–40 days for the drying stage alone, so say 70 days for the whole process, not "70 days in salt" | ✓ |
| F15 | The heart was the seat of **thinking, memory, moral character** | **Met (Hatnefer scarab)**: "the heart was not only the center of life, but also of thinking, memory, and moral values." **Münster**: "the seat of the mind and emotions". **Smarthistory**: the heart "the seat of emotions, intellect, and character" | ✓ |

## Film-worthy surprises (ranked)
1. **The ready-made afterlife (F4, F5).** You bought the guide off the shelf with your name left blank; Ani's name was written into the blanks. ✓
2. **The heart as a witness, and the gag order (F18–F20).** The scarab on your chest tells your own heart not to testify against you, and in Ani's papyrus he is muttering it while his heart is on the scale. ✓
3. **Even the "cheat" was reused (F21).** The Met's gold heart scarab of Hatnefer has her name cut over erased text: it was not made for her. ✓
4. **Paradise has a work roster (F45–F47).** 365 shabti workers, one per day, plus 36 overseers, one per ten-day week; they answer the call with "here I am". ✓
5. **Brain in the bin, heart kept (F11, F12, F15).** ✓
6. **The judges' names (F28)**: Breaker of bones, Swallower of shades, Blood eater; and the oddly specific oaths ("I have not talked too much", "I have not taken milk from babies", "I have not cheated on the scales"). ✓
7. **The picture is the guarantee (F42).** The papyrus paints you as passing; the BM curator: depicting "true of voice" assured a favourable outcome. ✓
8. **Scribes' fees (F7).** One ostracon prices an illustrated Book of the Dead at 1 deben (silver, per Janssen) against 15 copper deben for a plain one. caution
