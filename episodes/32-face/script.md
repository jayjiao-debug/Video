# Script: 刷脸 (ep32) · v1, gate 1

**Logline:** 133 of 208 phones Which? tested open with a printed photo, and Face ID doesn't. The difference is depth: 3D face unlock reads the *shape* of your face from 30,000+ infrared dots, so a flat photo has nothing to give. That shape is 1-in-a-million… unless you have an identical twin.
**Takeaway:** 刷脸认的不是照片，是你脸的形状。照片骗得了2D，骗不了3D；能骗过它的，只有和你一样的脸。
**Answers the owner's three questions:** 为什么照片不行 (act 2) · 为什么是独一的 (act 3) · 为什么模仿不了 (act 4: mostly can't, except a twin; a mask is claimed but unverified).
**Length:** ~110 s (107.8 s of lines + a ~2 s end card) · 36 lines, all ≤ 16 字, none faster than 5.2 字/s.
**发给谁:** 你长得最像的那个人 (the end-card question).

## Title options
1. **刷脸** (2 字): recommended. It's short and catchy like 《上头》, the word everyone says every day, and the whole film is what's inside it.
2. **你的脸是密码** (6 字): explains the idea at a glance.
3. **照片骗得过刷脸吗？** (9 字): the hook question itself.

## Honesty notes
- "128个数" is Google's FaceNet (2015, research), shown as an example of how face recognition works, not as what any phone uses. Apple only says "a mathematical representation".
- 64% is from the UK market's phones (Which?), not all phones everywhere. The line says 英国测了.
- The mask is a company's claim that NPR couldn't verify, and the line says so.
- NIST: "all algorithms produced high false-match rates on identical twins". The line says 常常认错, not 一定认错.
