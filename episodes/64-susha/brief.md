## Story spine (to verify in facts.md)
1. Hook: lighter than an egg (balance). Title.
2. 1971–72: Mawangdui, Changsha; two gowns: 直裾 49 g, 曲裾 48 g.
3. Q2 (30–60 s): the rumour that one was burned and flushed away. 1983: a 17-year-old stole 30+ objects incl. the 直裾 gown; it was recovered and restored; his mother burned 7 lacquer pieces; the 曲裾 gown was never stolen (the rumour is false).
4. How thin: silk of 10.2–11.3 denier — 900 m of thread weighs 1 g.
5. Copies were always heavier (since the 1980s). 2019: 湖南博物院 + 南京云锦研究所, two years; a custom hand loom; 10 cm a working day; ~1.5 years of weaving; dyed with black tea.
6. Answer: the silkworm changed. Western Han 三眠蚕 vs modern 四眠蚕 ("吃得太好"), thicker silk; the team made 四眠蚕 moult only three times to get the finest silk (≈ 1/5 the usual thickness).
7. Ending: the replica weighs ≈ 49.5 g — still half a gram heavier.
