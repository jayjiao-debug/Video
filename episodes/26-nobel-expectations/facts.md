## Story-worthy
1. One sentence (#12), no money spent on it (#16–17), and Spain's extra borrowing cost halved within a year (#14). The cleanest real-world proof of "words move the economy if believed".
2. The same summer, Woodford told central bankers at Jackson Hole: give a *promise*, not a *forecast* (#9). Draghi's "believe me" was a promise.
3. Hall's 1978 result in one human sentence: your spending moves when news about your future arrives, not when the money lands (#6).
4. Hall sat on the committee that officially dates US recessions for 46 years (#7).
