# Brief: 假如你是清明上河图

- **Episode:** ep40 · **Series:** VIBE知识大赏 (Juno)
- **Idea in one sentence:** Tell the ~900-year life of 《清明上河图》 in the second person — the viewer *is* the scroll — as it passes from emperor to conqueror to thief to a child emperor who smuggles it out, gets lost, is found, and ends up in a museum that almost never shows it.
- **Why a 18–25 viewer cares:** everyone has seen this painting in a textbook; almost nobody knows it was carried off by invaders, copied dozens of times, smuggled out of the Forbidden City by Puyi, and found in a pile of scrolls. "You" living through all of it makes 900 years feel like one life.
- **Takeaway (one line the viewer should remember):** to be decided with the screenwriter; direction: a thing everyone wanted to own survived because people kept passing it on — the same way iwanau's seal ends on "天下，从来不在一块石头里".
- **Length target:** about 3:00 (the reference is 3:02). **Format:** 1920×1080 landscape 16:9, subtitle-only, **music only, no narration** (the owner will choose or supply the track; time the cut to its bars).
- **Visual direction:** free, but see the format reference below. Built in code (three.js engine or HTML/canvas). **No AI images.** A public-domain scan of the scroll itself may be usable as the hero object's texture if the researcher confirms its licence.
- **Owner approvals required:** gate 1 (title + beat sheet / script), gate 2a (look, from 2–3 style stills), gate 4 (cut).

## Format reference (owner asked us to study and replicate this format, not the film)

iwanau, 《假如你是传国玉玺》, Douyin, 3:02, landscape; 5.5万 likes, 735 comments, 9,360 saves. Frames (local only,
not ours to redistribute): `/home/claude/video/input/40-qingming-ref/` (`ref_sheet_1..6.jpg` = one frame every
2 s in order; time = label ÷ 2; `ref_detail_*` = typography close-ups).

The template, in order:
1. **Paradox hook (0–3 s):** 谁拿到你，谁就是皇帝。可抢到你的人，大多没有好下场。
2. **Fate teaser (4–11 s):** four hard cuts, each one fate + year stamp (摔在地上 9年 / 扔进井里 189 / 丢进草丛 552 / 抱进大火 936). Every one is paid off later in the story.
3. **Present-day mystery (12–15 s):** a phone with a 2026 rumour headline stamped 谣言; "你，到底去了哪？"
4. **Title card (~16 s):** 假如你是___.
5. **Chronological life (16 s – 2:40):** one beat per 3–4 s; soft dissolves inside an era; one reusable "handover" set (only the dynasty banners and a big year counter change) compresses centuries; callbacks (the broken corner identifies it later; "honey water" denied twice).
6. **Twist + moral (2:40–2:55):** fakes keep turning up; a historical quote; empty museum case "如果你还在，今年2246岁"; "他们以为，得到你就得到了天下。可天下，从来不在一块石头里。"
7. **Comment question + end card (2:55–3:02):** 你觉得，它现在在哪？

Persistent screen furniture:
- top-left subtitle, 1–2 lines, revealed character by character, key words in red, heavy serif;
- top-right stamp: `— 191年 · 甄官井` / event + source in brackets `孙坚得玺（《吴书》载）` / a short quote from the source;
- bottom-left: dynasty label + age counter `你 411 岁`, counting up;
- the hero object is the only saturated, glowing thing in every frame (mint-green jade + red tassel); everything else is near-black silhouettes on warm gradient skies, moonlight, fire.

## Must include
- Second person throughout ("你" = the scroll).
- The fate teaser + present-day hook in the first ~15 s; every teaser fate paid off later.
- Year · place · source stamp on every historical beat (our facts rule: only verified rows; mark legends as 相传).
- An age counter ("你 N 岁").
- A callback structure and a reusable "handover" set.
- An ending comment question; the Juno end card (`关注 Juno · 一起看懂世界`).
- Subtitle rules: ≤ 16 字 per line, ？ centring, reading budget per line.

## Must avoid
- Copying iwanau's look 1:1: no mint-green jade object, no copy of their title card or their exact corner layout styling; take the format, make our own look and palette.
- AI images; game/IP screenshots.
- Hands reaching into frame from off-screen (owner rule). Whole figures holding or passing the scroll are fine.
- Presenting legends as fact (e.g. the 严嵩 / 王忬 story): either verify it or label it 相传 or leave it out.
- Black-and-white overall (owner rule); unmotivated transitions.
