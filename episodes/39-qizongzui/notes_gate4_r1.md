## (2) Do the three pauses read as 停顿 (hold, then hit)?
- **P1, 5.62–6.14 (before 傲慢): the hit yes, the hold no.** The picture is almost still for the whole moment bar (Δ ≈ 0.2 from 4.5 to 6.0). The "hold" at 5.62 has nothing to stop from, so the eye can't tell it from the tease. The gather at 6.03–6.10 is tiny (Δ 1.2 → 0.45). The reveal itself lands hard: Δ 28.6, luminance 32 → 52, kick and toll together. The ear gets the sub hole; the eye doesn't get a matching stop.
- **P2, bar 10 freeze (20.37–22.03): the hold yes, the hit no.** There is real contrast: the tremble (Δ ≈ 2) stops dead on the crack (Δ 0.12–0.2). Two problems. (a) That is 1.65 s of near-total stillness. The motion left (Δ 0.12) won't show on a phone, so it risks the "video froze" reading (row 91). (b) The release is wasted. The iris floods the frame blue at 22.07–22.2. Then the frame is **an empty blue field from 22.23 to 22.43 (Δ = 0.00)**, so the kick return at **22.351**, the strongest drop in the body, lands on nothing. 懒惰's silhouette opens at 22.50 and its line at about 22.6.
- **P3, bar 26 push/inhale → 54.80 slam: the slam yes, the hold is in the wrong bar.** **50.5–52.77 is a dead freeze (Δ = 0.00 for 2.3 s) while bars 24–25 still have their kick.** Then the kickless bar 26 is where things move (push Δ ≈ 0.5, inhale 0.75 → 0.22). The last ~0.1 s is nearly still, then the title slams exactly on 54.80 with the full bell, so the final beat does read as hold-then-hit. But the 2.3 s before it reads as frozen video. The slam is also visually small (Δ 10, versus about 28 for every reveal; luminance 17 → 24). The film's climax is its darkest, smallest frame (row 88: the peak should be the most spectacular frame).
