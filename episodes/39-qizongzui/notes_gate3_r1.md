**(2) Is the silhouette tease clear and fun?** The device is right: a "who's that Pokémon" format, a 200 px "?", and the rim glow. Results per meme: 3 刀盾狗 is excellent, 1 袋鼠 and 4 牛来 are good, 7 草地牛 is OK. 2 奶蛙 is a shapeless lump with a flat rectangular base (11.2). 6 哈基米 is a blob with chopsticks and white fringe (35.5). 5 鼠鼠 is **not a silhouette at all**: interior texture shows through and the top edge is square (30.2), because the PNG's alpha is semi-transparent across the body and the colour-matrix filter keeps it. Three of seven confuse rather than tease. The tease is also inert. Frames t+1.0 s and t+1.7 s are identical in every scene, and the only "build" is a 6 px jitter (`urge`) over the last 2 beats. That is a tremble *before* the hit, which is the opposite of the 停顿 lesson.

**(3) Where to apply 停顿.** These are the exact moments. The music must not be cut: the owner's rule is one untouched BGM, and the upload uses the Douyin library track. The track already breathes where we need it (measured, low band <150 Hz):
- **5.58–6.09 (bar 2, beat 4): the lows drop to −18 dB, from −11.** This is a built-in 声音停顿 right before the **first reveal**.
- **20.34–22.36 (bar 10): kick out, lows −16.7 dB.** This falls under 暴怒's second bar.
- **52.78–54.81 (bar 26): kick out, lows −17.6 dB.** This is right before the **title slam** on bar 27.

The pauses.md rule ("one or two real silences: the first big reveal, the title") is therefore already met by the track itself. The picture has to stop with it. See note 6 for the full choreography.

**(4) Do left text, picture and bubble compete?** Yes. One second after each reveal there are 6 text items: 七宗罪之X，它叫 (38 px), name (250), Latin (48), English (30), closing line (48), and the bubble (46). The bubble and the closing line arrive **on the same frame** (+2 beats) and share 3.04 s. Budget at 0.5 + 字/7 + 0.6: scene 1 needs 2.7 s (bubble, 11 字) + 3.1 s (closing, 14 字) = 5.8 s. The moment bar is worse: context line (12–14 字) + quote (4–8 字) in **2.02 s**, where 3.6–4.2 s is needed. In 4 of 7 scenes the bubble repeats the quote word for word (2 凭什么不是我, 3 你再说一遍, 7 再看一眼, 6 almost). The viewer reads the same line twice while the new line competes with it. Too small for a phone (owner: labels ≥56, tags ≥40): context line 42 px w600, 七宗罪之X 38, English 30, closing line 48, outro thesis **28**, SEVEN DEADLY SINS 28. Crowding: the calendar straddles the disc rim next to the Latin (25.8), the coins and boxes sit outside the disc at the frame edge (31.9, 38.0), and the 暴怒 tremble shakes the whole frame, including the closing line, for 2.5 s.

**(5) Is the meme humour landing with the closing lines, or is there a tonal clash?** Laugh-then-sting is the right format for Douyin, and most closing lines are good (想要更多，是怕失去 / 填不满的，往往不是胃 / 懒，有时是累到不想面对). The clash comes from timing, not tone: the gag (bubble) and the insight (closing line) land on the same frame, so neither gets its beat. Stagger them (note 3). Three small script issues: scenes 1 and 2 both open the quote with 凭什么. Closings 1 and 7 both hinge on 被看见, so the last scene echoes the first instead of adding something. 袋鼠's quip 你的胆子真是肥嘟嘟的啊 (11 字) mocks the viewer and has nothing to do with pride. The thesis 它们不是七个怪物 contradicts what we showed: we never showed monsters, we showed seven cute memes.

**(6) Does the intro stop a scroll in 2 s?** No. Frame 0 has mean luminance **9/255** with five grey 40 px characters (公元6世纪|) at the upper left. Nothing moves except the cursor. It is a history detour (a 6th-century pope) before anything about the viewer. It fails every point of the owner's opening formula: frame 0 striking, one recognisable object filling the frame and moving, the viewer's own situation in line 1, something happening on the music inside the first second. Account data: 2s跳出 ≤29 % got pushed, ≥34.7 % did not. This is the weakest frame in the film.

**(7) Is the ending inside the big circle a payoff?** Not yet. The circle is elegant and the question 你心里，哪一宗罪最大声？ is a good comment driver. But the cast vanishes: the ring holds the same seven text chips as the intro, so the film ends where it started instead of collecting what we met. The thesis line, the film's 原来如此, is 28 px and unreadable on a phone. The slam adds a white sweep streak across 罪 (54.9) and a full-frame white flash.

**(8) The single change that would most raise quality.** Rebuild the first 2 seconds around the seven silhouettes (note 1). Every other fix only counts if people are still watching at 0:02, and today frame 0 is a near-black screen with five small grey characters. A close second, for watch-through: the text diet in note 3 (quote into the silhouette's bubble).

## What must not change (it works)
- Layout A: text left, picture right, every scene. Iris transitions in the next disc's colour. The ending inside one big circle. The title slam on bar 27 (54.81).
- The sticker disc per sin: one saturated colour, slow comic rays, halftone dots, white rim. Colour per sin is consistent from the intro chips to the outro ring.
- The silhouette + giant white "?" as the tease device (fix the three bad shapes; don't replace the idea). 刀盾狗's silhouette is the model.
- The 250 px gradient name with the gold divider and Latin italic; the horizontal blur-in of the name; the NameBurst sparks.
- The 3-bar-per-scene rhythm locked to the grid (reveals on bars 3, 6, 9 … 21).
- The closing-line formula "X，是Y" with the key word in the sin colour, and most of the lines themselves (贪婪, 暴食, 懒惰, 暴怒).
- The meme cast and the 袋鼠 crown drop; the calendar-flip idea for 懒惰; whole-picture jelly/bounce motion (no head puppet).
- The outro question 你心里，哪一宗罪最大声？ as the comment driver.
- Serif type throughout (owner prefers the serif as 高级).
