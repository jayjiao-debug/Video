## New lesson from the owner's reference video (an editing tutorial on 停顿, 2026-10-22)
1. 动作停顿 — power needs a wind-up: a hold/pause just before the strike, slow-then-fast into the hit,
   fast-then-slow out of it, a shake on the start of the hit.
2. 声音停顿 — "让耳朵休息一下": cut the BGM to silence just before the drop; the next entry hits harder.
   Variant: split the music just before the chorus, mute the tail, add a riser/vocal SFX, land the picture on the chorus.
3. 画面停顿 — control density: fast cuts, then stop cutting; the stop grabs attention and sets up the strong beat.
Apply where it serves the film; don't put a silence before every scene (it would stop being a surprise).
