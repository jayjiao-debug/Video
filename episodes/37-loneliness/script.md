# Script: 孤独，会传染 — v1 (2026-10-22)

**Spine:** surrounded but lonely → a town that has been measured for 60 years → loneliness clusters and travels 3 steps, more through friends → why: the lonely brain reads people differently, withdraws, drifts to the edge and passes it on → it matters for life itself → what works: change how you read others; connection spreads too → send the message.
**Takeaway:** 孤独会传染，连接也会。
**Tone:** quiet, observational; turns warm at "连接，也会传染".
**Length:** ~2:10 (≈ 4 s a line).

## Title options
1. 《孤独，会传染》 — short, surprising, a claim the film proves.
2. 《你的孤独，可能是被传染的》 — puts the viewer in it.
3. 《群聊99+，为什么还是孤独？》 — scene-first, maximum recognition.

## Notes
- C2: "可能性高出52%" (more likely), up to three degrees; data are associations in one long-running US cohort.
- B1: the scan study had 23 students — phrase as "扫描过孤独者的大脑", no generalisation beyond "反应更弱".
- H1: survival odds (OR 1.50), not "15 cigarettes" (contested).
- I1: "far more effective" — no number on screen.
