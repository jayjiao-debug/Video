# Gate 1 notes: 相信我，这就够了 (ep16) script v1 + lines.json — verdict: REVISE

Scores (rubric 1–5, only the areas this gate covers): script **3** · visual — · info — · edit — · motion — · sound —

**Why 3, not higher.** The script is clear and the facts are clean. It has a real 2/3 reveal: at 78.9 s, 「这个计划一次都没用过」. The ending lands (「你怎么看明天，今天就怎么花」). Three things hold it back:
- **The stake for an 18–23 viewer is weak.** Line 1 is the parents' mortgage. The cold reader: "tells me this isn't for me".
- **The hook number has no scale.** 0.1 %, with no "before" value, sits inside a 0–0.25 % policy band, so nothing reads as moving.
- **Lecture risk.** Right after the title come two unknown names and a 1970s history lesson. From 52 s to 105 s the viewer hears three foreign bond cases with no "你" in them, plus unexplained jargon (两年期利率, 买债, 美债利率, 7个季度). The lines also run at 0 % waiting, which is denser than the owner's ~2:00 / ~4 s-per-line comfort (owner row ep14 v17).

The cold read gives 6/10 to the end and confirms all three problems. First round, so there is nothing to compare with.

## Title ranking (the owner picks; for the Douyin title/cover)

1. **利率没动，只加了一句话**, reworded to **利率没动，只改了一句话**. It is concrete, a true paradox, and pays off in the first 9 s. The cold reader would click it. It needs the 加 → 改 fix to be literally true. On the in-film plaque it would repeat lines 2–4, so pair it with title 1 as the plaque.
2. **相信我，这就够了.** It is the best in-film plaque, with 「2026 诺贝尔经济学奖」 under it, and it pays off at 1:10 and 1:19. As a feed title it reads as 情感/鸡汤 and the viewer feels baited when 房贷/利率 appear (cold reader). So it carries a misleading risk on the cover only.
3. **央行说句话，为什么管用.** Accurate and safe, but it sounds like a 经济学课 question and has the lowest pull.

Ask the screenwriter for one more ≤ 10-字 option that carries **诺奖/诺贝尔**. This is topical today, and none of the three tells a scroller why to watch *now*.
