# Brief: 央行说句话就够了 (ep16, topical: 2026 Nobel economics)

- **Episode:** ep16 · **Series:** VIBE知识大赏 (Juno) · **Working title:** 央行行长只说了一句话
- **Why now:** on Mon 12 Oct 2026 the economics prize went to Robert E. Hall (Stanford) and Michael Woodford
  (Columbia), "for the microeconomic foundations of macroeconomic stabilisation" (wording as reported; the researcher
  must get the official citation from nobelprize.org / kva.se). Topical, so speed matters: script today.
- **Angle (producer's pick, owner had no preference):** the economy runs on *expectations*. A central bank often
  doesn't have to act: announcing what it *will* do (forward guidance) already moves mortgage rates, savings yields,
  prices and hiring, because households and firms plan for the future. This is Woodford's core idea; Hall's work on
  unemployment, wages and recessions (and the NBER recession-dating committee he chaired) can supply the "why it
  matters to you" (jobs, pay) and the "how do we even know a recession started" twist.
- **One principle:** 你以为钱在动，其实是大家的「想法」在动 — what people expect about tomorrow changes today.
- **Why an 18–23 viewer cares:** parents' mortgage (房贷利率/LPR), their own 余额宝/存款 yield shrinking, prices
  of things they buy, whether there will be jobs when they graduate.
- **Hook idea (screenwriter may change):** 央行行长只说了一句话 / 他什么都没做 / 可你家的房贷，第二天就变了.
- **Evidence the researcher should look for (primary sources only):**
  1. The official citation and the committee's popular-science / scientific background (what each laureate did).
  2. Woodford's forward guidance / expectations argument (Interest and Prices, 2003; Jackson Hole 2012 paper).
  3. Concrete real cases where words alone moved markets: Draghi "whatever it takes" (26 Jul 2012) and Italian/Spanish
     bond yields afterwards; Fed forward guidance (e.g. "at least through mid-2013", Aug 2011) and Treasury yields;
     the 2013 "taper tantrum" (words moving yields the other way). Exact dates, exact figures, official sources
     (ECB, Fed, Treasury/central-bank data).
  4. China-side anchor if solid: PBoC / LPR announcement mechanics (official PBoC pages), only if it fits honestly.
  5. Hall: unemployment / wages research; NBER Business Cycle Dating Committee (he chaired it), and how late recessions
     are declared (e.g. the 2008 recession declared in Dec 2008, a year after it began — NBER announcement).
  6. The 1970s stagflation (inflation and unemployment rising together) as the problem their field answered.
- **Must not:** give investment or mortgage advice; say "央行能控制一切"; name or show real living officials' faces
