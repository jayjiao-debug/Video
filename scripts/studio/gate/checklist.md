## Review checklist (the notes ledger)

Tick each line with evidence (a time, a frame, a sheet) before the owner sees the cut. The checklist comes from every
kind of note the owner has given on the films so far; the full ledger lives in the project files
(`studio/notes_ledger.md`). When a new kind of note comes in, add it there and here.

1. [ ] Clipping report clean, or every on-screen finding looked at. Every seated or lying shot on the sheet: the body rests on its seat.
2. [ ] Ground check clean, or each flagged second looked at in ground_worst.jpg. Cards and the end card dead still at rest.
3. [ ] Sound gate clean. Sound page heard by Jay for this cut: no cut line edges, no looping one-shot, music audible under the voice, no thump.
4. [ ] Text gate clean: every caption and card readable, nothing lingering, effects leave with their scene, word-timed effects on the voice.
5. [ ] under_cards.jpg looked at: no card, title or effect over a sign, board, face or key prop. mask_sheet.jpg: everything inside the phone's safe area.
6. [ ] Format: no mic or hands; themed title 采访XX的一天; the intro card isolates the lead and stays simple; side characters who matter have a short title; 综艺 effects in the film's skin; 9:16 frame.
7. [ ] Every line read aloud as a Chinese speaker; every line fits the time of day and the facts file; every setup has its context.
8. [ ] The lead moves like themselves in every scene; big actions read.
9. [ ] changes.md read: every beat of the last version still there unless its removal was asked for.
10. [ ] Jay saw stills or clips of every change before this render.
