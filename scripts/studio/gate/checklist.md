## Review checklist (the notes ledger)

Tick each line with evidence (a time, a frame, a sheet) before the owner sees the cut. Built from every note the owner
gave on the films so far; the full ledger with the owner's own words is `/mnt/project-files/studio/notes_ledger.md`.
(This short form is replaced by the ledger's checklist when it is written.)

- [ ] Nothing passes through anything: robes, seats, tables, props, water, papers (clipping report + sheet)
- [ ] A body rests on what holds it: no sinking into a bench, a bed or the floor
- [ ] No card, title or effect hides an in-world sign, board, face or prop the joke needs (under_cards.jpg)
- [ ] Every caption and card is on screen long enough to read and not much longer
- [ ] No line cut at its start or end; no word said twice; no whisper where a voice was meant
- [ ] No sound loops that should play once; no stray steps, burps, knocks; no thump in the music
- [ ] Music can be heard under the voice and never buries it
- [ ] The ground, the outlines and the end card hold still when the camera moves (ground_worst.jpg)
- [ ] Everything that matters is inside the phone mask (mask_sheet.jpg, watch_phone_mask.mp4)
- [ ] Every beat of the last version is still there, or its removal was asked for (changes.md)
