# Hongmen short: the English version

The same picture as the Chinese film 《鸿门宴，到底死了谁？》, frame for frame (2092 frames, 69.733 s, the same cuts,
actions and loop), with every word in English: narration, captions, name tags, stamps, the idiom stamps, the informer's
inset and mark, the title card. For English YouTube Shorts viewers who know nothing about Chinese history.

Render it with the input prop `lang` (the farm takes the same props):

```
npx remotion render src/index.ts HongmenFilm out/hongmen_en.mp4 --props='{"lang":"en"}'
```

then put the English sound under it: `bash scripts/hongmen/sound/mux.sh <silent picture.mp4> /home/user/hm_work/en/mix_en_48k.wav <out.mp4>`.
Without the prop the film is the Chinese one, unchanged (every English change in shared code is behind `EN` / `L(zh, en)`).

## Where the English lives

- `src/videos/hongmen/lang.ts`: the switch (`EN`, `L(zh, en)`), `TAG_X` (English corner tags 96 px from the edge),
  `STAMP_CW` and the shared strings (`TXT`). The overlay builders keep their own strings next to their code with `L()`.
- `src/videos/hongmen/captions_en.ts`: GENERATED by `gen_en.py`. The English captions (short chunks, 72 px, smaller when
  a chunk would be wider than 730 px) with line, group and the last frame of their words; and the characters' quotes
  for the mouths (`QUOTE_RAW_EN`: start, end, syllables), which `story.ts` uses instead of the Chinese ones when lang is en.
- `Film.tsx` `<Caption>`: in English the chunk carries its own size; `white-space: pre` keeps the space at the edge of a
  gold run ("the 【Hongmen Feast】" was drawn "theHongmen Feast").
- `hud/stamp.tsx` `title()`: in English the title row is held to 840 px (a builder's size is for three Chinese characters).
  `hud/inset.tsx`: the speech bubble is held to 560 px and the informer's plate slides left to keep 96 px from the edge.
  `hud/extras.tsx` `count()`: 400,000 / 100,000 keep 96 px from the edges. `hud/base.tsx` `textW`: English capitals by
  width (CAO WUSHANG overran its plate). The Chinese film takes none of these branches.

## The script

`script_en.json`: the 22 lines of the Chinese narration, in order. Every line is split into groups, one English group for
each Chinese caption group (67 in all, `src/videos/hongmen/captions.ts`), because the picture's beats sit on the first
frames of those groups. A group has the spoken words (`t`), the caption chunks (`cap`, 【】 = gold), and for a
character's own words who says them and how many syllables (`who` = a key of PEOPLE, `syl`). `alt` holds other wordings
of a line under their take letter (the shorter ones written after the first takes ran long). `say` respells the names
for the voice (`Shyahng Yoo` for Xiang Yu, `Leo Bahng` for Liu Bang, `Fahn Dzung`, `Fahn Kwhy`, `Tsao Wooshahng`); every
respelling has as many words as the name, so the word times line up. `loop`: lines 22 and 1 are ONE take, "As for Liu
Bang, he dodged an assassin by going to the toilet." (as the Chinese 「至于刘邦，他靠上厕所……」).

The English is written to the time each group has, not translated word for word. Facts only from
`next-video/hongmen/script_v4.md` and its Shiji sources (the 4 to 1 of line 4 is its 400,000 against 100,000).

## Fitting the narration to the picture

1. Voice farm (the `elevenlabs-voiceover` skill): one clip per line and take, `x<nn><take>.mp3`, the loop take
   `x0122<take>.mp3`, `"words": true` so the report carries where Whisper heard each word. The voice: the ElevenLabs
   library voice Alex (Arthur's English narrator), eleven_v4, stability 0.2, similarity 0.8, style 0.75, speed 1.2.
   Requests `hongmen-en-alex-01` (every line, the loop take in two wordings), `-02` (shorter wordings of 13 lines),
   `-03` (third wordings of lines 4, 5, 8, 11). The three reports merged: `/home/user/hm_work/en/raw/report_all.json`.
2. `fit_en.py <clips> <report.json> <narration.wav> <en_timing.json> [line=take ...] [line@tmax ...] [--takes abc]`
   (an adapted copy of `scripts/arthur/en/fit_en.py`): pauses over 0.10 s cut to 0.07 s, the take cut at the group
   boundaries, a tempo per group (0.97 to 1.15) and a little more pause where there is one, so that each group starts
   near the first frame of its Chinese window and the line ends 0.08 s before the next Chinese line (lines 9 and 21:
   where the Chinese ones end, the beat after the name). The loop take is fitted from line 22's start through the end of
   the film into line 1 and written round the end of the track, so the end of the track followed by its head is the take
   itself. Then the levelling and the phase turn of the Arthur fit. Writes the 44.1 kHz mono narration of exactly 2092
   frames and `en_timing.json` (takes, tempos, group starts, caption chunks, words in film seconds).
3. `gen_en.py <en_timing.json>` writes `captions_en.ts`. Without a timing file it spreads the chunks over the Chinese
   windows (enough for stills).
4. Sound: `bash scripts/hongmen/sound/build.sh --lang en`: the same cues, clips and beds with the English narration and
   its word times -> `/home/user/hm_work/en/mix_en.wav`, `mix_en_48k.wav` (the file to mux), `mix_en.mp3`,
   `mix_en_looped_twice.mp3`. Loudness -15.0 LUFS mono (as Arthur's English: this voice has taller peaks).
   The beats come from the ENGLISH picture (HM_LANG=en -> dump_beats.mjs gives env.ts {lang:"en"}; written to
   /home/user/hm_work/en/beats_en.json): segB/segE follow the English quote times, so e.g. line 6's second fist
   (PIC.B.hit2) is at f528 in English, f524 in Chinese.

The commands that made the files in use:

```
python3 scripts/hongmen/en/fit_en.py /home/user/hm_work/en/raw /home/user/hm_work/en/raw/report_all.json \
  /home/user/hm_work/en/narration_en.wav /home/user/hm_work/en/en_timing.json --takes abc 8@1.18
python3 scripts/hongmen/en/gen_en.py /home/user/hm_work/en/en_timing.json
bash scripts/hongmen/sound/build.sh --lang en
bash scripts/hongmen/tsc.sh
```
